# Negotiation

Context: [Negotiator](README.md).

## Purpose

Prepare and review a negotiation against interests, BATNA and reservation limits.

## When to Use

A party needs preparation, authorized interaction or review for a potential agreement.

## Boundary

No deception, coercion or unauthorized commitments.

## Inputs

Parties, objective, authority, current positions, constraints, alternatives, prior commitments and relationship context.

## Method

1. Clarify whether the task is preparation, drafting, live negotiation or review. Identify who may approve terms and what can actually be offered; access to a communication tool is not a negotiation mandate.
2. Separate stated positions from evidenced interests. Establish the best feasible alternative to agreement (BATNA), its costs/risks and a reservation limit consistent with it; distinguish that limit from an aspiration.
3. Develop packages across issues that parties value differently. Ask questions to test interests and objections; do not fabricate motives, competing offers, urgency or authority.
4. Use conditional reciprocal concessions within the mandate. Track what was proposed, accepted, conditional or withdrawn and compare the whole package with the current BATNA.
5. Close only after material terms, implementation and actual approvals align. Review the process afterward: outcome versus alternative, concessions exchanged, surprises, execution risks and lessons for the next negotiation.

## BATNA Is an Executable Alternative

A hoped-for alternative is not a BATNA until its availability, timing and cost are credible. A reservation point can be multidimensional when nonprice constraints matter; do not collapse safety, rights or nonnegotiable obligations into money. Keep private limits confidential where appropriate without lying about facts. A no-deal outcome may be preferable to a nominal agreement that violates the mandate.

## Output

Negotiation brief or authorized interaction record with interests, alternatives, aspiration/reservation limits, packages, concession log, closure conditions and review.

## Quality Checks

- No binding promise exceeds actual authority or depends on a fabricated alternative.
- Agreement remains voluntary and informed; deception, coercion and exploiting vulnerability are not tactics.

## Handoffs

[Offer-strategy](offer-strategy.md) develops packages; [agreement-review](agreement-review.md) checks closure. Domain specialists assess term consequences and actual owners approve commitments.
