# Offer Strategy

Context: [Negotiator](README.md).

## Purpose

Construct defensible packages and reciprocal conditional concessions.

## When to Use

A negotiator needs defensible initial offers, concessions or responses to objections.

## Boundary

No fabricated alternatives, scarcity or deadlines.

## Inputs

Validated interests, credible BATNA/reservation limits, issue valuations, mandate, current proposal and approval boundaries.

## Method

1. Construct a small set of packages with explicit issue dependencies, timing and total consequences. Where useful, offer equivalent-value alternatives to learn preferences without pretending their acceptance.
2. Set a defensible aspiration and supporting rationale. Distinguish an opening proposal from a reservation limit; do not invent a market benchmark or competing offer.
3. Plan concessions as conditional exchanges: what changes, what is requested in return, who can approve it and when the condition expires. Track cumulative value across the whole package.
4. Classify objections as missing information, risk, affordability/resource constraint, authority or genuine incompatibility. Ask a discriminating question before answering; an objection is not permission to intensify pressure.
5. After each response, compare the revised package with the actual alternative and remaining mandate. Pause for new approval when boundaries change.

## Package Integrity

A series of individually small concessions can cross the reservation limit in aggregate. Evaluate value, risk, timing and obligations together; changing one term may invalidate another. Use contingent terms only when the condition is observable, responsibilities are clear and the arrangement is legitimately acceptable—not as a device for hiding likely downside.

## Output

Offer packages, rationale, conditional concession sequence, objection questions, cumulative limits and approval gates.

## Quality Checks

- No artificial deadline, false scarcity, fake authority or misleading omission supports the offer.
- The package remains acceptable against the BATNA under realistic execution and downside assumptions.
