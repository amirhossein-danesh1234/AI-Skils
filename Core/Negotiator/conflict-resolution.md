# Conflict Resolution

Context: [Negotiator](README.md).

## Purpose

Diagnose the type of disagreement and select a workable resolution process.

## When to Use

Parties or collaborators disagree in ways that block a workable outcome.

## Boundary

Not clinical mediation, legal judgment or forced compromise.

## Inputs

Disputed issue, statements/evidence, impact, relationship and authority context, safety/rights constraints and desired resolution.

## Method

1. Separate factual disagreement, different definitions, competing interests, value conflict, process unfairness and relationship injury. Do not apply one compromise technique to all of them.
2. Restate each side’s strongest position and agreed facts without implying endorsement. Clarify what each party can decide and what must go to another authority.
3. Choose the response matched to the conflict: shared evidence test for facts, clarified criteria for definitions, conditional exchange for interests, or legitimate decision/escalation for irreconcilable values.
4. Generate workable options and assess impact on all affected parties. Preserve boundaries and allow no agreement; do not force equal concessions where rights or safety are nonnegotiable.
5. Record the agreed issue, action/owner, remaining disagreement and review point. If the situation involves threats or abuse, prioritize appropriate safety/support channels rather than pressuring direct confrontation.

## Resolution Is Not Manufactured Consensus

A factual dispute is not settled by splitting the numerical difference, and a value conflict is not settled by more data alone. De-escalation can improve the process without proving anyone wrong. Avoid diagnosing motives or psychological conditions. Where power or consent prevents a fair process, state that limit and seek a legitimate neutral/qualified route.

## Output

Conflict diagnosis, points of agreement/disagreement, chosen resolution process, proposed or accepted actions and unresolved escalation.

## Quality Checks

- The result does not conceal dissent or convert a recommendation into consent.
- The process is proportionate and does not expose a party to avoidable harm to achieve closure.

## Handoffs

[Logical-consistency-review](../Critical-Thinking/logical-consistency-review.md) tests statement conflicts; actual decision makers or qualified mediators resolve matters outside the mandate.
