# Interest Mapping

Context: [Negotiator](README.md).

## Purpose

Separate positions from tested interests and identify joint gains.

## When to Use

Positions conflict or seem inflexible, but underlying needs may permit a different package.

## Boundary

Inferred motives remain hypotheses.

## Inputs

Parties’ actual statements, behavior, constraints, context and known decision rights.

## Method

1. Record each stated position accurately, then separate underlying goals, concerns, constraints and identity/relationship needs. Label inferred interests as hypotheses rather than facts.
2. Ask neutral questions about purpose, consequences and acceptable alternatives; use supplied evidence or authorized conversation rather than mind-reading.
3. Distinguish shared interests, differing valuations, genuine conflicts and nonnegotiable rights/constraints. Identify issues that can be traded and those that cannot.
4. Generate a few options that could satisfy interests through different means. Test each party’s acceptance criteria without pretending the options are approved offers.
5. Update the map when new evidence contradicts an inferred motive and identify the unresolved question most likely to unlock progress.

## Position-to-Interest Test

An interest explains why a position matters; it should suggest more than one possible means of satisfaction. If the proposed “interest” merely restates the demanded term, ask what loss or benefit the term controls. Do not treat every conflict as a misunderstanding: some resource or value conflicts remain real after clarification.

## Output

Party/position/interest map, confidence in inferred interests, hard constraints, possible joint gains and targeted questions.

## Quality Checks

- No private motive is asserted from identity, tone or a single behavior.
- A joint-gain proposal does not override an affected party’s legitimate constraint or consent.

## Handoffs

[Stakeholder-analysis](stakeholder-analysis.md) identifies whose interests and authority are missing; [offer-strategy](offer-strategy.md) turns validated interests into conditional packages.
