# Bias Detection

Context: [Critical-Thinking](README.md).

## Purpose

Find selection and interpretation mechanisms that could distort a conclusion.

## When to Use

Evidence collection, selection or interpretation may systematically favor one conclusion.

## Boundary

Do not diagnose a person or treat a bias label as disproof.

## Inputs

Claim, selection process, inclusion/exclusion choices, incentives, chronology and contrary cases.

## Method

1. Locate the mechanism: who entered the sample, who disappeared, what was measured, which comparisons were selected and when the hypothesis was formed.
2. Compare included versus excluded cases, observed versus missing outcomes and pre-result versus post-result criteria. Inspect source dependence and incentives without treating motive alone as disproof.
3. Construct a relevant counterfactual procedure: blinded assessment, predefined criteria, an alternative denominator, a disconfirming search or the same rubric applied to the favored and unfavored option.
4. Estimate whether the mechanism could change magnitude, direction or applicability. Separate demonstrated distortion from risk of distortion; propose a correction and preserve the uncorrected result for comparison.

## Symmetry Without False Balance

Apply equal evidential standards, not equal belief. Stronger evidence may deserve more weight after a symmetric review. Name the selection or interpretation process rather than diagnosing a person as biased. A changed conclusion after new evidence is not itself inconsistency; a post-hoc rule selected to protect a favorite conclusion needs scrutiny.

## Output

Bias mechanism, affected claim/population, concrete comparison, likely consequence with uncertainty, and a corrective or sensitivity check.

## Quality Checks

- The finding is reproducible from a process choice or observation, not inferred from identity or disagreement.
- A proposed correction does not hide excluded cases or create a new selection problem.

## Handoffs

[Data-cleaning](../Data-Analyst/data-cleaning.md) preserves records; [source-evaluation](../Researcher/source-evaluation.md) traces provenance; this skill tests the distortion mechanism.
