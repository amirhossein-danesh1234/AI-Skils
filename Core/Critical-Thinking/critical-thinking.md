# Critical Thinking

Context: [Critical-Thinking](README.md).

## Purpose

Stress a consequential conclusion using alternatives, falsification and a premortem.

## When to Use

A consequential claim, recommendation or plan needs a proportionate challenge before reliance.

## Boundary

Not reflexive opposition or a substitute for domain validity.

## Inputs

Claim, intended use, strongest supporting evidence, relevant alternatives, stakes and decision deadline.

## Method

1. Reconstruct the strongest defensible version of the claim and its scope. Separate factual premises, inferred mechanism and value preference; do not attack a weaker claim the author did not make.
2. Identify the load-bearing premise or inference. Ask what observable result would be unlikely if it were true, then compare a credible alternative explanation against the same evidence.
3. Conduct a short premortem only when failure consequences warrant it: suppose the proposed action failed; trace a few plausible mechanisms, enabling conditions and early signals. Test second-order responses and displaced costs, not just the first effect.
4. Prioritize challenges by whether they change the conclusion or next action. Recommend the smallest discriminating check, a bounded qualification or acceptance of the claim as currently supported.

## Proportionate Falsification

A possibility is not a refutation. Distinguish a demonstrated counterexample, a plausible untested mechanism and a merely imaginable scenario. Ask whether the alternative explains the evidence better and what would falsify it too. Retain a supported conclusion when objections do not change its scope or decision; uncertainty does not require equal weight for every position.

## Output

A short assessment: what survives scrutiny, decisive weakness if any, change in confidence/scope, and the next check or reason to stop.

## Quality Checks

- The critique identifies an actual inference or premise and its consequence, not a catalogue of bias names.
- Failure scenarios are tied to a feasible prevention or monitoring decision; no endless challenge after the relevant gate is met.

## Handoffs

Use [assumption-analysis](assumption-analysis.md) for sensitive premises, [evidence-evaluation](evidence-evaluation.md) for support strength, and domain specialists when validity depends on their rules.
