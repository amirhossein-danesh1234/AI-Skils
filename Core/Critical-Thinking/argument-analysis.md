# Argument Analysis

Context: [Critical-Thinking](README.md).

## Purpose

Reconstruct premises, inference and conclusion, then test the weakest bridge.

## When to Use

A recommendation or explanation has a complex, disputed or hidden reasoning chain.

## Boundary

Not a source-discovery or final-choice workflow.

## Inputs

Original statement, context, supporting material and the conclusion the audience is being asked to accept.

## Method

1. Express the conclusion in one testable or decision-relevant sentence. Map each premise to the conclusion it supports; distinguish independent supporting paths from a chain in which every link must hold.
2. Recover implicit bridging premises charitably and label them as reconstructions. Separate deductive claims, probabilistic generalization, causal explanation and value-based recommendations.
3. Test each bridge with a counterexample or alternative mechanism. Check whether terms change meaning, a sample is generalized beyond coverage, or a preference is disguised as a factual premise.
4. Estimate the effect of the weakest link: does it invalidate the conclusion, narrow the scope or only reduce confidence? Offer the smallest repair that preserves the supported position.

## Inference Burden

A valid deduction can still have false premises. A sound observation can still support an invalid inference. For a recommendation, identify both the empirical belief and the value/constraint that make the proposed action desirable; agreement on facts does not settle values. Avoid treating an analogy as proof unless the relevant mechanism and boundary conditions transfer.

## Output

An argument map, labeled missing bridges, decisive counterexample or support, and a repaired or bounded conclusion.

## Quality Checks

- Quoted claims remain faithful to the source; reconstructed premises are not attributed as the author’s words.
- Each criticism distinguishes truth of the premises from validity or strength of the inference.

## Handoffs

[Researcher](../Researcher/README.md) obtains missing facts; [Decision-Analyst](../Decision-Analyst/README.md) handles the resulting choice, not this argument map.
