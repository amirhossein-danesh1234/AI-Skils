# Writing

Context: [Writer](README.md).

## Purpose

Produce a supported draft, report or summary for a defined reader action.

## When to Use

A message, explanation, report or summary needs a clear supported draft for a defined purpose.

## Boundary

Do not create facts or approval through confident wording.

## Inputs

Audience, purpose/action, source material, factual position, voice, format, length and publication/communication mandate.

## Method

1. State what the reader must understand or decide. Separate the author’s position from facts, estimates and unresolved questions before drafting.
2. Choose a structure that matches the task: answer-first for a decision, sequence for a procedure, or claim/evidence organization for an explanation. Include only context needed to interpret the result.
3. Draft from an evidence outline. Preserve quantities, attribution, uncertainty, exceptions and the distinction between a proposed action and an action already taken.
4. For a summary, compress coverage and wording without changing the source’s balance or scope. Keep material dissent and conditions; do not infer a universal conclusion from selected passages.
5. Revise for clarity and concision, then check factual fidelity and the reader’s next step. Deliver a draft unless actual sending/publication is authorized.

## Compression Without Certainty Inflation

A shorter sentence must not turn “may,” “estimated,” “in this sample” or “pending approval” into a fact. If space is tight, keep the caveat attached to the claim and remove less decision-relevant detail. A report’s executive summary must not promise more than its evidence section supports. Missing facts become targeted author queries, not invented prose.

## Output

A usable draft or summary, with only material factual/approval queries and any deliberate scope limits.

## Quality Checks

- Every consequential claim retains the source’s strength, scope and attribution.
- The draft serves the audience and task without adding unnecessary headings, boilerplate or calls to action.

## Handoffs

[Content-structuring](content-structuring.md) handles organization; [editing](editing.md) handles an existing draft. Researchers and domain owners resolve facts and substantive meaning.
