# Audience Analysis

Context: [Writer](README.md).

## Purpose

Define reader decisions, prior knowledge and accessibility needs.

## When to Use

A draft’s vocabulary, evidence depth or structure depends on who must use it.

## Boundary

Do not stereotype identity or infer private traits.

## Inputs

Intended readers, task/decision, known knowledge level, setting, accessibility needs and communication constraints.

## Method

1. Identify primary and secondary readers by their task, not stereotypes. Determine what they need to know, decide or do and what prior context is actually supported.
2. Separate essential concepts from optional depth. Identify likely misunderstandings, unfamiliar terms and evidence or objections that could materially affect trust or action.
3. Choose tone, terminology, format and level of explanation based on purpose and medium. Preserve precision for experts while supplying definitions where newcomers need them.
4. Design a reading path: immediate answer or action, necessary rationale, then optional detail. Avoid forcing every reader through all background.
5. Where uncertainty about the audience matters, use a small comprehension check or ask one focused question; otherwise state a reasonable assumption and proceed.

## Reader Task Over Demographic Guess

Do not infer beliefs, ability, vulnerability or private traits from identity. A reader’s role is a starting hypothesis, not proof of knowledge. Multiple audiences may need layered detail rather than a compromise that is too vague for one and too technical for another. Accessibility is a communication requirement to verify, not an excuse to remove meaningful nuance.

## Output

Brief audience/task profile, assumed knowledge, likely confusion, tone/detail decisions and a proposed reading path.

## Quality Checks

- Adaptation changes presentation, not the factual claim or the user’s position.
- No unnecessary audience research or private-data collection is introduced for a straightforward message.

## Handoffs

[Content-structuring](content-structuring.md) implements the reading path; domain specialists identify concepts that cannot be simplified without distortion.
