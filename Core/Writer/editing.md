# Editing

Context: [Writer](README.md).

## Purpose

Improve clarity and concision without changing material meaning.

## When to Use

An existing draft needs clearer, shorter or more consistent language without losing meaning.

## Boundary

Substantive changes to stance, certainty or obligations need owner review.

## Inputs

Actual draft, author intent/voice, audience, source facts, protected wording and allowed edit scope.

## Method

1. Identify the edit level: mechanical, clarity, structural or substantive. Preserve protected terminology and distinguish an ambiguity to query from a defect that can be corrected directly.
2. Check factual and logical consistency before polishing: numbers, units, dates, actor/obligation, negation, causality and qualification must agree with sources and across the draft.
3. Remove redundancy, vague referents and unnecessary abstraction. Prefer concrete wording while retaining essential uncertainty and distinctions.
4. Reorder or split only where it improves comprehension; preserve emphasis and the author’s position. Flag changes to stance, commitment, attribution or confidence for owner review.
5. Compare the revision against the original meaning and task. Return a clean revision with concise notes only for material decisions or unresolved queries.

## Semantic Diff

Read the revision as a different reader might: did “can” become “will,” a recommendation become an obligation, a correlation become a cause, or an estimate become a measured figure? A shorter draft can be less accurate. When a source is inconsistent, do not silently pick the more convenient version; preserve the issue for clarification.

## Output

Edited draft plus material factual/meaning queries and, when requested, a transparent change summary.

## Quality Checks

- The revision preserves scope, certainty, actors, obligations and important dissent.
- No stylistic preference is presented as a factual correction or used to erase the author’s voice.

## Handoffs

[Technical-writing](technical-writing.md) handles procedural precision; researchers/domain owners verify disputed facts.
