# Content Structuring

Context: [Writer](README.md).

## Purpose

Build a claim/evidence hierarchy and navigable reading sequence.

## When to Use

Supported material needs a coherent hierarchy or a draft is hard to navigate.

## Boundary

Structure cannot repair missing evidence or settle a disputed position.

## Inputs

Purpose, audience task, claims/evidence, constraints, source scope and desired output size.

## Method

1. Choose the organizing question and the answer or outcome the reader should reach. Separate main claims from supporting detail, examples and background.
2. Map each material claim to its evidence and qualifications. Identify missing bridges or unresolved positions before turning them into polished sections.
3. Order by reader need: decision and reasons, chronological steps, comparison criteria or concept dependencies. Avoid mixing organizing principles at the same level without a clear purpose.
4. Assign one job to each section and remove duplication. Put warnings and exceptions where they affect interpretation/action, not in a detached appendix.
5. Test the outline by reading only headings and opening sentences, then by tracing one reader task from start to finish.

## Argument and Navigation

A structure is useful when its sequence reduces the reader’s inference burden. A table clarifies repeated comparisons; prose is better for one nuanced relationship. Do not introduce a visual or elaborate taxonomy solely because material has several parts. If the evidence does not support the desired conclusion, expose the gap rather than arranging the document to hide it.

## Output

A concise outline or reorganized draft with section purposes, claim/evidence placement and unresolved structural gaps.

## Quality Checks

- Each section advances the reader’s task and each material caveat is near its claim.
- The outline does not imply causality, completeness or certainty absent from the source.

## Handoffs

[Argument-analysis](../Critical-Thinking/argument-analysis.md) checks reasoning; [writing](writing.md) drafts the approved structure.
