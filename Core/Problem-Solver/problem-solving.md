# Problem Solving

Context: [Problem-Solver](README.md).

## Purpose

Move from an observed gap through hypotheses, tests and verification.

## When to Use

An observed gap needs an explanation or intervention and the correct path is not established.

## Boundary

Do not begin by implementing a favorite solution.

## Inputs

Observed and expected states, chronology, affected/unaffected cases, constraints, current evidence and safe test scope.

## Method

1. Frame the gap in observable terms and confirm that it is real rather than a measurement or expectation mismatch. Separate the requested remedy from the problem it is meant to solve.
2. Decompose the problem by boundaries, conditions or causal paths. Generate competing explanations, including a measurement/process explanation and a case that would falsify the favored one.
3. Choose the smallest safe test that discriminates among plausible mechanisms. Predict outcomes before observing them and avoid changing multiple uncontrolled factors at once.
4. Update the explanation from results, then design a bounded intervention linked to the supported mechanism. A reversible mitigation may be useful before full causal certainty if authorized and honestly labeled.
5. Verify the intended outcome, side effects and persistence against the original criteria. Stop at supported resolution, bounded mitigation or a specific unresolved evidence/authority gap.

## No Solution-First Shortcut

The first plausible cause is a hypothesis, not a diagnosis. Repeatedly trying fixes without preserving observations can destroy the evidence needed to distinguish causes. Keep a brief hypothesis/test/result trail. Do not insist on discovering a universal root cause when a safe bounded result meets the task; conversely, symptom relief is not proof that the mechanism was removed.

## Output

Problem statement, tested hypotheses, evidence-supported explanation or uncertainty, intervention recommendation and verification status.

## Quality Checks

- The final result answers the original gap rather than merely completing a chosen action.
- Tests stay inside the actual mandate and do not use consequential live effects as casual experiments.
