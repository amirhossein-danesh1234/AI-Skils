# Hypothesis Generation

Context: [Problem-Solver](README.md).

## Purpose

Build competing mechanisms with distinguishing predictions.

## When to Use

A defined gap has several possible explanations and needs discriminating tests.

## Boundary

A plausible story or correlation is not a confirmed cause.

## Inputs

Problem boundary, chronology, observations, counterexamples, known mechanisms and safe observation/test access.

## Method

1. Generate a small set of materially different mechanisms, including measurement error or changed conditions when plausible. Avoid many cosmetic versions of one favored explanation.
2. For each mechanism, state what it predicts in affected and unaffected cases, what evidence supports it and what observation would contradict it.
3. Rank by explanatory fit and decision consequence while keeping prior plausibility separate from proof. Do not invent numerical probabilities without a defensible basis.
4. Choose tests that separate leading alternatives at acceptable cost/risk. Prefer a result that changes the next action over a test that every hypothesis predicts.
5. Update, combine or retire hypotheses after results; preserve contrary observations rather than adding ad hoc exceptions indefinitely.

## Discriminating Prediction

A hypothesis explains more than the observed symptom: it should predict where the symptom will not appear or how it changes under a controlled condition. If every possible outcome confirms it, it is not currently testable. Multiple mechanisms may interact; a failed single-factor test does not eliminate an interaction without the needed design.

## Output

Hypothesis table with mechanism, supporting/contrary evidence, predictions, ranked test and update rule.

## Quality Checks

- The selected test has different expected outcomes under at least two credible alternatives.
- No hypothesis is labeled confirmed merely because the first attempted intervention seemed to help.

## Handoffs

[Root-cause-analysis](root-cause-analysis.md) follows causal paths; domain specialists validate mechanism plausibility and safe tests.
