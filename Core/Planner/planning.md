# Planning

Context: [Planner](README.md).

## Purpose

Turn an accepted objective into a feasible adaptive sequence.

## When to Use

An accepted objective needs a feasible sequence with ownership and adaptation.

## Boundary

Professional delivery rules remain with domain planners.

## Inputs

Accepted outcome, scope/constraints, actual capacity, dependencies, deadline basis and available estimates.

## Method

1. Define completion as an observable outcome and separate mandatory conditions from preferences. Keep the chosen objective fixed unless the owner explicitly reopens it.
2. Work backward to the minimum necessary sub-outcomes and decisions. Include evidence collection, integration, acceptance and transition work, not just production of intermediate artifacts.
3. Match work to real availability and required capabilities. Estimate remaining effort with the people or systems that must perform it; distinguish working effort from elapsed waiting time.
4. Sequence prerequisites and scarce resources, expose the binding constraint and put uncertainty buffers where they protect an actual commitment.
5. Set checkpoints that can change a decision early enough, then define what happens if capacity, scope or a dependency changes. Present infeasible scope/date/resource combinations for owner choice.

## Plan as a Conditional Model

A plan is an explicit relationship between scope, capacity, dependency timing and accepted results. It is not proof that resources or approvals exist. Use ranges or scenarios when estimates are weak and identify the next step that will narrow them. Prefer a short useful plan for a small reversible task; add ceremony only when coordination or consequences require it.

## Output

Outcome-based sequence, owner/availability assumptions, dependency gates, forecast range, contingency and next checkpoint.

## Quality Checks

- Essential support, review and recovery work are included and no resource is double-booked.
- The plan can report a feasible reduced scope or a blocked commitment instead of inventing a date.

## Handoffs

Use focused planning skills for bottlenecks. Work Project Manager, Academic Planner, Personal Planner or another domain planner retains professional scheduling rules and commitments.
