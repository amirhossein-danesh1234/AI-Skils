# Execution Review

Context: [Planner](README.md).

## Purpose

Reconcile accepted progress, remaining effort and baseline changes.

## When to Use

A plan checkpoint or material change requires an honest progress and replan decision.

## Boundary

Do not equate activity or ticket count with outcome completion.

## Inputs

Original/current baseline, accepted outputs, actual effort, scope changes/approvals, remaining work and dependency/capacity updates.

## Method

1. Preserve the original baseline and reconcile approved changes separately. Distinguish accepted outcomes, implemented-but-unaccepted work, in-progress work and blocked or unknown state.
2. Compare evidence with expected progress. Separate scope, capacity, dependency, estimate and execution variance; do not double-count overlapping causes or infer blame from a slip.
3. Re-estimate remaining work and acceptance effort with current information. Do not forecast solely from percentage complete, ticket count or a desired productivity increase.
4. Identify the binding blocker and compare feasible recovery options: resequence, reduce scope, change timing or add genuinely useful capacity within authority.
5. Record owner decisions, revised baseline/forecast, residual conditions and the next observable checkpoint.

## Progress Evidence

Activity can increase while accepted value stays flat. A producer’s completion claim must meet the consumer’s acceptance condition. Respect real approvals, including valid verbal ones under the actual process, while keeping product/scope approval distinct from budget, staffing or delivery-commitment approval. An adverse review can be complete even when no feasible recovery date is yet supported.

## Output

Evidence-based status, reconciled variance, remaining-work forecast, recovery trade-offs and explicit owner decisions or blockers.

## Quality Checks

- The revised plan does not erase the original promise or silently accept new scope.
- No unapproved overtime, imagined resource or unsupported velocity gain fills the schedule gap.

## Handoffs

[Problem-Solver](../Problem-Solver/README.md) investigates a specific blocker; domain delivery owners retain professional commitments.
