# Resource Planning

Context: [Planner](README.md).

## Purpose

Reconcile skill-specific availability, demand and switching costs.

## When to Use

A plan needs a credible capacity ledger or exposes a resource conflict.

## Boundary

No fictional 100 percent availability or unauthorized allocation.

## Inputs

Dated availability by resource/skill, work demand, calendars, support/maintenance, leave, allocation authority and uncertainty.

## Method

1. Establish gross availability over a named horizon and unit. Deduct known leave, support, recurring duties and nonoverlapping coordination; state each percentage’s denominator.
2. Map demand to required capabilities, equipment, access and approvals. Headcount or total hours are not interchangeable capacity when a particular skill or resource binds.
3. Include switching, onboarding, review and integration effort. Check overlapping commitments and availability at the time needed, not only aggregate monthly totals.
4. Compare feasible allocations, sequencing, reduced scope or delayed commitments. Treat proposed hires, contractors or budget as conditional until actually approved and usable.
5. Stress a plausible absence or overrun and retain a justified reserve. Return the capacity gap and trade-off to the actual allocation owner.

## Denominator and Scarcity Ledger

If support is stated as a fraction of gross time, deduct it from gross time consistently; do not silently change its basis after leave or another deduction. Avoid double subtraction when calendars already exclude duties. Reserve is explicit uncertainty capacity, not a universal percentage. No plan assumes theoretical 100% productive utilization or unpaid overtime to close a gap.

## Output

Capacity/demand ledger by period and scarce capability, commitments, assumptions, shortfalls, conditional resources and owner choices.

## Quality Checks

- Every unit is counted once and resource transfers have an identified source.
- A proposed resource contributes within the needed window after ramp/coordination costs, not merely after its nominal start date.

## Handoffs

Domain managers confirm actual allocation and professional obligations; [timeline-design](timeline-design.md) uses accepted availability.
