# Scenario Analysis

Context: [Decision-Analyst](README.md).

## Purpose

Compare options across coherent uncertain futures with signposts.

## When to Use

A choice depends on interacting uncertainties that make a single forecast misleading.

## Boundary

Scenario narratives are not forecasts or invented probabilities.

## Inputs

Decision options, horizon, uncertain drivers, known constraints, lead times and observable signposts.

## Method

1. Select the few uncertainties capable of changing the decision. Separate controllable actions from external conditions and fixed constraints.
2. Construct internally coherent futures with causal links and timing, including an adverse but plausible combination. Do not combine mutually inconsistent extremes merely to label a worst case.
3. Evaluate each option under each future using the same outcome definitions. Note viability failures, relative regret and actions that remain useful across futures.
4. Design contingent actions and signposts, including the latest time a signal can arrive for the response to work. Compare the cost of preserving an option with its possible value.
5. Recommend a robust action or a clearly conditioned strategy; distinguish scenario coverage from probability-weighted prediction.

## Scenarios Are Not Forecasts

Do not assign equal probabilities because there are three scenarios, or turn optimistic/base/pessimistic percentages into independent evidence. Use probabilities only with a justified model. If the signpost arrives after the irreversible commitment, it cannot protect that commitment; reduce exposure earlier or accept the downside explicitly.

## Output

Driver assumptions, coherent scenario paths, option consequences, robust/contingent choice and timed signposts.

## Quality Checks

- Scenarios differ in mechanisms that matter to the choice, not just arbitrary percentage changes.
- Each contingency has feasible lead time and a responsible decision owner.

## Handoffs

[Risk-analysis](risk-analysis.md) examines failure paths; [Planner](../Planner/README.md) schedules approved contingent actions.
