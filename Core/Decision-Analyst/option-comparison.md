# Option Comparison

Context: [Decision-Analyst](README.md).

## Purpose

Normalize credible options, remove dominance and test ranking sensitivity.

## When to Use

Credible alternatives need a fair side-by-side comparison for one framed decision.

## Boundary

Does not choose preferences for the user.

## Inputs

Feasible options, common objective/horizon, approved criteria, constraints, evidence and uncertainty ranges.

## Method

1. Normalize scope, timing, units, service/outcome level and included costs. Keep unavailable or conditional options labeled; do not compare a concrete current option with an idealized alternative.
2. Include the status quo and materially distinct routes, not cosmetic variants. Identify commitments and opportunity costs omitted from headline benefits.
3. Screen hard failures, then identify dominance: an option is dominated only if another is no worse on all relevant dimensions and better on at least one under the stated evidence.
4. Compare remaining trade-offs and test plausible reversals. If uncertain ranges overlap or preferences are unstated, report conditional leaders rather than inventing a winner.
5. Recommend a candidate or the precise owner/evidence question needed, and specify what would change the comparison.

## Comparable Basis

Use one evidence basis or explicitly reconcile differences. A quoted price, estimated cost and measured lifecycle cost are not interchangeable. Keep unknown separate from zero and “not applicable.” Do not hide the cost of waiting or the best displaced use simply because it is not on an invoice.

## Output

Normalized comparison, exclusions/conditions, dominated options with reasons, sensitivity and a conditional or robust recommendation.

## Quality Checks

- The result survives a check for unequal scope and double-counted benefits.
- A reader can reproduce why an option is eligible, excluded or preferred without trusting an unexplained score.

## Handoffs

[Trade-off-analysis](trade-off-analysis.md) examines contested exchanges; [scenario-analysis](scenario-analysis.md) tests coherent uncertain futures.
