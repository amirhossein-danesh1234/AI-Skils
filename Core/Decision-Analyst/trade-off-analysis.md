# Trade Off Analysis

Context: [Decision-Analyst](README.md).

## Purpose

Expose marginal exchanges and displaced alternatives on the feasible frontier.

## When to Use

Feasible options improve some valued outcomes while worsening others.

## Boundary

Not a ceremonial list of pros and cons.

## Inputs

Nondominated alternatives, owner values, constraints, incremental consequences and the displaced next-best use.

## Method

1. Locate the actual exchange: what improves, what worsens, for whom and when? Separate total from marginal consequences and preserve any noncompensable constraint.
2. Find the efficient frontier among feasible options. Compare the incremental gain and sacrifice between adjacent meaningful choices, not just their total scores.
3. Ask for a threshold preference when needed: how much cost, delay or lost flexibility is acceptable for the incremental benefit? Keep the answer conditional if the owner has not supplied that value.
4. Search for a redesign, sequence or staged choice that relaxes the conflict without assuming a free improvement. Include transition burden and effects shifted onto other people.
5. Return the trade-off and the conditions under which each alternative is preferable.

## Opportunity Cost and Thresholds

Opportunity cost is the value of the best feasible displaced alternative, not the sum of all rejected possibilities. Separate sunk costs from future avoidable costs, while retaining real exit obligations. When feasible, state the break point at which the preferred option changes; if units or utilities are incomparable, use an explicit owner choice instead of forced arithmetic.

## Output

Trade-off frontier or concise exchange table, affected parties, switching threshold, opportunity cost and unresolved value judgment.

## Quality Checks

- No claimed win-win merely exports the cost to an omitted stakeholder or later period.
- The recommendation preserves the owner’s values rather than treating the analyst’s preferences as neutral.

## Handoffs

[Negotiator](../Negotiator/README.md) may develop mutually acceptable exchanges; [decision-making](decision-making.md) integrates the choice.
