# Research

Context: [Researcher](README.md).

## Purpose

Acquire sufficient traceable evidence with a bounded search strategy.

## When to Use

A bounded question needs external or supplied-source evidence before an answer.

## Boundary

Not maximum browsing or a domain interpretation authority.

## Inputs

Question, decision use, scope, freshness requirement, accessible sources and time/effort budget.

## Method

1. Break the question into the few claims that must be resolved. Define what evidence would answer each and what uncertainty is acceptable for this use.
2. Search supplied/local authoritative material first when appropriate, then targeted external sources. Vary terms, synonyms, dates and contrary phrasing; follow a claim to its originating evidence rather than copying search summaries.
3. Inspect the actual source content, method, date and scope. Record a locator for material facts and distinguish publication/update date from the date of the underlying event or data.
4. Triangulate consequential claims with genuinely independent evidence where available. Trace syndication, shared datasets and repeated company claims before counting corroboration.
5. Synthesize an answer proportionate to support, attach citations to the claims they establish, and stop when required claims are sufficiently resolved or a specific access/evidence limit remains.

## Search Stop Rule

Do not continue merely to increase the source count. Stop when new credible searches no longer change a material claim, the remaining uncertainty cannot alter the due choice, or the budget/access boundary is reached. A consequential unresolved claim stays qualified or blocked; budget exhaustion cannot turn it into a fact. A source found but not inspected is a lead, not consulted evidence.

## Output

Answer with claim-level sources, dates, agreement/conflict, explicit inference/unknowns and a concise search/access limit.

## Quality Checks

- Citations point to actual consulted material supporting the nearby claim, not just a related homepage.
- Freshness matches the decision; old evidence is not presented as a current rule, price or state.

## Handoffs

Use [source-evaluation](source-evaluation.md) for a material credibility question and [deep-research](deep-research.md) only when interacting claims need a larger investigation.
