# Source Evaluation

Context: [Researcher](README.md).

## Purpose

Assess provenance, method, applicability, freshness and source dependence.

## When to Use

A source’s origin, method, currency or applicability could change reliance on its claims.

## Boundary

Primary and official are provenance categories, not automatic truth.

## Inputs

Actual source, claim to support, publication/data dates, author/publisher, methods and source chain.

## Method

1. Identify the source and original evidence. Distinguish first-hand data/document, secondary interpretation, vendor/company statement and community experience; these categories can overlap.
2. Inspect how the claim was produced: measurement, sample, comparison, methodology, access to evidence, incentive and correction/retraction history when relevant.
3. Check applicability to the target population, jurisdiction, version and time. Verify whether an update changes the claim or merely the page date.
4. Trace citations and repeated wording to detect dependent sources. Compare with an independent source on the material point, preserving conflict if scope cannot reconcile it.
5. Assign claim-specific use: direct support, contextual background, lead requiring verification or unusable for this conclusion. Record the reason and any remaining access limit.

## Provenance Is Not Truth

A primary company announcement is primary evidence of what the company claims, not independent proof of performance. A community report can reveal a failure mode but not its prevalence. Official status does not cure an inapplicable version, and a secondary synthesis can be stronger than an isolated primary anecdote. Separate source quality from the strength of the inference drawn from it.

## Output

Source record with origin, claim/locator, evidence category, dates, method/interest limits, independence and permitted interpretation.

## Quality Checks

- No source is ranked solely by search position, domain prestige or citation count.
- A source whose body is inaccessible is not described as read; snippets are used only within their visible scope.

## Handoffs

[Evidence-evaluation](../Critical-Thinking/evidence-evaluation.md) judges claim support; domain specialists determine professional applicability.
