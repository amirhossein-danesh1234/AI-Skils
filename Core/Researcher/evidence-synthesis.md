# Evidence Synthesis

Context: [Researcher](README.md).

## Purpose

Combine compatible claim evidence while preserving disagreement and gaps.

## When to Use

Multiple sources must support a coherent answer while retaining differences in scope and strength.

## Boundary

Not raw statistical pooling or majority voting among citations.

## Inputs

Claim question, consulted sources with locators, methods/populations, dates, independence and evidence limitations.

## Method

1. Extract claims into a matrix with source, observed finding, scope, method, uncertainty and whether it directly supports the target claim.
2. Group evidence by compatible question, population, outcome and period. Identify common datasets or copied claims; count independent evidence, not documents.
3. Explain agreement and disagreement through definitions, design, timing, measurement and applicability. Do not average incompatible quantities or resolve conflict by citation majority.
4. Weight the conclusion by relevance and methodological support. Separate direct evidence, reasonable inference and unresolved unknown; preserve credible contrary evidence.
5. Write the strongest supported synthesis and identify which missing observation would change it. Keep each material conclusion traceable to actual passages/data.

## Synthesis Without Flattening

A coherent narrative must not erase heterogeneity. Several weak dependent reports cannot outweigh a directly relevant robust observation merely through volume. Quantitative pooling requires a justified statistical design and compatible estimates; otherwise use structured qualitative comparison. “Evidence is mixed” should name what differs and why it matters, not conceal an unresolved choice.

## Output

Claim-level synthesis, support/conflict matrix, scope and confidence limits, citations and decision-sensitive gaps.

## Quality Checks

- Each conclusion can be traced back to evidence without relying on the synthesis as its own source.
- Uncertainty and dissent remain visible in the summary, not only in notes.

## Handoffs

[Critical-Thinking](../Critical-Thinking/README.md) challenges the inference; [Writer](../Writer/README.md) communicates it without changing evidential strength.
