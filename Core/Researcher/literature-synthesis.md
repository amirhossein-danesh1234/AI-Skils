# Literature Synthesis

Context: [Researcher](README.md).

## Purpose

Map a defined body of studies, methods and unresolved findings.

## When to Use

A defined collection of studies or scholarly work needs a defensible map of findings and gaps.

## Boundary

Not a systematic-review or meta-analysis claim without its actual method.

## Inputs

Research question, study corpus or search access, scope/eligibility, available full texts, methods and intended review type.

## Method

1. State whether this is a supplied-corpus synthesis, scoped narrative review or a formally designed systematic review. Define search boundaries and inclusion/exclusion before claiming coverage.
2. Deduplicate reports and link multiple publications from one study. Extract question, design, population/context, outcomes, findings, uncertainty and methodological limitations.
3. Group by mechanism, method, outcome or context rather than presenting one disconnected paragraph per paper. Examine inconsistent findings and whether designs actually test the same proposition.
4. Assess bias, missing/unpublished results, retractions/corrections and transfer limits where relevant. A gap is a question the existing evidence cannot answer, not simply a topic absent from a small corpus.
5. Synthesize supported conclusions and a research agenda tied to unresolved mechanisms or design weaknesses. Report search/corpus limitations and preserve study-level citations.

## Coverage and Review Claims

Do not call an informal search systematic, or a qualitative synthesis a meta-analysis. Formal review methods require their own protocol, search/selection record and suitable specialist/statistical work. Reporting guidance such as [PRISMA 2020](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020-statement) can support a requested systematic-review report; following a checklist does not establish exhaustive coverage or sound methodology by itself.

## Output

Review scope/method, study matrix, thematic or methodological synthesis, disagreement, supported gaps and traceable bibliography.

## Quality Checks

- Study independence is preserved and the bibliography contains only identifiable consulted works or explicitly labeled uninspected leads.
- A research-gap claim is bounded to the reviewed evidence; no invented novelty or universal absence claim.

## Handoffs

University Scientific Researcher or another domain expert validates subject interpretation; [statistical-reasoning](../Data-Analyst/statistical-reasoning.md) supports appropriate quantitative work.
