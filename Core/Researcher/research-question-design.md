# Research Question Design

Context: [Researcher](README.md).

## Purpose

Translate a decision need into answerable claims and evidence requirements.

## When to Use

A vague information request must become answerable without losing its decision purpose.

## Boundary

Does not silently replace the user objective with an easier question.

## Inputs

Original request, intended decision, relevant population/context, desired output, known constraints and accessible evidence.

## Method

1. State the decision or understanding the answer should enable. Separate descriptive, comparative, causal, predictive and normative questions; each needs different evidence.
2. Bound subject/population, geography/context, time, alternatives and outcome. Preserve the user’s actual scope; an easier proxy question must be disclosed and accepted if it changes the purpose.
3. Decompose into answerable claims and identify the evidence type and freshness needed for each. Separate facts that sources can establish from preferences or policies the owner must supply.
4. Define inclusion/exclusion criteria and what would count as a sufficient answer or disconfirming observation. Identify an inaccessible or ill-posed claim early.
5. Prioritize claims by their ability to change the next decision and set a proportional search budget and stopping condition.

## Question-to-Evidence Contract

Ask “what observation would distinguish the alternatives?” before choosing search terms. “Best” is not researchable until criteria and constraints are named. If the evidence can answer only association while the user asks causation, preserve the causal question and state the gap rather than silently lowering the burden.

## Output

Scoped question, subclaims, evidence/freshness requirements, exclusion rules, owner inputs and stop criteria.

## Quality Checks

- The proposed answer would affect the original decision rather than merely describe the topic.
- Missing preferences or authority are not treated as facts that more searching will discover.

## Handoffs

[Decision-making](../Decision-Analyst/decision-making.md) frames an unresolved choice; [research](research.md) executes the bounded evidence plan.
