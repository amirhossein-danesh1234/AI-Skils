# Deep Research

Context: [Researcher](README.md).

## Purpose

Resolve a multi-claim question through adaptive bounded investigation.

## When to Use

A consequential multi-claim question has interacting uncertainties or conflicting evidence that targeted lookup cannot resolve.

## Boundary

Not the default for a simple question or an unbounded search promise.

## Inputs

Scoped decision question, claim map, access, budget, initial evidence, deadline and uncertainty tolerance.

## Method

1. Build an investigation map of material claims, dependencies and competing explanations. Mark which unresolved claim gates the rest; avoid researching downstream detail before that gate.
2. Search in bounded rounds, each with a specific evidence target. Combine direct records, appropriate studies/data and independent critiques; record query/source/date and selection rationale.
3. Update the claim ledger after each round. Trace high-impact assertions to originals, reconcile version/population differences and deliberately seek evidence against the leading interpretation.
4. Reallocate effort toward decision-sensitive uncertainty. Request specialist interpretation where method or domain constraints exceed Core; do not replace it with more generic sources.
5. Synthesize a coherent answer with claim-level confidence and unresolved conflicts. Explain coverage, exclusions, access limits and why investigation stopped.

## Adaptive Depth

Depth means resolving an inferential bottleneck, not maximizing document length. Set a review checkpoint before an irreversible decision or the research budget is consumed. Continue only when the next feasible search can plausibly change the answer enough to justify delay. If an authoritative source is unavailable, report the exact limitation and bounded alternatives; do not fill the gap with repeated secondary claims.

## Output

Decision-focused research report, evidence/claim ledger, competing interpretations, source trail, remaining gaps and stop rationale.

## Quality Checks

- New rounds have a decision-changing target and do not repeat the same source chain.
- The report distinguishes verified claims from estimates, inference and unknowns even when the overall narrative is compelling.

## Handoffs

[Evidence-synthesis](evidence-synthesis.md) integrates heterogeneous material; [literature-synthesis](literature-synthesis.md) is for a defined body of studies.
