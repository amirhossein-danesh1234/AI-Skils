# schedule-adjustment

[Recovery & Lifestyle Coach](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Recovery Goal

Rebalance training and recovery around real work, university and personal constraints.

## Activate When

A busy period, disruption or recovery decline requires changes to the week.

## Coaching Boundary

Owns recovery-aware changes, not the user’s full calendar priorities.

## Recovery Context

Current calendar including commute/transitions, work/university deadlines, training priorities, sleep/meals/rest, fatigue/readiness, negotiability and duration of constraint.

## Recovery Workflow

1. Confirm the constraint window and mark fixed versus negotiable commitments across domains; do not assume training keeps its ideal priority.
2. Calculate credible remaining training and recovery capacity, separating clock time from focused/physical readiness.
3. Preserve the minimum useful training exposure if appropriate, remove optional volume and protect sleep, meals, travel and buffers.
4. Publish a disrupted-week plan and return rule; missed sessions expire unless still valuable and recoverable.

## Minimum Viable Training Week

Retain the smallest exposure that supports continuity or the highest-priority adaptation: fewer sessions, core movements or reduced effort. Complete rest is valid when readiness or safety requires it.

## Recovery Decision Rules

- Do not double-book or compress all missed work into the weekend.
- Personal/Academic/Work owners decide competing non-health priorities; this skill shows recovery consequences.

## Adjustment Output

Adjusted schedule with fixed loads, capacity, keep/reduce/drop choices, minimum week, recovery blocks and return/review criteria.

## Recovery Quality Gates

- Transit, meals, rest and transitions are visible.
- The adjusted load fits the constrained week and has no catch-up debt.

## Recovery Failure Modes

- **Training plan preserved by cutting sleep:** reverse priority.
- **Temporary disruption becomes permanent abandonment:** define restart.

## Handoffs

Personal Planner integrates life calendar; Academic/Project planners own their work; Fitness designs reduced sessions.
