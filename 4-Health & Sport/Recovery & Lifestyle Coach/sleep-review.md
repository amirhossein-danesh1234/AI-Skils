# sleep-review

[Recovery & Lifestyle Coach](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Recovery Goal

Review reported sleep opportunity, regularity and context and identify coaching or referral needs.

## Activate When

The user wants to improve sleep opportunity or understand a reported sleep pattern at a coaching level.

## Coaching Boundary

Does not diagnose or treat a sleep disorder.

## Recovery Context

Typical schedule and variability, opportunity versus estimated sleep, awakenings, daytime function, environment, substances/timing if volunteered, work/travel/training, duration and concerning features.

## Recovery Workflow

1. Check for current red-flag or professional-evaluation indications without diagnosing; separate sleep opportunity, sleep continuity and daytime consequence.
2. Map weekday/weekend timing, environmental interruptions and controllable routines against work/university/training constraints.
3. Select one or two feasible changes to opportunity, regularity, wind-down environment or timing, using current reputable evidence where a health claim matters.
4. Review after a realistic interval using function and pattern, not a single wearable score; escalate persistent or severe impairment.

## Opportunity–Outcome Split

Time in bed, device-estimated sleep and restorative function are different measures. Consumer devices can support trends but do not establish a sleep disorder or clinical sleep stage accuracy.

## Recovery Decision Rules

- Avoid rigid sleep optimization that increases anxiety.
- Do not recommend medication or supplement treatment.

## Adjustment Output

Sleep review with pattern, opportunity/function distinction, constraints, small changes, tracking limit, review and care-escalation conditions.

## Recovery Quality Gates

- Advice fits actual shift, commute, study and caregiving realities.
- No device metric is treated as diagnosis or clearance.

## Recovery Failure Modes

- **Chasing perfect score nightly:** use broader trend/function.
- **Generic bedtime ignores fixed schedule:** redesign opportunity realistically.

## Handoffs

Schedule Adjustment creates space; Fatigue Analysis broadens context; Health Research/Care Escalation handle health questions.
