# training-readiness

[Recovery & Lifestyle Coach](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Recovery Goal

Decide whether today’s planned training exposure should proceed, reduce, change or stop.

## Activate When

A planned session needs a same-day proceed, modify, substitute or stop decision.

## Coaching Boundary

Readiness screening is not medical clearance or diagnosis.

## Recovery Context

Planned session, recent training, sleep and fatigue trend, soreness/pain or symptoms, warm-up response, performance trend, work/university stress, nourishment/hydration and restrictions.

## Recovery Workflow

1. Screen new, unusual or concerning symptoms and qualified restrictions first; stop and escalate when appropriate rather than using a readiness score to override them.
2. Compare today with personal baseline across function, motivation/fatigue, soreness and warm-up performance; avoid universal cutoffs.
3. Choose proceed, reduce volume/intensity, substitute lower-cost work, recovery-only or stop; specify the exact modified dose.
4. Record response and define the next-session rule, escalating when impaired readiness persists or worsens.

## Readiness Decision

Use multiple signals and the consequence of being wrong. Subjective fatigue plus poor warm-up may justify reduction; one noisy wearable score alone should rarely dictate the day.

## Recovery Decision Rules

- Readiness tools inform training exposure, not medical clearance.
- Do not test maximal performance to decide whether someone is safe to train.

## Adjustment Output

Readiness call with baseline comparison, concern screen, decision, modified session/rest, monitoring and return/escalation criteria.

## Recovery Quality Gates

- The decision materially changes exposure when readiness is poor.
- The rationale uses trends/context and preserves uncertainty.

## Recovery Failure Modes

- **Score says green despite concerning symptom:** symptom gate wins.
- **“Listen to body” without operational option:** specify change.

## Handoffs

Fitness Recovery Adjustment implements; Fatigue/Sleep analyze trends; Red Flag/Care Escalation handles concern.
