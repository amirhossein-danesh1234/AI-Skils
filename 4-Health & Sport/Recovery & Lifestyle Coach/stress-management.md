# stress-management

[Recovery & Lifestyle Coach](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Recovery Goal

Select practical non-clinical responses to manageable stressors.

## Activate When

Manageable situational stress needs a practical non-clinical response plan.

## Coaching Boundary

Severe distress, crisis, trauma treatment and mental-health diagnosis require appropriate support.

## Recovery Context

Stressor and controllability, duration, functional impact, current coping, support, schedule/load, preferred strategies and any severe distress or safety indicators.

## Recovery Workflow

1. Assess whether this is ordinary situational stress or includes crisis, severe impairment or safety concerns requiring appropriate support.
2. Separate controllable problem, influence, uncertainty and unavoidable load; choose problem-solving, boundary/support, recovery or acceptance response accordingly.
3. Implement one low-burden action such as load reduction, brief decompression, social support or environmental change rather than a long wellness checklist.
4. Set a review and escalation trigger based on function and persistence; do not diagnose or frame coping difficulty as failure.

## Demand–Control Match

Use problem-focused action for controllable causes and recovery/acceptance/support for what cannot be immediately changed. A breathing or mindfulness exercise cannot substitute for removing an excessive demand when that is possible.

## Recovery Decision Rules

- Do not promise that a routine treats an anxiety, trauma or mood disorder.
- Respect user preference; no single coping practice is mandatory.

## Adjustment Output

Stress plan with context/risk screen, controllability map, chosen action, load/support adjustment, review and escalation route.

## Recovery Quality Gates

- The intervention is proportionate and feasible during the stressor.
- Severe distress or safety concerns are not managed solely with lifestyle coaching.

## Recovery Failure Modes

- **Coping tips obscure structural overload:** reduce demand.
- **Clinical language inferred from ordinary stress:** keep distinction.

## Handoffs

Personal/Communication supports boundaries; Schedule Adjustment reduces load; qualified mental-health/local emergency support handles crisis.
