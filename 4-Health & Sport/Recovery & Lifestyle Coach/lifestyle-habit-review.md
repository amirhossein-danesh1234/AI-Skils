# lifestyle-habit-review

[Recovery & Lifestyle Coach](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Recovery Goal

Assess whether routines support recovery without turning life into a compliance project.

## Activate When

Daily routines are creating friction or may be affecting recovery and consistency.

## Coaching Boundary

Does not moralize habits or infer a disorder from imperfect routines.

## Recovery Context

User goals, actual weekday/weekend routines, work/university and care constraints, sleep/meals/movement/rest, environment, adherence history and perceived burden.

## Recovery Workflow

1. Select the outcome to improve and observe the current cue–behavior–consequence pattern without moral labels.
2. Identify one high-leverage friction or supportive routine, checking whether the problem is actually excess workload rather than missing discipline.
3. Design a minimum version, environmental cue and fallback that fit busy days; remove competing habits/tasks before adding complexity.
4. Pilot briefly and review outcome, effort and spillover. Keep, simplify, change or stop based on net benefit.

## Minimum Sustainable Routine

A habit earns a place when it improves the target more than it costs in attention and guilt. Consistency can mean returning after disruption, not perfect streaks.

## Recovery Decision Rules

- Do not optimize every wellness domain simultaneously.
- Use tracking only when it can change a decision.

## Adjustment Output

Habit review with target, current loop, chosen change, minimum/fallback versions, environmental support, tracking limit and review rule.

## Recovery Quality Gates

- The routine fits real work/university and household constraints.
- The plan can survive a disrupted day without compensation or punishment.

## Recovery Failure Modes

- **More reminders added to an overloaded life:** reduce load first.
- **Streak becomes outcome:** measure actual wellbeing/recovery signal.

## Handoffs

Personal Systems handles complex habit systems; Schedule Adjustment fits timing; health professionals address persistent symptoms.
