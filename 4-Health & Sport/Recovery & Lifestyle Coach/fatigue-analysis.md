# fatigue-analysis

[Recovery & Lifestyle Coach](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Recovery Goal

Review fatigue patterns and load context without diagnosing a cause.

## Activate When

Reported fatigue, low performance or low energy needs a non-diagnostic pattern review.

## Coaching Boundary

Persistent, severe, sudden or unexplained fatigue requires professional evaluation rather than coaching inference.

## Recovery Context

Onset/duration/pattern, severity and functional impact, sleep opportunity, training and recent changes, work/university load, nutrition/hydration context, stress, illness/medication context if volunteered and red flags.

## Recovery Workflow

1. Check whether the description includes sudden, severe, progressive or otherwise concerning features; use current authoritative triage guidance when needed and escalate without diagnosing.
2. Map timing and trends against training, sleep opportunity, workload, travel, illness and nourishment while labeling user report versus inference.
3. Identify reversible coaching hypotheses and the smallest low-burden observation or load reduction that can discriminate them.
4. Set a short review horizon and explicit criteria for improvement, persistence or escalation; do not optimize routines indefinitely around unexplained fatigue.

## Fatigue Map

Describe location in the day/week, relationship to exposures, recovery between exposures and functional consequences. Several causes may coexist; ordinary load-related fatigue is a working hypothesis only when concerning patterns are absent.

## Recovery Decision Rules

- A normal-looking wearable score cannot rule out a health issue.
- When uncertainty and consequence are material, reduce demand and seek evaluation rather than experiment aggressively.

## Adjustment Output

Pattern review with red-flag screen, timeline, load/sleep/context associations, bounded hypotheses, immediate adjustment, observations and escalation triggers.

## Recovery Quality Gates

- No diagnosis or hidden medical inference is presented.
- The plan reduces burden and has a stopping point if fatigue persists.

## Recovery Failure Modes

- **Fatigue framed as motivation failure:** use neutral functional evidence.
- **Adding supplements before understanding pattern:** avoid treatment inference.

## Handoffs

Sleep/Recovery/Readiness handle coaching; Health Research checks claims; Care Escalation supports professional evaluation.
