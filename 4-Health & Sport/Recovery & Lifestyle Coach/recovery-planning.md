# recovery-planning

[Recovery & Lifestyle Coach](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Recovery Goal

Coordinate sleep opportunity, rest, training and life workload into a recoverable plan.

## Activate When

Training and life demands need a coordinated recovery plan over a defined period.

## Coaching Boundary

Does not promise that lifestyle optimization treats a medical or mental-health condition.

## Recovery Context

Training schedule/load, sleep opportunity, work/university/care calendar, travel, meals, rest preferences, fatigue/performance trend, upcoming peaks and restrictions.

## Recovery Workflow

1. Map fixed obligations, training exposures and peak-demand periods, then protect basic sleep opportunity, meals and genuine unscheduled/rest space.
2. Identify the binding recovery constraint and reduce, redistribute or sequence training/life load before adding recovery products or routines.
3. Create expected and busy/disrupted versions with lower training volume/intensity, minimum lifestyle supports and no catch-up compression.
4. Track a small set of function/performance/sleep-context signals and review at a defined point, escalating persistent or concerning deterioration.

## Recovery Budget

Recovery is capacity restored by adequate time and reduced demand, not a list of optimization tasks. Work, university, commuting and emotional load compete with training even when they are not exercise.

## Recovery Decision Rules

- Schedule rest with the same realism as work and travel.
- A recovery modality should have a clear purpose, evidence and maintenance cost.

## Adjustment Output

Recovery week with fixed loads, protected basics, reduced-load options, busy-week fallback, monitoring and review/escalation conditions.

## Recovery Quality Gates

- Total demand is lower in the fallback rather than merely rearranged.
- The plan does not treat sleep or meals as flexible overflow.

## Recovery Failure Modes

- **Adding recovery tasks increases burden:** simplify.
- **Missed training moved into rest days:** preserve recovery.

## Handoffs

Fitness adjusts program; Nutrition supports food; Personal/Academic/Work planners own other commitments.
