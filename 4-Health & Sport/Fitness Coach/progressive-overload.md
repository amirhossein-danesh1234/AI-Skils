# progressive-overload

[Fitness Coach](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Coaching Goal

Progress training stimulus gradually from demonstrated performance and recovery.

## Activate When

Current training is tolerated and the next progression needs a rule.

## Training Boundary

Overload is not a mandate to add load every session or train through concerning symptoms.

## Training Context

Goal, exercise and technique standard, recent sets/reps/load/duration or pace, effort and recovery trend, progression history, schedule and symptoms.

## Coaching Protocol

1. Define the performance quality that must remain stable and the signal showing the current dose is repeatable.
2. Select one progression dimension—load, repetitions, sets, range, tempo, density, complexity or frequency—aligned with the goal.
3. Set a small increment and upper/lower decision thresholds, including repeat, regress and deload options. Avoid automatic calendar-based increases.
4. Observe the next exposures for performance, technique, effort and recovery, then update only after the response is credible.

## Progression Gate

Progress only when the current prescription is completed at the intended quality and tolerability across sufficient exposure. Overload means a purposeful stimulus trend; it can include improved execution or range, not only more weight.

## Training Decision Rules

- Do not progress several variables simultaneously unless the program phase explicitly requires it.
- Pain, neurological symptoms, faintness or other concerning changes stop the progression and route appropriately.

## Program Output

Progression rule with baseline, chosen variable, increment, quality threshold, repeat/regress/deload criteria, monitoring and review timing.

## Training Quality Gates

- The progression is measurable and compatible with the goal.
- The rule permits holding or reducing load without treating it as failure.

## Training Failure Modes

- **Load increases because the calendar says so:** use response gates.
- **Technique degrades but target is counted:** protect the standard.

## Handoffs

Intensity/Volume own dose variables; Progress Review decides from trends; Health Research/care handles symptom questions.
