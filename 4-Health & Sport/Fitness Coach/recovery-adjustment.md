# recovery-adjustment

[Fitness Coach](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Coaching Goal

Modify training exposure when recovery has deteriorated.

## Activate When

Training performance or recovery signals have worsened enough to reconsider current exposure.

## Training Boundary

Handles training adjustments, while broader fatigue causes and medical concerns route elsewhere.

## Training Context

Recent training load and changes, sleep opportunity, life workload, soreness/fatigue trend, performance, motivation, nourishment/hydration context, symptoms and deadlines.

## Coaching Protocol

1. Check for red flags or unexplained deterioration before assuming ordinary training fatigue; route concerning cases rather than prescribing through them.
2. Compare current load with baseline and identify whether frequency, volume, intensity, novelty or life stress changed together.
3. Choose the smallest effective adjustment: reduce sets/load/effort, swap exercise, shorten session, add rest or temporarily pause, while preserving the goal where feasible.
4. Define readiness and recovery observations for resuming or rebuilding; avoid catching up missed work by compression.

## Exposure Triage

Preserve high-value skill or movement practice with a lower fatigue cost when ordinary recovery is limited. When the cause is unclear or symptoms are concerning, uncertainty itself can justify stopping and professional evaluation.

## Training Decision Rules

- Reduce training demand before adding a complex recovery routine.
- A deload is a planned reduction, not punishment or proof of failure.

## Program Output

Adjustment with observed trigger, risk screen, changed dose, minimum viable session/rest option, return criteria and follow-up.

## Training Quality Gates

- The adjustment materially lowers fatigue demand.
- It does not override clinical restrictions or delay needed evaluation.

## Training Failure Modes

- **Keeping intensity and volume while shortening rest:** fatigue is not reduced.
- **Missed sessions rescheduled into overload:** let them go.

## Handoffs

Recovery Coach owns broader sleep/load plan; Readiness informs today; Care Escalation handles concern.
