# training-intensity

[Fitness Coach](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Coaching Goal

Select effort targets that fit the exercise, goal, experience and current readiness.

## Activate When

A program needs target effort or load for exercises and sessions.

## Training Boundary

Does not prescribe maximal effort by default or use pain as an intensity scale.

## Training Context

Goal/adaptation, exercise, experience and technique, recent performance, available load increments, readiness, volume/frequency and restrictions.

## Coaching Protocol

1. Choose the intensity representation the user can apply reliably: load/pace, repetition reserve, exertion, velocity or technical quality.
2. Set a target zone that matches exercise risk/skill and goal, with warm-up/ramp and clear termination cues.
3. Coordinate intensity with volume and frequency so high effort is not assigned everywhere; reserve maximal testing for justified, prepared contexts.
4. Calibrate from observed performance and session response, then adjust for readiness without turning every feeling into a program change.

## Effort Calibration

Use several anchors: completed repetitions or pace, remaining-capacity estimate, technique change and recovery. Subjective effort is useful but learned and noisy; it should be calibrated against repeatable performance.

## Training Decision Rules

- Higher effort is not automatically a better stimulus.
- Do not use sharp pain, dizziness or concerning symptoms as acceptable effort markers.

## Program Output

Intensity prescription with method, target zone, warm-up/ramp, technique/stop criteria, volume interaction, readiness adjustment and calibration task.

## Training Quality Gates

- The user can identify when the target has been reached without guesswork.
- Intensity fits the exercise, experience and recoverable weekly plan.

## Training Failure Modes

- **Every set near failure:** distribute effort intentionally.
- **Percent or pace used without current baseline:** calibrate.

## Handoffs

Volume completes dose; Program Design sequences intensity; Readiness adjusts today; clinician handles concerning responses.
