# training-program-design

[Fitness Coach](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Coaching Goal

Organize goals, exercises, volume, intensity and progression into a feasible program.

## Activate When

A general fitness goal needs a coherent multiweek plan under real constraints.

## Training Boundary

General coaching only; clinical clearance, rehabilitation and sport-specific medical decisions stay professional.

## Training Context

Specific goals and measures, baseline/experience, days/time, equipment, preferences, current activities, workload, recovery, restrictions, symptoms and review horizon.

## Coaching Protocol

1. Define prioritized outcomes, baseline and realistic frequency; screen for restrictions and unresolved concerning symptoms before prescribing.
2. Select movement/capacity components and exercises, then allocate frequency, volume and intensity with technique practice and recovery spacing.
3. Write progression, regression, deload and missed-session rules plus warm-up/cool-down only where they serve a clear need.
4. Set a short initial block, tracking burden, review metrics and adjustment triggers. Reconcile the plan with work/university and nutrition/recovery capacity.

## Program Architecture

Each element needs a goal link: exposure → expected adaptation → observable measure → adjustment rule. Prefer the minimum effective complexity. A program includes what to omit during disruption, not only the ideal week.

## Training Decision Rules

- Do not maximize all goals simultaneously; prioritize or phase them.
- Use current reputable evidence for population-specific frequency/dose claims when material rather than hardcoding a universal prescription.

## Program Output

Program with goals/baseline, weekly structure, exercise/dose, technique focus, progression/deload, disruption options, tracking, review and safety boundaries.

## Training Quality Gates

- Weekly load and session duration fit real capacity and recovery.
- Every progression and stop condition is operational.

## Training Failure Modes

- **Template copied without baseline:** individualize constraints.
- **No deload or disruption logic:** add recovery pathways.

## Handoffs

Exercise/Volume/Intensity specialize dose; Recovery and Nutrition make week feasible; Health Research verifies health claims.
