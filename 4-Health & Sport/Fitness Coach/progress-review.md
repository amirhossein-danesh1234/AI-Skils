# progress-review

[Fitness Coach](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Coaching Goal

Interpret training outcomes, adherence and tolerability before changing the plan.

## Activate When

Enough training observations exist to decide whether the current plan should continue or change.

## Training Boundary

A short-term fluctuation is not automatically progress, regression or a reason to overhaul training.

## Training Context

Goal and timeframe, completed sessions, adherence, performance/technique trend, volume/intensity exposure, subjective effort, recovery, schedule changes and symptoms.

## Coaching Protocol

1. Compare planned versus completed exposure and data quality; separate missed opportunities from a program that was actually tested.
2. Review multiple outcome signals appropriate to the goal, including performance, technique, tolerance and consistency; treat day-to-day noise cautiously.
3. Generate competing explanations for a plateau or decline: measurement noise, adherence, progression, volume/intensity, exercise fit, recovery or changed constraints.
4. Choose the smallest change with a review horizon, or keep the plan when evidence is insufficient. Escalate concerning symptom patterns.

## Change Attribution

Change one material variable when practical and record the expected signal. If several changes are unavoidable, reduce certainty about what caused the response. A stable useful trend may justify no change.

## Training Decision Rules

- Judge trends over a goal-appropriate window rather than a single session.
- Performance improvement does not automatically mean technique, recovery or risk is acceptable.

## Program Output

Progress review with data-quality note, outcome trends, adherence/tolerability, likely explanations, keep/change decision, next measure and escalation boundary.

## Training Quality Gates

- The recommendation follows observed exposure and response, not impatience.
- The next review can determine whether the adjustment helped.

## Training Failure Modes

- **Poor adherence labeled program failure:** distinguish them.
- **Everything changes after one bad session:** preserve signal.

## Handoffs

Progressive Overload or Recovery Adjustment implements the change; Nutrition/Recovery coaches address their owned constraints.
