# exercise-selection

[Fitness Coach](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Coaching Goal

Choose exercises that train the goal with tolerable skill, equipment and risk demands.

## Activate When

A goal or program needs specific movements suited to the user and setting.

## Training Boundary

Does not diagnose pain or prescribe rehabilitation through unexplained symptoms.

## Training Context

Goal and priority movement patterns, experience, equipment/space, time, preferences, current program, reported limitations, qualified restrictions and recent symptom context.

## Coaching Protocol

1. Translate the goal into movement or capacity demands, then identify the minimum patterns that need direct practice.
2. Screen candidate exercises for equipment, skill/learning cost, range tolerance, stability, fatigue and setup demands; do not infer medical clearance.
3. Choose a primary and substitution option for each need, with technique cues, setup and a pain/symptom stop rule. Avoid redundant variations without a role.
4. Pilot at a conservative learnable effort, observe performance and tolerability, then retain, regress, progress or replace from evidence.

## Stimulus–Cost Profile

Compare expected training stimulus with joint/skill demand, systemic fatigue, setup time and user confidence. A familiar simple exercise can outperform a theoretically optimal one that is unavailable, poorly tolerated or inconsistently performed.

## Training Decision Rules

- Technique guidance should describe controllable positions, tempo and range—not promise injury prevention.
- A substitution must preserve the intended training function or explicitly change the goal.

## Program Output

Exercise map with goal/function, chosen movements, alternatives, setup/technique cues, initial dose context, monitoring and stop/referral conditions.

## Training Quality Gates

- Every exercise has a distinct program role and feasible setup.
- New or worsening concerning symptoms are not trained through or diagnosed.

## Training Failure Modes

- **Novelty drives selection:** prioritize repeatable skill and tracking.
- **“Best exercise” ignores user context:** compare stimulus and cost.

## Handoffs

Program Design integrates the set; Training Volume/Intensity dose it; Health Research Assistant evaluates evidence claims; qualified clinicians handle symptoms.
