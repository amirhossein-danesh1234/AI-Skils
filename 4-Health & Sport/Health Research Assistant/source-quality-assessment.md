# source-quality-assessment

[Health Research Assistant](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Evidence Goal

Assess a health source’s provenance, design, directness, currency and claim support.

## Use This Protocol When

A health source may influence a claim or decision and needs rigorous appraisal.

## Clinical Boundary

Reputation alone does not validate a health claim.

## Required Evidence Context

Full source/record, exact target claim, source type/version/date, methods/data, funding/conflicts, corrections/retractions and comparison evidence.

## Evidence Workflow

1. Verify identity, publisher/organization, version, authorship, date and correction/retraction status through authoritative records.
2. Locate the exact supporting passage/result and reconstruct study design, population, exposure/intervention, comparator, outcomes and analysis.
3. Assess bias, directness, precision, consistency, applicability, conflicts and freshness; distinguish peer review from methodological quality.
4. Assign a bounded use—authoritative guidance, direct evidence, contextual evidence, hypothesis lead or exclude—with limitations.

## Health Evidence Fit

Quality is claim-specific. A strong randomized trial may be indirect for a different population; an observational design may be appropriate for harms but limited for causal benefit.

## Evidence Decision Rules

- Venue prestige and citation count are not validity tests.
- A source cannot support a stronger or broader claim than its design.

## Evidence Output

Appraisal with verified provenance, claim location, design/population, bias/directness/precision, currency, conflicts, use decision and limitations.

## Health Evidence Gates

- The bibliographic/source identity is real and current.
- The appraisal separates source quality from relevance to this question.

## Health Evidence Failure Modes

- **Abstract-only judgment:** inspect methods/results.
- **Mechanistic plausibility presented as clinical outcome evidence:** label distance.

## Handoffs

Medical Search locates sources; Evidence Synthesis integrates; Claim Verification compares wording.
