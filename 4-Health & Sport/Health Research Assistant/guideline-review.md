# guideline-review

[Health Research Assistant](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Evidence Goal

Compare current clinical or public-health guidelines with scope and population preserved.

## Use This Protocol When

One or more health guidelines need interpretation or comparison.

## Clinical Boundary

Guidelines inform evidence context; they do not establish personal suitability or override clinicians.

## Required Evidence Context

Exact guideline organizations, versions/dates/links, target population, jurisdiction/setting, recommendation question, evidence grades and user context without unnecessary identifiers.

## Evidence Workflow

1. Verify the current official version, update status, scope, intended users, jurisdiction and conflicts/disclosures.
2. Extract relevant recommendations with strength, evidence certainty, eligibility/exclusions and implementation conditions from the source—not a summary snippet.
3. Compare definitions, evidence cutoffs, values/resources and update dates that may explain differences; preserve disagreement.
4. State what applies generally, what is an evidence-based inference and what requires clinician judgment for the individual.

## Recommendation Context Table

Record recommendation, population, setting, strength/certainty, evidence basis, exceptions, version date and implementation notes. Guidelines may differ legitimately because scopes and value judgments differ.

## Evidence Decision Rules

- Do not average conflicting guidelines into a fabricated consensus.
- A guideline threshold is not an individual diagnosis.

## Evidence Output

Guideline brief with verified versions, comparison table, evidence/strength, differences, general implications, uncertainty and professional decision points.

## Health Evidence Gates

- Recommendations are accurately paraphrased and versioned.
- Population and exclusions match the claim being made.

## Health Evidence Failure Modes

- **Old guideline quoted as current:** check update/retirement.
- **Recommendation stripped of strength/exceptions:** restore context.

## Handoffs

Evidence Synthesis reviews underlying studies; Claim Verification checks public claims; clinicians apply to care.
