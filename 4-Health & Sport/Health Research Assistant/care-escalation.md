# care-escalation

[Health Research Assistant](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Evidence Goal

Recommend an appropriate level and timing of professional evaluation without diagnosing.

## Use This Protocol When

General coaching or evidence review reaches a point where professional evaluation is appropriate.

## Clinical Boundary

Does not choose treatment or guarantee the urgency category when essential context is unavailable.

## Required Evidence Context

Reason for escalation, onset/course/severity, functional impact, red flags, relevant restrictions, location/jurisdiction, available care and barriers.

## Evidence Workflow

1. Define what cannot be safely resolved within coaching/research and whether urgency has already been determined by a red-flag protocol.
2. Recommend a proportionate level—local emergency service, urgent/same-day care, timely primary/specialist care or routine discussion—based on current authoritative guidance.
3. Help prepare concise information: symptoms/timeline, exposures, medications/conditions if relevant, measurements, questions and what has changed.
4. Provide a safety net for worsening/new features and practical access alternatives without prescribing treatment or promising an outcome.

## Escalation Fit

Match service and timing to consequence, pace and access. Ordinary uncertainty may justify a routine clinician discussion; credible time-sensitive risk calls for urgent local help. The recommendation is not a diagnosis.

## Evidence Decision Rules

- Do not recommend waiting solely to collect better tracking when timely evaluation matters.
- Respect local service availability and emergency numbers; verify location-specific details.

## Evidence Output

Care recommendation with rationale, level/timing, preparation summary, access options, safety net and limits of the assessment.

## Health Evidence Gates

- The action is specific and feasible in the user’s location.
- No treatment change or clinical guarantee is implied.

## Health Evidence Failure Modes

- **Vague “see a doctor” gives no timing:** specify proportionately.
- **Remote confidence downgrades concern:** preserve uncertainty.

## Handoffs

Red Flag sets urgency; Uncertainty Communication explains limits; coaches pause affected advice.
