# health-claim-verification

[Health Research Assistant](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Evidence Goal

Check whether a health claim matches current evidence and its intended population.

## Use This Protocol When

A specific public, product, wellness or medical claim needs an evidence verdict.

## Clinical Boundary

Cannot verify an individual diagnosis, prognosis or treatment response from general literature.

## Required Evidence Context

Exact claim and source, population/context, implied outcome and timeframe, cited evidence, current search access and consequence if believed.

## Evidence Workflow

1. Rewrite the claim into testable components and identify whether it is causal, diagnostic, prognostic, safety or general-wellness language.
2. Trace cited sources to original evidence and run a current focused search for supporting, conflicting and authoritative guidance.
3. Compare wording with study design, population, magnitude, uncertainty and endpoints; identify extrapolation, surrogate outcomes or absolute-versus-relative framing.
4. Issue a calibrated verdict and corrected version, with source links/dates and a clear boundary before individual medical advice.

## Claim Verdict

Use supported, partly supported, uncertain/misleading, unsupported or contradicted, with reasons. A true narrow finding can still make a broad marketing claim misleading.

## Evidence Decision Rules

- Do not declare safety from absence of reported harm alone.
- Separate “may help” evidence from treatment recommendation and diagnosis.

## Evidence Output

Verification brief with decomposed claim, source trail, current evidence, mismatch analysis, verdict, corrected wording, uncertainty and care boundary.

## Health Evidence Gates

- The verdict matches both evidence quality and claim scope.
- No citation or current fact is supplied from memory when verification is required.

## Health Evidence Failure Modes

- **Headline endpoint differs from studied endpoint:** expose mismatch.
- **Relative effect lacks baseline:** add absolute context when available.

## Handoffs

Source Quality assesses studies; Guideline Review checks recommendations; coaches use only verified general guidance.
