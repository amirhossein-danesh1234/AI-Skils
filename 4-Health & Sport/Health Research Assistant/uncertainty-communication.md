# uncertainty-communication

[Health Research Assistant](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Evidence Goal

Communicate health evidence and unknowns without false reassurance or alarm.

## Use This Protocol When

Health evidence or a personal question needs a clear explanation of what is known, inferred and unresolved.

## Clinical Boundary

Does not hide action-relevant uncertainty behind vague disclaimers.

## Required Evidence Context

Question/claim, evidence and quality, population/context, effect estimates/ranges, conflicting sources, user decision and consequence of error.

## Evidence Workflow

1. Separate established facts, evidence-based inference, individual unknowns and unavailable evidence; state source dates when currency matters.
2. Explain magnitude and confidence using absolute frequencies/ranges where possible, avoiding unsupported precision and vague “more research” language.
3. Describe how population, measurement, bias or heterogeneity limits personal applicability and what evidence could change the conclusion.
4. Pair uncertainty with a proportionate action: ordinary choice, monitor, verify current guidance or seek professional evaluation.

## Useful Uncertainty

Uncertainty should change the decision or confidence, not merely decorate the answer. Distinguish unknown probability from known variability and from disagreement caused by different questions.

## Evidence Decision Rules

- Avoid both false reassurance and catastrophic possibility lists.
- Use plain language alongside technical certainty terms.

## Evidence Output

Evidence explanation with known/inferred/unknown labels, magnitude/confidence, applicability, decision implication, verification or care next step.

## Health Evidence Gates

- The reader can tell which statements are general evidence versus individual advice.
- Uncertainty is neither hidden nor used to avoid a practical conclusion.

## Health Evidence Failure Modes

- **“Evidence is mixed” without why:** explain design/population/outcome differences.
- **Precise percentage exceeds data quality:** use calibrated range.

## Handoffs

Evidence Synthesis supplies basis; Risk Assessment frames choices; Care Escalation addresses individual concern.
