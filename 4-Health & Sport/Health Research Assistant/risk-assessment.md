# risk-assessment

[Health Research Assistant](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Evidence Goal

Frame potential health risks by evidence, consequence and uncertainty.

## Use This Protocol When

A health-related choice or exposure needs a non-diagnostic risk framing.

## Clinical Boundary

Not a clinical risk score, triage diagnosis or substitute for individualized professional assessment.

## Required Evidence Context

Decision/exposure, population and duration, possible benefits/harms, baseline context, evidence quality, modifiers, alternatives, reversibility and urgency.

## Evidence Workflow

1. Clarify the risk question and decision horizon; screen for personal symptoms/red flags that require care rather than literature-only analysis.
2. Identify plausible outcomes and evidence for probability/magnitude, separating known evidence, inference, unknowns and individual modifiers requiring clinician input.
3. Compare alternatives including no change, risk-reduction controls and reversibility; use absolute denominators/timeframes where evidence permits.
4. Provide a bounded risk tier or decision matrix, not a clinical score, with uncertainty and escalation/verification requirements.

## Consequence–Evidence Matrix

The response threshold depends on both potential consequence and evidence certainty. Low-probability severe harms may justify conservative controls, while uncertain trivial harms need not make ordinary wellness unusable.

## Evidence Decision Rules

- Do not multiply speculative risks into a precise number.
- Personal medical history affects individualized risk and may require a clinician rather than inference.

## Evidence Output

Risk brief with outcomes, evidence/confidence, absolute context when available, modifiers, alternatives/controls, uncertainty and care threshold.

## Health Evidence Gates

- Risk language is calibrated and avoids reassurance or alarm unsupported by evidence.
- The analysis distinguishes general population evidence from personal medical advice.

## Health Evidence Failure Modes

- **Relative risk presented without baseline:** add context.
- **Worst case dominates ordinary decision:** weigh likelihood and control.

## Handoffs

Red Flag Identification handles symptom urgency; Evidence Synthesis supplies evidence; clinician performs individual assessment.
