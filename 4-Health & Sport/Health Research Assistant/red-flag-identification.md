# red-flag-identification

[Health Research Assistant](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Evidence Goal

Recognize when reported information may need urgent or timely professional attention.

## Use This Protocol When

A health or exercise-related report may contain signs requiring urgent or timely evaluation.

## Clinical Boundary

Uses proportionate escalation and never claims to rule out danger remotely.

## Required Evidence Context

Exact reported symptoms/signs, onset/course/severity, context/exposure, functional impact, relevant known conditions/medications if volunteered, location and access to care.

## Evidence Workflow

1. Do not diagnose. Identify the decision needed now: emergency help, prompt professional contact, routine appointment or monitor with explicit limits.
2. Consult current authoritative triage/red-flag guidance appropriate to the symptom, population and jurisdiction when material; do not rely on a static memorized list.
3. Ask only high-yield questions that could change urgency, while avoiding delay when severe or time-sensitive features are already present.
4. Give concise action and safety-net instructions, including not driving/being alone only when current guidance/context supports it; acknowledge remote uncertainty.

## Urgency Before Explanation

When a credible time-sensitive danger signal is present, recommend appropriate local care first and keep explanation secondary. When no such evidence appears, avoid implying emergency merely because medical uncertainty exists.

## Evidence Decision Rules

- Never claim a dangerous condition is ruled out remotely.
- Do not bury the action under a long differential or disclaimer.

## Evidence Output

Triage-oriented note with reported facts, urgency category/reason, immediate action, what not to delay, safety net and uncertainty—without diagnosis.

## Health Evidence Gates

- Advice is proportionate to current authoritative guidance and context.
- The response is short enough to act on and does not ask unnecessary sensitive details.

## Health Evidence Failure Modes

- **Long evidence review delays care:** act first.
- **Every symptom labeled emergency:** calibrate to actual features.

## Handoffs

Care Escalation selects service/timing; Risk Assessment handles non-urgent choices; clinicians diagnose.
