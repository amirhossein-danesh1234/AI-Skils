# medical-evidence-search

[Health Research Assistant](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Evidence Goal

Search current health literature and authoritative sources for a focused evidence question.

## Use This Protocol When

A focused health question requires current sources rather than memory or generic advice.

## Clinical Boundary

Provides research support, not individualized diagnosis or treatment selection.

## Required Evidence Context

Structured question and population/context, claim/decision type, date and language range, source access, preferred authorities, inclusion criteria and urgency/safety context.

## Evidence Workflow

1. Translate the question into population, exposure/intervention, comparator, outcome and decision type where applicable; set scope and currency needs.
2. Search current authoritative guidelines/public-health sources and primary literature in appropriate databases, recording queries, filters, dates and versions.
3. Screen relevance and source type, trace pivotal citations and check updates, corrections or retractions; do not rely on snippets or fabricate inaccessible details.
4. Return an evidence map and search limits, separating general guidance from evidence-based inference and any need for individual professional evaluation.

## Evidence Search Ladder

Start with current authoritative guidance for practice questions, then primary systematic evidence and pivotal studies as needed. Use secondary summaries to orient, not to obscure source distance.

## Evidence Decision Rules

- Current means verified for the decision date, not assumed from model memory.
- Do not search longer when urgent red flags require care escalation first.

## Evidence Output

Search record with question, sources/databases, exact strategy/dates, included evidence map, gaps, currency and next evaluation step.

## Health Evidence Gates

- Every cited source resolves and supports the described claim.
- The search scope matches the population and consequence of the question.

## Health Evidence Failure Modes

- **Popular article defines evidence:** trace its sources.
- **Urgent symptom query becomes literature project:** escalate first.

## Handoffs

Source Quality appraises; Guideline Review compares recommendations; Care Escalation handles personal urgency.
