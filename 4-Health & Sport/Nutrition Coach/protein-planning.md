# protein-planning

[Nutrition Coach](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Nutrition Goal

Estimate a practical protein range and distribute it across preferred foods.

## Use This For

A general nutrition or training goal needs a practical protein intake and distribution plan.

## Nutrition Boundary

Does not prescribe clinical protein restriction or supplementation as treatment.

## Food and Context Inputs

Goal, body context if needed, training/activity, current food pattern, preferences/culture, budget, meal frequency, health restrictions and clinician advice.

## Nutrition Workflow

1. Determine whether a numeric target is useful; estimate a range from current reputable guidance matched to population and goal, showing assumptions.
2. Compare with the current pattern using approximate food portions rather than claiming laboratory precision.
3. Distribute feasible protein opportunities across existing meals and snacks, prioritizing preferred food sources and substitutions.
4. Review total nourishment, tolerance, cost and adherence; avoid escalating when clinical kidney/liver or other restrictions are unresolved.

## Range to Food

Translate the evidence range into meal-level options and flexible portions. Distribution can improve practicality and training support, but exact per-meal numbers should not become a rigid rule without need.

## Nutrition Decision Rules

- Do not let protein crowd out energy, fiber-rich foods, variety or enjoyment.
- Supplement use is optional convenience, not proof of a superior plan.

## Food Plan Output

Protein plan with evidence source/date if material, target range/assumptions, current estimate, meal options, substitutions, review and clinical boundary.

## Nutrition Quality Gates

- The range matches the stated population and goal.
- Food examples sum plausibly without spurious gram-level certainty.

## Nutrition Failure Modes

- **Highest advertised target chosen:** use goal and context.
- **All intake deferred to one meal without considering feasibility:** distribute.

## Handoffs

Meal and Calorie integrate; Fitness supplies training demand; dietitian/clinician handles clinical protein needs.
