# pre-workout-nutrition

[Nutrition Coach](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Nutrition Goal

Plan tolerable pre-training food and fluids around timing and session demand.

## Use This For

The user needs food and fluid that support a planned session without avoidable discomfort.

## Nutrition Boundary

Does not prescribe around unexplained gastrointestinal or metabolic symptoms.

## Food and Context Inputs

Session start/duration/intensity, prior meal timing, tolerance history, schedule, climate, preferences, total-day intake and relevant health restrictions.

## Nutrition Workflow

1. Classify session demand and time since the last meal; determine whether additional intake is useful or optional.
2. Choose a familiar, digestible amount/composition and fluid approach matched to lead time and individual tolerance, with current evidence for specialized claims.
3. Provide nearer/farther timing and portable alternatives; trial new strategies in training rather than an important event.
4. Review energy, comfort, thirst and session performance, then adjust one variable while routing recurring or concerning symptoms.

## Tolerance–Timing Matrix

As start time approaches, simplify portion and digestion burden according to the person’s experience. No single food or fasting pattern is universally optimal; the whole-day plan remains primary.

## Nutrition Decision Rules

- Do not prescribe around medication, diabetes or unexplained symptoms without qualified guidance.
- Avoid forced hydration and unfamiliar high-dose products.

## Food Plan Output

Pre-session plan with need assessment, timing options, familiar foods/fluids, portable fallback, trial and review conditions.

## Nutrition Quality Gates

- The plan fits the actual session and schedule.
- Recommendations avoid false precision and preserve individual tolerance evidence.

## Nutrition Failure Modes

- **Generic meal ignores start time:** match lead time.
- **Event day becomes first experiment:** practice earlier.

## Handoffs

Meal Planning handles routine; Post-workout handles recovery; Health Research verifies performance-health claims.
