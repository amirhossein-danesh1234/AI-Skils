# body-composition-planning

[Nutrition Coach](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Nutrition Goal

Plan sustainable body-composition change with uncertainty, nourishment and adherence protected.

## Use This For

The user wants a sustainable change in body composition or weight-related performance.

## Nutrition Boundary

Does not diagnose weight change, treat eating disorders or guarantee a body outcome.

## Food and Context Inputs

User-defined goal and reason, current trend with measurement conditions, food/activity pattern, preferences/budget, training, timeline, health context, relevant clinician guidance and body-image/eating concerns.

## Nutrition Workflow

1. Clarify the outcome and whether scale, circumference, performance, fit, wellbeing or another measure actually represents it; avoid moral framing.
2. Screen for red flags, unexplained change, pregnancy/complex disease or disordered-eating concerns and route qualified care before restriction.
3. Choose a conservative nourishment-preserving starting approach using energy, protein, meals, activity and adherence levers appropriate to the context.
4. Track trends under consistent conditions and adjust slowly from multiple observations, with maintenance breaks or goal revision when burden rises.

## Trend and Uncertainty

Body measurements contain biological and measurement noise; composition estimates also have method error. Use ranges and trends, never promise a date or exact tissue change from an equation.

## Nutrition Decision Rules

- Preserve adequate food variety, recovery and social sustainability.
- Stop escalating restriction when adherence, wellbeing, performance or symptoms deteriorate.

## Food Plan Output

Plan with goal/measure, starting assumptions, food/activity strategy, monitoring method, adjustment range, adherence protections and referral triggers.

## Nutrition Quality Gates

- The target and pace are not presented as guaranteed or universally healthy.
- The plan has a maintenance or stop option and avoids extreme restriction.

## Nutrition Failure Modes

- **Single weigh-in drives changes:** use standardized trend.
- **Aesthetic goal overrides concerning signals:** escalate appropriately.

## Handoffs

Calorie/Protein/Meal skills implement; Fitness supports training; Health Research verifies claims; dietitian/clinician handles clinical needs.
