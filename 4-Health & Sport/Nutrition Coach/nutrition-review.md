# nutrition-review

[Nutrition Coach](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Nutrition Goal

Review actual eating patterns and select a small evidence-based adjustment.

## Use This For

The user wants to improve an actual food pattern from observed information.

## Nutrition Boundary

Does not infer deficiency or disease from a food log.

## Food and Context Inputs

Representative intake record with context, goal, hunger/energy/training, preferences, access/budget, hydration, symptoms, supplements and relevant guidance.

## Nutrition Workflow

1. Check record completeness and representativeness without treating self-report as exact; note weekdays, weekends and unusual days.
2. Assess pattern-level adequacy, distribution, variety, hydration, training support and practical barriers using current evidence where a health claim matters.
3. Prioritize one or two high-impact feasible changes, preserving what already works and offering substitutions.
4. Define observation and review timing; route persistent symptoms, suspected deficiency, disordered eating or clinical complexity.

## Pattern Before Nutrient Microscopy

Start with regularity, adequacy, food-group variety and goal fit. Detailed nutrient analysis is useful only when data quality and decision need justify it; it cannot diagnose deficiency.

## Nutrition Decision Rules

- Separate reported intake, database estimate and evidence-based inference.
- Do not compensate for one atypical meal with restriction or punishment.

## Food Plan Output

Review with data caveats, strengths, prioritized gaps, bounded adjustments, substitutions, monitoring and referral conditions.

## Nutrition Quality Gates

- Recommendations follow the actual pattern and user constraints.
- No diagnosis or false nutrient precision is implied.

## Nutrition Failure Modes

- **Food log judged morally:** use neutral observations.
- **Ten changes overwhelm adherence:** prioritize.

## Handoffs

Meal Planning implements; Health Research checks claims; dietitian/clinician handles suspected clinical issues.
