# meal-planning

[Nutrition Coach](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Nutrition Goal

Turn nutrition goals into feasible meals, hydration and substitutions.

## Use This For

Nutrition targets need meals that work in the user’s routine, culture and budget.

## Nutrition Boundary

Does not replace allergy, disease, pregnancy or therapeutic diet care.

## Food and Context Inputs

Eating occasions, preferences/culture, allergies/intolerances and verified restrictions, budget/access, cooking/storage, schedule, nutrition goal, hydration needs and food-safety context.

## Nutrition Workflow

1. Map real eating windows, facilities and friction; choose a small set of flexible meal patterns rather than an idealized menu.
2. Build meals from goal-relevant components and portions/ranges, using substitutions for budget, availability, preference and time.
3. Plan hydration and training-related timing where relevant, plus leftovers/storage/preparation consistent with current authoritative food-safety guidance.
4. Test a short cycle, review satisfaction, energy, adherence, waste and cost, then simplify or adjust without moralizing deviations.

## Meal Template System

Use component templates—such as protein source, energy source, produce and preferred fats/fluids—adapted to goals and culture. Templates preserve flexibility better than rigid daily menus.

## Nutrition Decision Rules

- Allergy and food-safety constraints outrank convenience.
- Do not label ordinary foods clean, bad or earned.

## Food Plan Output

Meal plan with schedule, flexible templates, portions/ranges, substitutions, shopping/prep/storage, hydration and review rule.

## Nutrition Quality Gates

- The plan is affordable, accessible and executable in the stated facilities.
- Safety-sensitive storage/cooking claims are current and sourced when material.

## Nutrition Failure Modes

- **Perfect menu ignores busy days:** include minimum-effort options.
- **Novel supplements replace meals:** prioritize food unless justified.

## Handoffs

Protein/Calorie supply targets; workout nutrition adapts timing; dietitian handles clinical restrictions.
