# calorie-planning

[Nutrition Coach](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Nutrition Goal

Estimate an energy-intake starting range and revise it from observed trends.

## Use This For

A practical energy-intake starting point and feedback rule are needed.

## Nutrition Boundary

Energy equations are estimates, not individual metabolic measurements or permission for extreme restriction.

## Food and Context Inputs

Goal, body/weight trend if volunteered, age/context needed for chosen equation, activity/training, intake pattern, schedule, food access, health context and evidence quality.

## Nutrition Workflow

1. Decide whether calorie counting is useful and acceptable; a meal-pattern approach may be lower burden and equally actionable.
2. Estimate expenditure/intake as a range using a transparent current method and assumptions; do not treat wearable or formula output as measured truth.
3. Translate the range into a feasible intake or meal-pattern starting point that protects nourishment and training/recovery.
4. Review consistent trends, hunger, energy, performance and adherence after an appropriate interval; change modestly or stop/refer when concerning.

## Estimate–Observe Loop

The starting estimate is a prior. Real-world intake reporting and energy expenditure are uncertain, so update from multiple outcomes rather than “correcting” the user or chasing daily fluctuations.

## Nutrition Decision Rules

- Do not create large deficits/surpluses from a short-term trend.
- Use current reputable guidance for population-specific minimums or clinical contexts; do not hardcode them as universal.

## Food Plan Output

Energy plan with method/assumptions, starting range or meal proxy, uncertainty, monitoring, adjustment rule and non-counting alternative.

## Nutrition Quality Gates

- Numbers carry no more precision than inputs and method.
- The approach remains feasible and does not override clinical or eating-disorder concerns.

## Nutrition Failure Modes

- **Calculator output treated as prescription:** frame it as start.
- **Exercise calories automatically eaten/deducted:** avoid double counting.

## Handoffs

Meal Planning operationalizes; Body Composition owns goal; dietitian/clinician handles therapeutic needs.
