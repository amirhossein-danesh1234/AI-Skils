# post-workout-nutrition

[Nutrition Coach](README.md) / [Health & Sport domain](../../README.md)

## Nutrition Goal

Plan practical post-training food and fluids within the whole-day pattern.

## Use This For

A session or competition needs practical recovery food and fluids.

## Nutrition Boundary

Timing details should not displace adequate total intake or clinical guidance.

## Food and Context Inputs

Training type/duration/intensity, next session timing, total-day intake, meal schedule, appetite/tolerance, environment/sweat context, preferences and health restrictions.

## Nutrition Workflow

1. Determine whether rapid recovery is actually needed based on session demand and time until the next meaningful exposure.
2. Fit protein, carbohydrate, fluid and electrolytes into the next normal meal/snack using current population-appropriate evidence rather than a universal window.
3. Offer tolerable food-first options and a lower-appetite/travel alternative, considering food safety and total daily intake.
4. Review gastrointestinal tolerance, thirst/urine or other non-diagnostic hydration cues, performance and overall nourishment; refer concerning responses.

## Urgency Ladder

Recovery timing matters most when sessions are demanding and close together. When the next session is distant, a normal balanced meal may be sufficient; exact timing should not create anxiety.

## Nutrition Decision Rules

- Replace estimated sweat/fluid losses cautiously; avoid forced overdrinking.
- Supplements require a clear convenience or evidence role and safety/quality consideration.

## Food Plan Output

Post-session plan with urgency, food/fluid options, timing range, next-session context, tolerability and review/stop cues.

## Nutrition Quality Gates

- The plan supports whole-day targets and does not double-count recovery intake.
- Current evidence is checked when prescribing population-specific timing or electrolyte claims.

## Nutrition Failure Modes

- **Rigid “window” overrides appetite and day context:** scale urgency.
- **Only protein considered after long endurance work:** match session demands.

## Handoffs

Meal/Protein/Calorie integrate day; Health Research verifies claims; clinician handles abnormal symptoms.
