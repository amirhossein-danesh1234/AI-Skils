# schedule-review

[Personal Planner](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Use planned-versus-actual evidence to re-estimate and adjust commitments without blame.

## Activate When

A schedule repeatedly slips, feels exhausting, or needs evidence-based adjustment after execution.

## Do Not Use When

Not performance surveillance or proof of personal worth.

## Required Context

**Needed**

Planned and actual timing, completion/state, interruptions, energy, transitions and contextual changes for a defined period.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Perfect time tracking is unnecessary; representative observations and confidence levels suffice.

## Workflow

1. Reconstruct planned versus actual at a useful level, protecting privacy and distinguishing estimates from observed timestamps.
2. Classify variance: scope/estimate, dependency, transition, interruption, energy, avoidance, priority change or external event. Avoid character judgments.
3. Find the smallest recurring cause with material impact and check whether the schedule itself was infeasible.
4. Change one or two planning rules, estimates, buffers or scopes; preserve what worked and define the next review evidence.

## Variance Without Blame

A review improves the model, not the user’s moral worth. Completion alone is insufficient: inspect recovery cost, lateness, displaced obligations and whether the work still mattered.

## Decision Rules

- One exceptional disruption does not establish a recurring rule.
- Repeated overruns update future estimates or scope; they are not hidden by denser blocking.

## Output Contract

Review with observed variance, likely causes/confidence, retained practices, bounded changes and next measurement.

## Quality Gates

- Causes are supported by observations or labeled hypotheses.
- Actions modify the planning system rather than merely demanding more effort.

## Failure Modes

- **Tracking burden exceeds insight:** sample fewer fields.
- **Review becomes self-criticism:** return to model and constraints.

## Handoffs

System Audit examines tool/process burden; Capacity Planning resets load; qualified support handles persistent health or safety concerns.
