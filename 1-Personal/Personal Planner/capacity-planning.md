# capacity-planning

[Personal Planner](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Estimate real personal capacity across clock time, focus, low-energy work and recovery.

## Activate When

A period has more possible work than dependable capacity, or a commitment needs a feasibility check.

## Do Not Use When

Does not create availability by sacrificing essential sleep, meals or travel.

## Required Context

**Needed**

Planning horizon, fixed commitments, sleep/meals/care, commute/transitions, task effort ranges, energy patterns and uncertainty.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Exact placement can wait for weekly or daily planning; unknown effort stays a range with buffer.

## Workflow

1. Build the gross clock-time inventory, then reserve sleep, meals, care, fixed events, travel, setup, shutdown and ordinary life maintenance.
2. Estimate usable focused time separately from low-energy time and recovery. Apply uncertainty and interruption buffers before accepting discretionary work.
3. Compare demand by type—not only total hours—to the matching capacity. Identify the binding constraint and the work that must shrink, move, delegate or stop.
4. Publish a base capacity and a reduced-capacity case, then set an intake rule and trigger for replanning.

## Four Capacity Ledgers

Clock time is elapsed availability; focused time supports demanding work; low-energy time supports routine work; recovery restores capacity. They are not interchangeable currencies. A free calendar block is not automatically focus capacity, and recovery is not overflow storage.

## Decision Rules

- Do not plan more than the lower credible capacity merely because an optimistic week is possible.
- New commitments displace named work or buffer; they never enter an unbounded list.

## Output Contract

Capacity brief with four ledgers, assumptions, demand comparison, overload amount, protected buffer and accept/defer/drop choices.

## Quality Gates

- Fixed needs, transitions and recovery are visible rather than hidden in task estimates.
- Demand and capacity use compatible units and uncertainty is explicit.

## Failure Modes

- **Summing every unscheduled hour as productive capacity:** reserve real-life overhead.
- **Treating low-energy time as a substitute for deep focus:** match work to energy.

## Handoffs

Priority Planning decides what survives; Weekly Planning places it; Strategist resolves value conflicts; health specialists address persistent impairment.
