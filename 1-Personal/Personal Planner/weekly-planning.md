# weekly-planning

[Personal Planner](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Coordinate fixed commitments, chosen outcomes, maintenance and slack across a week.

## Activate When

The next week needs coordination across obligations, goals, maintenance and recovery.

## Do Not Use When

Does not promise a feasible week when obligations exceed capacity.

## Required Context

**Needed**

Week calendar, deadlines, current commitments, capacity/energy, carryover, household/care needs and relevant uncertainties.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Longer-range goals can stay at outcome level; only near-term executable work enters the week.

## Workflow

1. Review the prior week for carryover and model corrections without automatically re-accepting unfinished work.
2. Map fixed events, commute/transitions, sleep, meals, care, maintenance and recovery. Calculate credible capacity by type.
3. Choose weekly outcomes and minimum versions, then place the most constrained work. Balance focused work, low-energy maintenance and protected buffer.
4. Publish not-now items, disruption triage and a midweek checkpoint that can reduce scope before recovery is consumed.

## Weekly Load Test

The week is feasible only if tasks, life maintenance and uncertainty fit together. Carryover competes again; it does not inherit priority. Reserve capacity for the week to be lived, not just worked.

## Decision Rules

- When credible demand exceeds capacity, remove or renegotiate before blocking.
- Keep at least one recovery/flexible window appropriate to volatility.

## Output Contract

Weekly plan with anchors, capacity, outcomes/minimums, blocks, maintenance, recovery, buffer, not-now list and midweek reset.

## Quality Gates

- Committed work fits focused and low-energy capacity separately.
- Known high-risk days have reduced load or explicit contingency.

## Failure Modes

- **Planning only work/study:** include the infrastructure of living.
- **Optimistic Monday plan pushes failure downstream:** distribute by constraint and energy.

## Handoffs

Strategist resolves competing commitments; Daily Planning adapts execution; Systems Designer supports capture/review mechanics.
