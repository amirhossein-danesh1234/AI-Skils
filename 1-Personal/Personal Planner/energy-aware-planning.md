# energy-aware-planning

[Personal Planner](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Match task demands to observed energy patterns and protect recovery.

## Activate When

Work demands and likely energy vary enough that timing or task type affects feasibility.

## Do Not Use When

Not medical assessment or a moral judgment about low-energy periods.

## Required Context

**Needed**

User-observed energy patterns, sleep/recovery context, fixed constraints, task cognitive/emotional/physical demands and deadlines.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Clinical explanations are not required; patterns may remain provisional and user-reported.

## Workflow

1. Classify tasks by demand and identify user-observed high, moderate and low-energy windows without diagnosing causes.
2. Protect recovery and place the highest-value demanding work in the best credible windows. Pair routine work with lower-energy periods.
3. Create lower-energy versions of important work and switching rules based on observable state, not shame or heroic assumptions.
4. Review whether the pattern holds and adjust task design, environment or load before trying to optimize every hour.

## Energy Matching

Energy is variable context, not a moral score. Do not infer medical or psychological causes. Persistent, severe or concerning fatigue belongs with qualified care; ordinary fluctuation calls for proportionate planning.

## Decision Rules

- Use recent personal evidence over generic chronotype claims.
- When a deadline conflicts with energy, reduce scope or add recovery/buffer before extending work indefinitely.

## Output Contract

Energy map with task-demand classes, placement guidance, fallback versions, protected recovery and review observations.

## Quality Gates

- The plan does not convert every good-energy period into work.
- Fallbacks reduce demand or scope rather than pretending capacity is unchanged.

## Failure Modes

- **Diagnosing the user from a schedule pattern:** describe observations only.
- **Optimizing around chronic overload:** escalate load reduction and appropriate care.

## Handoffs

Capacity Planning sets total load; Daily/Weekly Planning schedule it; health support handles symptoms or medical risk.
