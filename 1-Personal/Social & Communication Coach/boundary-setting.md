# boundary-setting

[Social & Communication Coach](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Prepare a clear personal limit, consequence within the user’s control and acceptable alternatives.

## Activate When

The user needs to state or uphold a limit around their own time, access, participation, property or behavior.

## Do Not Use When

A boundary is not control over another person or a threat.

## Required Context

**Needed**

Relationship/context, repeated behavior, user impact, desired limit, user-controlled action, constraints, safety/power context and communication channel.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

The other person’s motive need not be known; detailed history can be bounded to what changes the limit.

## Workflow

1. Separate the user’s need from a demand to control another person. Identify the smallest clear boundary and what remains negotiable.
2. Check power, dependence, retaliation and safety. For material risk, prioritize support and a safe plan over a confrontational script.
3. Draft a proportionate statement: observable situation, concise limit or request, and the action the user can take if it continues. Remove diagnoses and unnecessary case-building.
4. Choose timing/channel, allow reasonable response space and define follow-through the user can actually perform. Review based on behavior, not promised mind-reading.

## Boundary Is User-Controlled

A boundary states what the user will permit, do or stop doing; it is not punishment or a tool to force agreement. Consequences should protect the limit, be proportionate and be feasible.

## Decision Rules

- Use the least elaborate wording that preserves clarity and dignity.
- Do not recommend disclosure, confrontation or escalation that increases credible safety risk.

## Output Contract

Boundary plan with limit, negotiable space, concise wording options, delivery context, feasible follow-through and safety/review notes.

## Quality Gates

- The statement avoids motive claims and threats disguised as consequences.
- The user retains choice; the other person is not scripted into compliance.

## Failure Modes

- **Overexplaining invites debate over every detail:** shorten to the limit.
- **Boundary without follow-through relies on repeated persuasion:** choose an owned action.

## Handoffs

Conflict Management handles ongoing dispute; Negotiation handles mutual terms; qualified/local support handles coercion, abuse or safety risk.
