# negotiation

[Social & Communication Coach](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Prepare a personal agreement around interests, alternatives, limits and relationship effects.

## Activate When

Two or more people need a voluntary agreement about personal plans, responsibilities, resources or expectations.

## Do Not Use When

No deception, coercion or unauthorized commitments.

## Required Context

**Needed**

Parties, decision scope, interests, constraints, alternatives, authority, relationship value, timing, prior terms and safety/power context.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Precise concessions can wait until interests and alternatives are understood.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the decision is negotiable and identify each party’s authority. Distinguish interests from positions and list known versus inferred interests.
2. Define the user’s best alternative, acceptable range and non-negotiables without inventing threats. Consider relationship and implementation costs.
3. Generate packages that trade differences in timing, scope, effort or certainty. Check fairness, feasibility and hidden burdens.
4. Plan questions, proposal sequence and pause authority. Capture a specific agreement, ownership, dates, contingencies and review/exit terms.

## Voluntary Agreement

Negotiation is not manipulation. Do not exploit sensitive information, deceive about alternatives or manufacture deadlines. The other person may decline; the user may also walk away where feasible.

## Decision Rules

- Do not bargain over safety, consent or basic dignity.
- A concession requires an explicit return or intentional gift, not resentful ambiguity.

## Output Contract

Negotiation brief with scope, interests/unknowns, alternatives, range, option packages, conversation plan and agreement checklist.

## Quality Gates

- Proposals are executable and within the parties’ authority.
- Power/safety and relationship consequences are addressed, not hidden by optimization.

## Failure Modes

- **Assumed motives drive trades:** ask or label hypothesis.
- **Agreement stays vague to preserve harmony:** specify behavior and review.

## Handoffs

Boundary Setting handles unilateral limits; Conflict Management repairs process; qualified mediators/legal/HR support formal disputes.
