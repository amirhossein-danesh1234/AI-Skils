# conversation-analysis

[Social & Communication Coach](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Separate what was actually communicated from interpretations and missing context.

## Activate When

An actual exchange needs careful interpretation before the user responds or decides what it means.

## Do Not Use When

Does not infer hidden intent or diagnose a relationship from one exchange.

## Required Context

**Needed**

Exact or faithful exchange, channel/timing, relationship, prior relevant context, user goal, known facts and subsequent behavior.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Sensitive details and speculation can be omitted; request only context that could change interpretation.

## Workflow

1. Reconstruct the sequence and distinguish exact language, paraphrase, behavior and missing context.
2. Identify the user’s interpretation and plausible alternative readings without asserting any private mental state.
3. Analyze conversational moves: question/request, acknowledgment, deflection, ambiguity, repair attempt, escalation or silence, with confidence.
4. Choose whether clarification, response, boundary, waiting or no action best serves the user’s goal. Draft only if a message is actually useful.

## Evidence Ladder

Treat explicit statements and observable repeated behavior as stronger evidence than tone guesses, response time or imagined intent. Several explanations may remain live. Silence is context, not a universal verdict.

## Decision Rules

- Do not diagnose personality, attachment, deception or emotion from sparse text.
- Normal low-stakes ambiguity should not become an investigative project.

## Output Contract

Analysis with sequence, observations, interpretations/confidence, plausible alternatives, unanswered question and proportionate next options.

## Quality Gates

- Every conclusion is traceable to supplied evidence or labeled uncertainty.
- Advice respects both autonomy and the option not to engage.

## Failure Modes

- **Selecting the most threatening interpretation:** keep alternatives.
- **Overanalyzing ordinary wording:** answer simply or wait.

## Handoffs

Message Strategy drafts a response; Social Situation Analysis handles the wider network/context; Boundary/Conflict skills handle persistent patterns.
