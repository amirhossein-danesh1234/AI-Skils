# conflict-management

[Social & Communication Coach](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Prepare a constructive response to a concrete disagreement while protecting autonomy and safety.

## Activate When

A disagreement or recurring interaction is harming coordination or the relationship and needs a managed process.

## Do Not Use When

Not forced compromise, motive diagnosis or treatment of abuse as ordinary conflict.

## Required Context

**Needed**

Parties/relationship, observable events, each stated concern, impacts, prior attempts, stakes, power/safety, constraints and desired future state.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Unverified motives and a complete history can be deferred; distinguish facts, accounts and hypotheses.

## Workflow

1. Assess whether this is a solvable disagreement, a boundary violation, a safety/power problem or a decision to disengage.
2. Map observable facts, each party’s reported perspective, interests, impacts and the escalation loop without assigning hidden intentions.
3. Choose an aim: understand, repair, decide terms, set a limit or exit. Plan timing, opening, listening, a narrow issue and pause rules.
4. Develop options and a specific next agreement or boundary. Record follow-up and escalation/exit conditions if behavior does not improve.

## Conflict Loop

Address the interaction pattern and concrete issue rather than prosecuting character. Mutual understanding does not require agreement, forgiveness or continued access. Repair is voluntary and evidence comes from changed behavior.

## Decision Rules

- Use symmetrical curiosity only where power and safety permit; neutrality must not erase harm.
- When escalation is high, pause before composing more messages or expanding the audience.

## Output Contract

Conflict plan with issue map, goal, conversation structure, options, agreement/boundary, pause rule and follow-up evidence.

## Quality Gates

- Facts, reports and interpretations are labeled separately.
- The plan is safe, proportionate and does not require the user to win a motive argument.

## Failure Modes

- **False balance minimizes coercion or threat:** shift to safety/support.
- **Relitigating every incident prevents a workable next step:** scope the issue.

## Handoffs

Boundary Setting owns unilateral limits; Negotiation structures mutual agreement; legal/HR/clinical/local support handles formal or dangerous contexts.
