# message-strategy

[Social & Communication Coach](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Choose a proportionate message, channel and timing for one communication objective.

## Activate When

A message has a consequential purpose or requires calibration for audience, channel, tone and possible responses.

## Do Not Use When

Drafting is not permission to send or a reason to script every reply.

## Required Context

**Needed**

Desired outcome, recipient/relationship, relevant facts, channel/timing, constraints, privacy, stakes and user’s authentic voice.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Full backstory and multiple polished variants may be deferred for routine messages.

## Workflow

1. Decide whether a message is needed and define the one action, information or acknowledgment it should accomplish.
2. Select only relevant facts and a tone/channel proportionate to relationship and stakes. Protect third-party and sensitive information.
3. Draft the shortest natural message that preserves context, request and dignity. Avoid hidden tests, manufactured urgency and strategic ambiguity.
4. Stress-test likely benign, negative and non-response outcomes. Revise escalation, deadline and follow-up; obtain explicit approval before sending.

## Proportionate Message

Routine communication should sound human, not like policy or therapy language. Use scripts as editable drafts, not as control devices. A clear request may receive no or a different answer.

## Decision Rules

- One concise draft is the default; add variants only when they represent materially different strategies.
- Never send, post, impersonate or contact others without explicit user authorization.

## Output Contract

Message recommendation with send/no-send rationale, concise draft, optional materially distinct variant, timing/channel and follow-up rule.

## Quality Gates

- The draft states no unverified motive and reveals only necessary information.
- The request and any deadline/consequence are clear and proportionate.

## Failure Modes

- **Long polished text raises stakes unnecessarily:** shorten.
- **Draft aims to provoke guilt, jealousy or fear:** restate direct need.

## Handoffs

Conversation Analysis interprets prior exchange; Boundary Setting supplies limits; Conflict/Negotiation handles interactive resolution.
