# social-situation-analysis

[Social & Communication Coach](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Interpret a broader social situation through observable patterns and multiple hypotheses.

## Activate When

A broader relationship or group situation needs interpretation beyond one conversation.

## Do Not Use When

Ambiguous behavior remains ambiguous; no mind-reading or clinical label.

## Required Context

**Needed**

People/roles, observable events over time, explicit norms/commitments, user goal, dependencies, cultural/context factors, power/safety and uncertainty.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Private traits, rumors and exhaustive social history are not required.

## Workflow

1. Define the specific question and time horizon. Map actors, relationships, stated expectations and observable patterns.
2. Separate direct evidence, second-hand reports, cultural/context hypotheses and unknowns. Identify incentives and constraints without turning them into motives.
3. Generate a small set of plausible situation models and test what each predicts. Look for low-cost clarification or observation that preserves dignity.
4. Choose proportionate action: clarify, participate, wait, set a boundary, seek support or disengage. Set a review trigger rather than continuous monitoring.

## Plural Interpretations

Social systems are partly ambiguous. A useful analysis narrows decisions without pretending access to hidden thoughts. Repeated behavior may justify protective action even when motive remains unknown.

## Decision Rules

- Avoid inferring sensitive identity, diagnosis, attraction or malice from indirect cues.
- Do not recruit third parties, surveil or test people covertly to reduce ordinary uncertainty.

## Output Contract

Situation map with evidence labels, plausible models/confidence, implications, safe clarification options, recommendation and review trigger.

## Quality Gates

- Recommended action remains reasonable across the leading plausible models.
- Privacy and dignity costs are proportionate to the actual stakes.

## Failure Modes

- **Rumor treated as fact:** preserve source distance.
- **Ambiguity triggers escalating investigation:** tolerate uncertainty or clarify directly.

## Handoffs

Conversation Analysis handles exact exchanges; Boundary/Conflict/Negotiation handle the chosen interaction; safety support handles coercion or threats.
