# life-strategy

[Personal Strategist](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Shape a coherent long-horizon direction across competing life roles and seasons.

## Activate When

Several life roles or long-term options need a coherent direction rather than a single decision.

## Do Not Use When

Not a productivity-maximization plan or a rigid life forecast.

## Required Context

**Needed**

User-stated values, current responsibilities, desired/undesired patterns, resources, horizons and uncertainties.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Exact multi-year milestones can remain provisional; current high-consequence external rules require specialist verification.

## Workflow

1. Describe the life the user wants to experience and protect across roles, not only achievements. Identify current commitments and structural constraints.
2. Find a few directional choices and exclusions: what to deepen, maintain, explore, reduce or leave. Include rest, relationships, health and option preservation as legitimate outcomes.
3. Trace interactions and concentration: one choice may consume the same money, location, attention or identity flexibility needed by another.
4. Build a staged direction with near-term experiments and long-horizon principles. Set signposts for when evidence, values or circumstances justify revision.

## Seasons and Coherence

Strategy can deliberately emphasize different roles in different seasons without claiming every area is balanced at all times. Coherence means trade-offs are conscious and survivable, not that all goals maximize one metric. Preserve slack and uncommitted identity: option value can justify delaying premature specialization.

## Decision Rules

- If the direction requires persistent self-neglect or fictional capacity, redesign rather than moralize discipline.
- If several paths remain genuinely attractive, fund learning and preserve options instead of forcing false finality.

## Output Contract

Directional portfolio: valued patterns, commitments/exclusions, role interactions, exploration/maintenance choices and review signposts.

## Quality Gates

- The strategy contains an explicit less/not-now list and protects essential obligations.
- Near-term actions can teach or help without pretending to prove a fixed future self.

## Failure Modes

- **Five-year narrative presented as forecast:** use principles, scenarios and signposts.
- **Productivity or status becomes the only definition of a good life:** return to plural values.

## Handoffs

Planner tests lived capacity; Finance tests affordability; Social Coach identifies relationship consequences; domain experts validate health/legal constraints.
