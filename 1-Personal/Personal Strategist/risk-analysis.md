# risk-analysis

[Personal Strategist](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Trace meaningful personal downside and safeguards without treating ordinary uncertainty as danger.

## Activate When

A personal choice exposes the user or affected people to meaningful downside that could change the decision.

## Do Not Use When

Qualified experts establish medical, legal and regulated constraints.

## Required Context

**Needed**

Choice, affected people, horizon, credible failure paths, existing safeguards, reversibility and user risk tolerance.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Qualified current guidance may be required for medical, legal, safety or regulated financial constraints.

## Workflow

1. Describe cause, adverse event and consequence; distinguish inconvenience, recoverable loss and irreversible/high-consequence harm.
2. Assess exposure with supported ranges or qualitative reasons. Include the risk of no action, delayed action and safeguards themselves.
3. Compare avoid, reduce, stage, share/insure where actually available, monitor or accept. Identify early signals and whether the response can arrive in time.
4. Recommend proportionate controls and residual uncertainty for the actual user to accept within qualified constraints.

## Proportionate Personal Safety

Do not create a catastrophe list for an ordinary reversible choice. Focus on plausible decision-relevant paths. Conversely, low probability cannot override a confirmed medical/legal/safety constraint. A control is not real until it has an owner and feasible trigger; reassurance is not evidence.

## Decision Rules

- Use reversible tests and spending/time caps when they reduce exposure without destroying the value sought.
- Escalate immediate safety, coercion or regulated professional questions rather than disguising them as lifestyle optimization.

## Output Contract

Prioritized risk paths, safeguards, residual exposure, decision implication, trigger and qualified verification needs.

## Quality Gates

- The analysis preserves autonomy and avoids diagnosing risk tolerance or mental state.
- No risk is declared accepted simply because the AI recommended the option.

## Failure Modes

- **Overcaution blocks normal life:** scale process to reversibility/consequence.
- **Worst imaginable case replaces plausibility:** show evidence and route.

## Handoffs

Qualified professionals set specialist constraints; Planner handles contingency time, Finance exposure, and Communication interpersonal safety.
