# opportunity-cost-analysis

[Personal Strategist](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Show which personal options, identity investments and flexibility a choice displaces.

## Activate When

A personal commitment would consume scarce time, attention, location flexibility, identity investment or relationship capacity.

## Do Not Use When

Finance owns monetary affordability; this skill covers the broader life portfolio.

## Required Context

**Needed**

Proposed choice, next-best feasible alternatives, affected resources, horizon, reversibility and user values.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Detailed cash calculation may be delegated to Finance; uncertain future alternatives stay conditional.

## Workflow

1. Name the scarce resource and the specific feasible alternative most likely to be displaced. Do not add every imagined possibility as a cost.
2. Compare incremental future benefits and burdens on a common horizon, including transition, recovery, foregone learning and option value.
3. Separate reversible occupancy from lock-in. Identify the last time the user could switch without losing disproportionate value.
4. Test whether a smaller, delayed or delegated commitment preserves the most valuable option. Return the displacement in plain language.

## Option Value and Regret

Option value is useful when waiting preserves a real future choice and relevant evidence can arrive; waiting that closes the window also has a cost. Future regret is a perspective test, not a probability or decision rule. Compare regret from action and inaction under plausible values, then return to present evidence and owner choice.

## Decision Rules

- Sunk effort is excluded from future comparison except for actual exit consequences.
- A nonexistent or unaffordable alternative is not counted as the next-best displaced use.

## Output Contract

Opportunity-cost statement with scarce resource, next-best use, timing/lock-in, option-preserving alternatives and decision effect.

## Quality Gates

- The displaced alternative is concrete enough to be chosen, not a fantasy composite.
- Financial cost is not double-counted again as time or emotional burden without an actual mechanism.

## Failure Modes

- **All unchosen lives treated as simultaneous losses:** use only the best feasible alternative.
- **Delay praised as flexibility when it actually expires the opportunity:** show both sides.

## Handoffs

Finance owns financial-only opportunity cost; Planner checks capacity; Decision Analysis integrates the whole choice.
