# goal-setting

[Personal Strategist](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Turn a valued direction into a bounded outcome with evidence, limits and a review/exit rule.

## Activate When

A valued direction needs a deliberate goal, or an existing goal needs clearer success and limits.

## Do Not Use When

A goal is optional; not every valued part of life needs measurement.

## Required Context

**Needed**

Why the outcome matters, current state, user control/influence, competing commitments and acceptable costs.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Detailed milestones and calendar may be deferred to Personal Planner after the goal is accepted.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the underlying value and whether a formal goal improves action or would turn a healthy activity into unwanted optimization.
2. Define an observable outcome or learning goal, scope and horizon. Separate controllable actions from results the user can only influence.
3. Name the minimum viable version, limits on time/money/energy and what will deliberately not be pursued.
4. Choose progress evidence and a review point. Define continue, revise, pause and release conditions before attachment to the goal grows.

## Goal Worthiness

A goal deserves maintenance only while its expected contribution to the user’s values exceeds its burdens and displaced alternatives. Some directions are better held as a principle, routine or permission rather than a target. Negative evidence can complete a learning goal. Stopping or redefining a goal is not automatically failure.

## Decision Rules

- If measurement damages the experience more than it guides action, use a qualitative review instead.
- If success depends mostly on another person or external event, define the user’s contribution without promising the outcome.

## Output Contract

Goal statement with meaning, outcome/learning evidence, bounds, minimum version, non-goals and review/exit rules.

## Quality Gates

- The goal coexists with essential recovery and obligations or its trade-off is explicitly accepted.
- Progress evidence cannot be gamed by completing activity while missing the intended outcome.

## Failure Modes

- **A goal adopted because it sounds respectable:** return to the user’s own reason.
- **Too many goals labeled simultaneous priorities:** select or sequence them.

## Handoffs

Planner translates an accepted goal into capacity-feasible action; Systems Designer supports recurring mechanics only if needed.
