# option-comparison

[Personal Strategist](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Compare credible life alternatives on a common basis while preserving competing values.

## Activate When

Two or more feasible personal alternatives need fair comparison without hiding differences in scope.

## Do Not Use When

Does not force incomparable values into a single score.

## Required Context

**Needed**

Decision, options, nonnegotiable constraints, user criteria, evidence, horizon and uncertainty.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Weights may remain qualitative when the user cannot meaningfully trade values numerically.

## Workflow

1. Normalize what each option includes, when benefits/costs occur and which commitments remain. Keep conditional or unavailable options labeled.
2. Screen hard constraints, then compare lived outcomes, money/time/energy, relationships, learning, reversibility and next-best displacement as relevant.
3. Remove genuinely dominated options and test sensitivity to uncertain facts and competing values. Do not optimize weights to make a favorite win.
4. Recommend a robust option or state the exact preference/evidence threshold that changes the leader.

## Comparable Personal Basis

A “best version” of one path must not be compared with the likely version of another. Include the status quo and a smaller option when credible. Some values are noncompensable; do not let a high convenience score override consent, safety or a user-stated hard boundary.

## Decision Rules

- If plausible assumptions reverse the result, prefer conditional advice or a learning step.
- If options differ mainly by timing, compare wait/commit value rather than duplicate categories.

## Output Contract

Normalized comparison, hard gates, trade-offs, sensitivity, recommendation and owner choice still required.

## Quality Gates

- The comparison contains no invented preference, benefit or external availability.
- A reader can see why an option is excluded or preferred without trusting an unexplained total score.

## Failure Modes

- **Scorecard creates false objectivity:** show decisive trade-off/threshold.
- **Doing nothing omitted because it looks unproductive:** include its real benefits and costs.

## Handoffs

Finance supplies total-cost evidence, Planner feasibility, and Social Coach relationship effects when those could change the result.
