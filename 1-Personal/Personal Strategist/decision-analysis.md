# decision-analysis

[Personal Strategist](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Make a consequential personal choice using the user’s values, constraints and uncertainty.

## Activate When

A personal choice matters across values, time, identity or relationships and the next commitment is not obvious.

## Do Not Use When

Does not choose values, schedule implementation or approve professional-domain risk.

## Required Context

**Needed**

Decision owner, due choice and timing, user-stated values, feasible options, constraints and relevant evidence.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Precise forecasts can remain ranges; ask only for preferences or constraints that could reverse the choice.

## Workflow

1. Define the smallest decision due now, including do nothing, defer, narrow and reversible trial where feasible. Separate the user’s objective from a requested solution.
2. Screen actual constraints, then compare remaining options against the user’s competing values, likely lived experience, future obligations and what each displaces.
3. Identify irreversible elements, exit costs, cost of delay and information that could change the option before the decision window closes.
4. Test the leading option against uncertainty and a future-self perspective without treating imagined regret as a fact. Recommend a next commitment and a reconsideration trigger.

## Personal Decision Record

Keep facts, assumptions, owner preferences and external constraints separate. Use scoring only when the user can defend the criteria/ranges; a score cannot resolve an unstated value conflict. A recommendation may be “not now,” “less,” or “stop.” Past effort informs transition cost but is not a reason to continue by itself.

## Decision Rules

- If a low-cost reversible step preserves options and produces decision-changing evidence, prefer it to an irreversible commitment.
- If two values cannot both be satisfied, expose the trade-off and let the user own it rather than optimizing one silently.

## Output Contract

Decision brief with options, value trade-offs, recommendation, uncertainty, next reversible/authorized step and revisit conditions.

## Quality Gates

- The recommendation traces to the user’s stated values and actual constraints.
- No domain-specific safety, financial, legal or medical gate is inferred or overruled.

## Failure Modes

- **Productivity treated as the default value:** include rest, relationships, meaning and doing less where relevant.
- **Sunk cost protects a failing option:** compare future-only consequences plus real exit obligations.

## Handoffs

Finance checks affordability, Planner checks capacity, Communication checks relationship effects, and qualified specialists validate professional constraints.
