# scenario-planning

[Personal Strategist](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Explore plausible future paths, option value, regret and contingent choices.

## Activate When

A long-term choice depends on interacting uncertain futures and flexible responses.

## Do Not Use When

Scenarios are not predictions or invented probabilities.

## Required Context

**Needed**

Decision/options, horizon, decision-sensitive uncertainties, constraints, lead times and observable signposts.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Probabilities are optional and must not be invented; detailed forecasts can remain ranges.

## Workflow

1. Select a few uncertainties capable of changing the choice, such as income stability, location, relationship needs, energy or opportunity timing.
2. Build coherent paths with causal sequence rather than arbitrary optimistic/base/pessimistic percentages. Include a plausible adverse and an option-rich path.
3. Evaluate each choice under the same values and constraints, including regret, lock-in and the cost of preserving flexibility.
4. Design contingent actions and signposts early enough to act. Recommend a robust step or an explicit if/then path.

## Plausible Futures, Not Predictions

Scenarios expose dependencies and choices; they do not forecast identity or life events. Equal scenario count does not imply equal probability. If the signal arrives after an irreversible commitment, it cannot protect that commitment; stage earlier or consciously accept the downside.

## Decision Rules

- Prefer actions useful across futures when they do not impose disproportionate current sacrifice.
- Retain a no-change path and consider whether delay increases or destroys option value.

## Output Contract

Scenario map with assumptions, option consequences, future-regret perspective, signposts and contingent/robust recommendation.

## Quality Gates

- Each scenario differs through a meaningful mechanism and remains internally coherent.
- Contingencies fit actual response lead time and do not assume future courage/capacity as guaranteed.

## Failure Modes

- **Scenario stories become destiny:** keep revision and agency explicit.
- **Only downside imagined:** include possibility/cost of missed upside and no action.

## Handoffs

Decision Analysis integrates the current choice; Planner sequences an accepted contingency; specialists validate domain constraints.
