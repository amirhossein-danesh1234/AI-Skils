# purchase-analysis

[Personal Finance Advisor](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Decide whether, when and how an expensive purchase fits the need and finances.

## Activate When

A significant purchase needs a need, timing, affordability and ownership decision.

## Do Not Use When

No spending action, credit approval or product endorsement from price alone.

## Required Context

**Needed**

User need and alternatives, price/terms/date, available cash, obligations/reserves, expected use, ownership costs and return/cancellation terms.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Nonmaterial accessories may be deferred. Changing prices, stock, rates, taxes, laws and financing terms require current verification.

## Workflow

1. Define the underlying outcome and whether buy, borrow/rent, repair, buy used, defer or do without can meet it. Avoid starting from the preferred product.
2. Verify comparable current offers and full ownership cost. Separate known price from estimated use/maintenance/resale and financing forecast.
3. Test payment timing against cash flow and protected liquidity. Do not treat credit approval/limit as affordability or budget as spending authorization.
4. Compare expected use, useful life, failure/exit, regret and opportunity cost. Recommend buy now, wait, choose a bounded alternative or decline with a review trigger.

## Purchase Gate

A purchase can be affordable but not valuable, valuable but not currently liquid, or cheap but expensive to own. Keep these questions separate. For high-consequence credit, contracts, tax or investment trade-offs, request qualified current advice rather than converting general analysis into regulated guidance.

## Decision Rules

- If waiting can yield decision-changing evidence without unacceptable cost, preserve the option.
- If the product is mostly desired experience/status, treat that as a legitimate stated value—not a necessity or moral failure.

## Output Contract

Purchase brief with need, alternatives, verified costs/terms, cash impact, TCO, uncertainty, recommendation and approval still required.

## Quality Gates

- The recommendation fits current liquidity after obligations/reserve.
- External facts are dated/sourced and estimates are not relabeled guarantees.

## Failure Modes

- **Monthly payment anchors decision:** show total and exit terms.
- **Sale urgency creates false deadline:** verify expiry/stock and cost of waiting.

## Handoffs

Cost Comparison supplies equivalent totals; Cash Flow timing; Strategist weighs nonfinancial values; qualified tax/legal/credit advisers as needed.
