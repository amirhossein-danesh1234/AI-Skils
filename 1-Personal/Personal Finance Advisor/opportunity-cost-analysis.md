# opportunity-cost-analysis

[Personal Finance Advisor](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Identify the next-best financial use and liquidity/flexibility displaced by a choice.

## Activate When

A financial allocation would reduce another feasible use of money or liquidity.

## Do Not Use When

Strategist owns nonfinancial life opportunity cost.

## Required Context

**Needed**

Proposed amount/timing, unrestricted resources, next-best feasible use, horizon, commitments and uncertainty.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Returns/savings can remain ranges; current rates/tax/fees require verification before use.

## Workflow

1. Identify the actual next-best financial use: reserve, obligation, debt reduction, saving goal or another purchase, not every possible use.
2. Compare incremental future cash effects, risk, liquidity, reversibility and timing on a common horizon. Exclude sunk spending except real exit costs.
3. Account for flexibility: money committed to an illiquid or hard-to-reverse use has a different cost from money held for known near-term needs.
4. Test smaller/staged/delayed allocation and state what financial outcome or resilience is displaced.

## Financial Opportunity Cost

Opportunity cost is the value of the best feasible alternative, not a fictional guaranteed investment return or the sum of all rejected uses. If returns, interest or tax effects determine the result, use current qualified evidence and disclose that suitability may require licensed advice.

## Decision Rules

- Do not count the same foregone cash both as reserve loss and as a separate full alternative unless effects differ.
- If the user has no adequate liquidity for known obligations, show that constraint before discretionary comparisons.

## Output Contract

Financial displacement, liquidity/reversibility effect, supported range, alternatives and decision implication.

## Quality Gates

- The next-best alternative fits the same money and time period.
- No guaranteed return, permanent rate or invented tax result is used.

## Failure Modes

- **Prestigious alternative used as assumed opportunity:** require feasibility.
- **Only nominal return compared while lock-in/timing differs:** include liquidity.

## Handoffs

Strategist owns time/identity/relationship displacement; Cash Flow tests payment timing; qualified advisers handle investment/debt suitability.
