# saving-planning

[Personal Finance Advisor](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Build a contribution path that serves a goal without creating a nearer liquidity failure.

## Activate When

A chosen financial goal needs a feasible contribution path and protection from nearer cash needs.

## Do Not Use When

No guaranteed return, tax treatment or investment recommendation.

## Required Context

**Needed**

Goal amount/date/reason, current earmarked balance, income/obligations, liquidity need, contribution timing and uncertainty.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Return assumptions may be omitted or scenario ranges; no investment choice is required to create a cash contribution plan.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the goal and whether amount/date are fixed, preferred or negotiable. Separate contribution progress from market returns or external events.
2. Calculate required contributions from the remaining amount and periods using explicit timing; model irregular income/expenses and missed periods.
3. Test contributions against dated cash flow and protected liquidity. Sequence goals or adjust amount/date rather than funding all by fictional surplus.
4. Choose a sustainable path, automation only if authorized, review points and catch-up/reduction rules. Keep account/product choice separate.

## Goal and Liquidity Balance

Savings are not progress if contributions create expensive shortfalls or underfund essential obligations. A contribution target can be complete even with zero assumed investment return. If returns/tax/fees matter, verify current terms and use qualified suitability guidance; do not promise growth.

## Decision Rules

- If the plan relies on variable income, use base and low-receipt contribution rules.
- If several goals compete, show sequence and displacement rather than labeling every target mandatory.

## Output Contract

Saving schedule with required/optional contributions, liquidity check, scenarios, automation/review rules and trade-offs.

## Quality Gates

- Contribution arithmetic reconciles with the goal and dates.
- The plan remains feasible under a relevant disruption or states the shortfall honestly.

## Failure Modes

- **Aggressive target silently cuts essentials/recovery:** expose trade-off.
- **Nominal target ignores inflation/tax/fees when material:** verify current context or state exclusion.

## Handoffs

Budgeting allocates funds; Cash Flow proves timing; Strategist confirms value/priority; qualified advisers handle financial products.
