# cost-comparison

[Personal Finance Advisor](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Compare alternatives on equivalent total cost and time horizon.

## Activate When

Alternatives have different prices, timing, useful lives or hidden ownership burdens.

## Do Not Use When

Does not choose whether the underlying need matters.

## Required Context

**Needed**

Comparable requirement, quotes/prices with date/source, included/excluded costs, usage horizon, payment timing and uncertainty.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Maintenance/resale/usage may be ranges; current rates, fees, taxes and terms require verification when material.

## Workflow

1. Normalize quantity, quality/requirement, currency, date and horizon. Keep unavailable or conditional alternatives labeled.
2. Build total cost of ownership: acquisition, financing where actual, setup, operation, maintenance, consumables, fees/tax, switching, exit and realistic residual value.
3. Avoid double-counting included costs and discount uncertain savings appropriately. Show cash timing separately from lifetime total.
4. Test usage, life and cost sensitivity; identify the threshold where the cheaper choice changes. Return financial evidence, not a product/life preference decision.

## Equivalent Scope

The lowest sticker price is not necessarily the lowest comparable cost, and a forecast resale value is not guaranteed cash. A financed monthly payment cannot be compared with an upfront total without interest/fees/horizon. Unknown stays unknown rather than a balancing zero.

## Decision Rules

- Use current verified external facts only for the exact product, location, date and terms.
- If differences are small relative to uncertainty, report them as practically unresolved.

## Output Contract

Comparison table with equivalent scope, total and dated cash costs, assumptions, sensitivity and financial preference thresholds.

## Quality Gates

- Every included cost has a source/assumption and is included once.
- The recommendation does not imply regulated suitability or override nonfinancial values.

## Failure Modes

- **Nominal monthly price hides total:** calculate full terms.
- **Different service/quality levels compared as equals:** normalize requirement.

## Handoffs

Purchase Analysis decides whether/when; Strategist compares nonfinancial outcomes; qualified tax/credit specialists validate regulated implications.
