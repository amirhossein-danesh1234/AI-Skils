# budgeting

[Personal Finance Advisor](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Allocate current personal money among obligations, flexibility and chosen goals.

## Activate When

The user needs a current allocation plan or spending has no clear envelope.

## Do Not Use When

Cash-flow planning owns dated liquidity; no tax/investment suitability advice.

## Required Context

**Needed**

Currency, dated available balances/income, recurring obligations, variable essentials, priorities and irregular known costs.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Future variable amounts can be estimates/ranges. Tax, rate, price and legal treatment must be verified when material.

## Workflow

1. Choose a named period and reconcile starting cash plus receipts against obligations and allocations. Distinguish restricted/reserved money from genuinely available funds.
2. Fund essential obligations and minimum commitments first, then protect liquidity/reserves chosen by the user before discretionary goals.
3. Use realistic variable ranges and sinking funds for irregular but expected costs. Keep savings, spending and debt actions tied to actual dates/terms.
4. Balance the plan, show trade-offs and define a lightweight actual-versus-plan review without turning every purchase into surveillance.

## Budget as an Allocation Contract

A budget is a values-and-constraints plan, not a moral score. Every currency unit is allocated once; expected income is not available cash before receipt. Zero spending in a category is not automatically realistic. If obligations exceed resources, present a shortfall and options rather than inventing income or silently underfunding essentials.

## Decision Rules

- Known amounts remain separate from estimates and unallocated buffer.
- Changing tax/rate/price facts are cited with date/source when used, not embedded as permanent defaults.

## Output Contract

Balanced period budget with assumptions/ranges, protected obligations, flexibility, shortfall/trade-offs and review date.

## Quality Gates

- Totals reconcile to balances and expected receipts by the same period/currency.
- The plan reflects the user’s priorities and permits ordinary flexible spending where affordable.

## Failure Modes

- **Annual total hides near-term shortage:** use cash-flow-planning.
- **Overly granular categories cost more attention than decisions improve:** consolidate.

## Handoffs

Cash Flow checks timing; Strategist resolves competing values; qualified debt/tax/investment advice handles regulated choices.
