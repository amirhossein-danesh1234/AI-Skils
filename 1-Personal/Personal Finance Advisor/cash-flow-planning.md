# cash-flow-planning

[Personal Finance Advisor](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Sequence expected receipts and payments to expose the first potential cash shortfall.

## Activate When

Timing of receipts and payments may create a shortfall even when the overall budget looks affordable.

## Do Not Use When

Expected income is not available cash and a forecast is not a guarantee.

## Required Context

**Needed**

Starting unrestricted cash, dated receipts/payments, minimum balances, payment flexibility and horizon.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Uncertain dates/amounts can use ranges and separate scenarios; do not treat expected receipts as confirmed.

## Workflow

1. Create a chronological ledger from authoritative balances and commitments. Label each entry known, estimate, forecast or scenario.
2. Calculate running unrestricted cash and identify first/maximum shortfall plus concentration around large dates. Preserve restricted/reserved balances separately.
3. Test delayed/lower receipts and earlier/higher expenses where plausible. Include automatic payments, annual/irregular obligations and transaction timing.
4. Compare timing changes, spending deferral, sufficient reserves or authorized funding routes; do not assume credit availability or transfer permission.

## Liquidity Before Totals

A positive month-end total can conceal a midmonth failure. A pending transfer, credit limit or expected salary is not cash until its availability and terms are supported. Avoid double-counting money moved between the user’s own accounts. Use actual calendar dates and settlement delays when material.

## Decision Rules

- If a scenario breaches the user’s minimum liquidity, flag it before discretionary allocation.
- If a remedy depends on fees/rates/terms, verify current authoritative conditions and keep it conditional.

## Output Contract

Dated cash ledger, low point/shortfall, uncertainty scenarios, feasible timing choices and review trigger.

## Quality Gates

- Opening cash plus receipts minus payments reconciles to each closing balance.
- No shortfall is filled by invented income, unapproved borrowing or restricted money.

## Failure Modes

- **Budget affordability mistaken for payment readiness:** show dates.
- **Uncertain incoming money shown as certain:** retain scenarios.

## Handoffs

Budgeting owns category allocation; Purchase/Saving plans use the verified liquidity envelope; qualified advisers handle complex debt.
