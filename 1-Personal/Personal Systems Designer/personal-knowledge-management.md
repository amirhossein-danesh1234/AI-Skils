# personal-knowledge-management

[Personal Systems Designer](README.md) / [Personal domain](../../README.md)

## Purpose

Make captured knowledge retrievable for actual decisions and projects.

## Activate When

Notes and references need a sustainable path from capture to retrieval and use.

## Do Not Use When

Not an archive-everything mandate or a replacement for task tracking.

## Required Context

**Needed**

Knowledge uses, source types, current capture/retrieval habits, trusted tools, privacy needs, review frequency and representative searches.

**Can be deferred or bounded**

Complex linking, tags or migrations may be deferred unless evidence shows need.

## Workflow

1. Define decisions or work the knowledge should support and sample recent failures to remember, find, trust or apply information.
2. Separate inbox/capture, durable notes, source/reference records and active outputs. Assign one source of truth and minimal metadata.
3. Design processing, linking only when useful, source/provenance recording, retrieval and review. Bound AI access and preserve citations when it summarizes.
4. Pilot with real material and test retrieval plus reuse; remove fields, tags or rituals whose value does not exceed upkeep.

## Use-Driven Knowledge Loop

Capture is not knowledge management unless material can be trusted, retrieved and used. Avoid collecting for hypothetical future selves. Personal observations may be valuable but must not be relabeled external fact.

## Decision Rules

- Preserve source, date and confidence when claims may change or matter.
- Use the smallest classification vocabulary that supports retrieval decisions.

## Output Contract

PKM design with use cases, object types, source-of-truth model, capture/process/retrieve/review flow, provenance/privacy rules and pilot metrics.

## Quality Gates

- Representative questions can be answered from the system with clear provenance.
- Maintenance fits a realistic review cadence.

## Failure Modes

- **Linking/tagging becomes the hobby:** measure downstream use.
- **AI summaries erase provenance:** retain source pointers and uncertainty.

## Handoffs

File Organization owns artifact storage; Task System owns commitments; Core research validates external evidence.
