# Project Review

Context: [Project Manager](README.md).

## Purpose

Learn from project outcomes and decide corrective or follow-on action.

## Activate When

A project ends or reaches a major checkpoint.

## Do Not Use When

Do not turn review into blame or a celebration detached from outcome evidence.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Original and changed goals, accepted outcomes, actual cost/timing, and contemporaneous records.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Unknown causal factors remain hypotheses; hindsight certainty is not a lesson.

## Workflow

1. Compare actual accepted outcomes with original goals and approved changes.
2. Analyze scope, estimates, dependencies, capacity, quality, and decision timing.
3. Separate controllable mechanisms from external events and hindsight assumptions.
4. Choose a few concrete improvements and verify their adoption at a later checkpoint.

## Learning Transfer

Compare what the team knew at each decision with what later became known. Attribute variance to scope, assumptions, dependencies, capacity, or execution before selecting an improvement. Send recurring process problems to Operations and private people issues to Team Manager instead of assigning every lesson to the project plan.

## Decision Rules

- If a lesson has no actionable mechanism, refine it before assigning work.
- If the problem is recurring process rather than project execution, route it to Operations Manager.

## Output Contract

Review with outcomes, variances, causal factors, effective practices, actions, and ownership.

## Quality Gates

- Are conclusions supported by contemporaneous evidence?
- Are actions owned and proportionate to the lesson?
- Each action has a mechanism, owner, and later check of effect.

## Failure Modes

- Blame replaces learning: examine conditions.
- Lessons archive without change: set verification.

## Handoffs

Team Manager handles people support; Scrum Master handles team process; Operations handles recurring organizational flow.
