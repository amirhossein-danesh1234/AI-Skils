# Project Manager — Scope Management

Context: [Project Manager](README.md).

## Purpose

Control changes to an agreed delivery baseline and make their consequences visible.

## Activate When

A project receives additions, removals, or changed acceptance conditions.

## Do Not Use When

Product Manager decides product inclusion and value; this skill coordinates delivery impact and approval.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Delivery baseline, change request, acceptance impacts, and actual approval rules.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** A verbal approval may be valid under local rules; record and confirm its scope rather than inventing a universal written-only gate.

## Workflow

1. Compare the request with the accepted baseline and identify affected deliverables.
2. Estimate cost, schedule, capacity, risk, and dependency effects with owners.
3. Offer accept, substitute, defer, or reject options rather than silently adding work.
4. Record the authorized decision and update plans, acceptance, and stakeholder communication.

## Baseline Change

Separate the Product Manager value decision from sponsor authorization of delivery impacts. Show cost, schedule, capacity, risk, and displaced commitments for each change. Maintain original and revised baselines so scope growth is not mislabeled as pure execution failure.

## Decision Rules

- If no authorized decision exists, keep the baseline and label the change proposed.
- If scope is added under a fixed deadline, identify what is removed or what capacity is credibly added.

## Output Contract

Change assessment with rationale, impacts, options, approval status, and updated baseline.

## Quality Gates

- Do plan, budget, acceptance, and communication reflect the same approved scope?
- Are displaced commitments explicit?
- Scope, acceptance, budget, and forecast reflect the same authorized decision.

## Failure Modes

- Quiet additions create hidden delay: use visible change decisions.
- Delivery manager redefines value: obtain product approval.

## Handoffs

Product Manager owns product trade-off; Team Manager validates capacity; sponsor approves baseline changes.
