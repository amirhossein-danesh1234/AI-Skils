# Team Risk Analysis

Context: [Team Manager](README.md).

## Purpose

Identify threats to team delivery and sustainability before they become crises.

## Activate When

A team has concentration, overload, ownership, or continuity concerns.

## Do Not Use When

Do not speculate about individuals’ private intentions or collect sensitive personal data unnecessarily.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Critical responsibilities, skills coverage, workload, handoffs, and observable continuity evidence.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Private intentions or health assumptions are unnecessary; focus on documented availability and coverage.

## Workflow

1. Map single-person knowledge, overloaded roles, unclear authority, and fragile handoffs.
2. Assess consequences of absence, demand spikes, conflict, or skill gaps using observable evidence.
3. Choose cross-training, documentation, workload changes, clearer ownership, or succession support.
4. Review whether mitigation improves resilience without creating excessive process.

## Continuity Drill

Test absence of a key person, a demand spike, and a failed handoff. Verify another qualified person can actually perform the critical work with needed access, not merely that documentation exists. Choose cross-training, clearer ownership, workload changes, or specialist support proportional to the risk.

## Decision Rules

- If a risk depends on guessing a person’s motives, replace speculation with observable conditions.
- If wellbeing or serious conduct concerns arise, use appropriate confidential support and policy channels.

## Output Contract

Team risk assessment with scenarios, evidence, impact, mitigations, owners, and review triggers.

## Quality Gates

- Are mitigations practical and respectful of privacy?
- Are critical responsibilities covered during plausible absence or overload?
- Mitigation improves continuity without relying on gossip, surveillance, or perpetual overtime.

## Failure Modes

- Risk register becomes gossip: use evidence.
- Documentation alone solves concentration: verify another person can act.

## Handoffs

Team Manager coordinates; Operations validates continuity; HR handles sensitive people matters; Project Manager adjusts commitments.
