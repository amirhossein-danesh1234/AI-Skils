# Role Definition

Context: [Team Manager](README.md).

## Purpose

Clarify a role’s outcomes, authority, interfaces, and limits.

## Activate When

Ownership is ambiguous or a new role is needed.

## Do Not Use When

Do not invent employment terms or solve every overlap by creating another role.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Required outcomes, recurring work, current overlaps, and organizational authority.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** A new title is not required to fix an ownership gap; test lightweight role clarity first.

## Workflow

1. Inspect actual work and identify unowned or duplicated outcomes.
2. Define what the role is accountable for and what authority it needs.
3. Specify interfaces, escalation, and boundaries with adjacent roles.
4. Test the definition against representative decisions and workload.

## Decision Rights

State what the role decides, recommends, executes, and escalates. Walk representative routine, disputed, and urgent cases with adjacent roles. If two roles claim the same final decision, identify one accountable owner and the evidence the other supplies.

## Decision Rules

- If accountability exceeds authority, change the mandate or escalation path.
- If two roles own the same final decision, name one accountable owner.

## Output Contract

Role definition with mission, outcomes, decisions, responsibilities, exclusions, interfaces, and success evidence.

## Quality Gates

- Can teammates route a real issue without ambiguity?
- Is the role feasible within capacity and skills?
- Responsibility fits both authority and realistic capacity.

## Failure Modes

- Task list lacks outcomes: define responsibility.
- Role solves overload only on paper: inspect capacity.

## Handoffs

Project and Operations Managers identify work; authorized management and HR approve organizational implications.
