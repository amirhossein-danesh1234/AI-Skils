# Delegation

Context: [Team Manager](README.md).

## Purpose

Transfer a bounded outcome with sufficient context, authority, and support.

## Activate When

Work should move to another person without losing accountability.

## Do Not Use When

Do not delegate decisions the recipient lacks authority or capacity to make.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Outcome, recipient capacity/skill, authority limits, resources, and acceptance.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Detailed method may belong to the delegate; do not mistake autonomy for absence of support.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the result and why it matters before prescribing steps.
2. Assess readiness and choose an appropriate level of autonomy and support.
3. Agree authority, constraints, available resources, and review points.
4. Verify shared understanding and review outcomes without unnecessary micromanagement.

## Delegation Level

Specify whether the recipient investigates, recommends, decides within limits, or executes an approved decision. Agree escalation triggers, available support, and proportionate checkpoints. Ask the recipient to restate outcome and boundaries; include learning time rather than silently assigning overload.

## Decision Rules

- If the recipient lacks a required skill, provide support or narrow the assignment.
- If a decision crosses the mandate, require escalation rather than implicit permission.

## Output Contract

Delegation agreement with outcome, boundaries, resources, checkpoints, escalation, and definition of done.

## Quality Gates

- Can the recipient explain success and limits in their own terms?
- Are workload and dependencies feasible?
- The recipient knows what they may decide without returning every choice for approval.

## Failure Modes

- Task dumped without authority: supply decision rights.
- Delegation becomes surveillance: use proportionate checkpoints.

## Handoffs

Project Manager aligns delivery; relevant specialists provide support; [role-definition.md](role-definition.md) clarifies mandate.
