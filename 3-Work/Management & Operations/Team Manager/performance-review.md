# Team Manager — Performance Review

Context: [Team Manager](README.md).

## Purpose

Assess work against clear expectations using fair, observable evidence.

## Activate When

A scheduled review or support decision needs an evidence-based assessment.

## Do Not Use When

Do not diagnose personality, use protected attributes, or make employment decisions without authority and policy.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Agreed role expectations, representative work evidence, prior feedback/support, and policy.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Unknown context is a question, not a character judgment; employment consequences need authorized HR/management.

## Workflow

1. Inspect agreed expectations and whether the person had authority, resources, and clarity.
2. Collect representative work evidence across the period, not only recent incidents.
3. Separate skill, effort, system constraints, unclear goals, and support gaps.
4. Discuss specific observations, invite context, and agree development or correction steps under policy.

## Fair Evidence

Review the full period and comparable expectations, separating results within control from systemic constraints. Consider whether goals, authority, resources, and feedback were available. Invite the person’s response and set specific support/development actions; do not infer effort or personality from story points or activity logs.

## Decision Rules

- If expectations were unclear or changed, address that before judging failure.
- If the matter may require discipline or legal action, involve authorized HR and management.

## Output Contract

Balanced assessment with outcomes, examples, context, strengths, gaps, support plan, and review point.

## Quality Gates

- Are conclusions supported by observable examples and consistent criteria?
- Are privacy, employee response, and support needs respected?
- Assessment criteria are consistent and material contrary evidence is addressed.

## Failure Modes

- Recency or personality bias: use representative evidence.
- Metrics become character judgment: assess role outcomes and context.

## Handoffs

HR handles policy and employment matters; Project Manager supplies delivery context; relevant mentors support development.
