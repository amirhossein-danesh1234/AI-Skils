# Impediment Analysis

Context: [Scrum Master-Agile Coach](README.md).

## Purpose

Identify why work cannot progress and route the blocker to the right owner.

## Activate When

A team is blocked or the same obstacle recurs.

## Do Not Use When

Do not solve every blocker with another meeting or assume all delay is a team-process problem.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Blocked outcome, elapsed wait, attempted actions, and authority/dependency evidence.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** A cause may be a hypothesis; the next action should discriminate it instead of adding generic coordination.

## Workflow

1. Define what progress is blocked and since when.
2. Separate missing information, technical dependency, capacity, ownership, approval, and process causes.
3. Test the likely cause against evidence and identify who can change it.
4. Arrange a bounded unblock action and review whether recurrence needs systemic work.

## Owner of Change

Separate missing decision, missing information, unavailable capacity, technical dependency, and recurring process constraint. Identify who can actually remove the block, what they need, and the latest useful decision time. A facilitator may coordinate escalation without becoming the owner of every underlying problem.

## Decision Rules

- If the team lacks authority, escalate with a specific decision request and consequence.
- If the issue is unclear ownership, resolve the owner before adding coordination.

## Output Contract

Impediment diagnosis with category, cause hypothesis, owner, immediate action, escalation, and prevention.

## Quality Gates

- Does the proposed action remove the actual constraint?
- Are escalation timing and outcome checks explicit?
- The proposed action changes the binding constraint rather than merely reports it.

## Failure Modes

- Symptom restated as cause: identify the mechanism.
- Blocker list without owner: assign authority.

## Handoffs

Project Manager resolves external dependencies; Team Manager resolves capacity; Operations Manager fixes recurring process constraints.
