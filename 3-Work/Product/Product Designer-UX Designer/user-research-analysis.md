# User Research Analysis

Context: [Product Designer-UX Designer](README.md).

## Purpose

Translate user research into supported design implications.

## Activate When

Interviews, observations, or usability sessions need synthesis.

## Do Not Use When

Do not infer prevalence from a convenience sample or replace research with invented personas.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Research question and raw permitted observations for synthesis.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** When observations are absent, return a collection plan; no synthetic findings or prevalence estimates.

## Workflow

1. Inspect sampling, prompts, recording quality, and missing user groups.
2. Separate direct observations and quotations from researcher interpretation.
3. Cluster recurring needs and behaviors while retaining contradictory cases.
4. Connect each finding to a design decision and identify the next evidence that could change it.

## When Raw Research Is Missing

Return the decision question, the participant or evidence needs, and a proportionate collection plan with consent and authorization requirements. Assign a proposed research owner and state which design decision the evidence must inform. Existing support examples can be inspected within their permitted use, but they are not automatically representative user research. Mark synthesis blocked until observations exist; do not fabricate findings, quotations, prevalence, or user emotions. A useful research handoff can be complete even when the requested synthesis is not.

## Evidence Coding

Keep observation IDs attached to themes and retain disconfirming cases. Distinguish repeated statements by one participant from independent participants. Show the reasoning from observation to need to design implication; a theme that changes no decision should not dominate the synthesis.

## Decision Rules

- If the method cannot support a frequency claim, report qualitative patterns without percentages.
- If one vocal participant dominates, test whether the finding appears elsewhere.

## Output Contract

Findings linked to observations, contradictory cases, confidence, design implications, and follow-up questions.

## Quality Gates

- Can each major finding be traced to raw evidence?
- Are selection bias and alternative explanations visible?
- A finding can be traced to its original participant/context without disclosing unnecessary identity.

## Failure Modes

- Theme counts mistaken for population truth: state sampling limits.
- Recommendations detached from observations: show the evidence chain.

## Handoffs

Product Analyst triangulates behavior; Product Manager decides scope; qualified researchers handle material sampling or consent complexity. No Core protocol is needed to execute this skill.
