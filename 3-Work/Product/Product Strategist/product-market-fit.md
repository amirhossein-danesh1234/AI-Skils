# Product Market Fit

Context: [Product Strategist](README.md).

## Purpose

Assess evidence that a defined customer group repeatedly receives sufficient product value.

## Activate When

A team must decide whether to refine, expand, or scale an offering.

## Do Not Use When

Do not declare permanent fit from survey enthusiasm, growth alone, or one successful customer.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Segmented sustained-value behavior and observation duration.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Early cohorts allow a directional assessment; survey enthusiasm cannot fill missing retention or willingness-to-pay evidence.

## Workflow

1. Inspect sustained behavior and repeat value over a period meaningful to the product’s usage cadence.
2. Separate acquisition spikes, incentives, contracts, and switching costs from genuine value retention.
3. Triangulate usage with willingness to pay, unsolicited demand, support burden, and qualitative explanations.
4. Identify segments with different outcomes and test the constraint before recommending broad scale.

## Fit Versus Friction

Separate value retention from contracts, incentives, and costly switching. Examine whether customers who can leave freely still choose repeated use, and whether support effort makes that value economically sustainable. State which segment and product version the evidence covers instead of declaring universal permanent fit.

## Decision Rules

- If retention or value evidence is immature, report insufficient evidence rather than binary fit.
- If demand is strong but service cost is unsustainable, recommend operating or pricing work before scaling.

## Output Contract

Fit assessment by segment, supporting and contrary evidence, constraints, and next learning or scale decision.

## Quality Gates

- Are cohorts mature enough and cancellations or inactive accounts included?
- Would the conclusion survive removing a promotional or atypical cohort?
- The assessment includes churned or inactive customers and a meaningful product usage cycle.

## Failure Modes

- Survey threshold treated as proof: triangulate behavior.
- Average conceals one strong segment: report segment variation.

## Handoffs

Product Analyst validates cohorts; Sales and Marketing explain acquisition mix; Financial Analyst checks economic sustainability.
