# Product Strategist — Strategic Prioritization

Context: [Product Strategist](README.md).

## Purpose

Rank product-level bets under scarce capacity and strategic uncertainty.

## Activate When

Leadership must choose which outcomes or opportunities receive investment.

## Do Not Use When

Product Manager prioritizes deliverable backlog items; this skill allocates attention across strategic bets.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Product bets, strategic choices, evidence, and agreed product resource envelope.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Company-level trade-offs go to Founder Advisor; preliminary scoring can use ranges.

## Workflow

1. Inspect whether each bet addresses the chosen customer and strategic obstacle.
2. Remove infeasible or policy-violating options before scoring preference.
3. Compare expected customer value, advantage, learning, economics, dependencies, and downside.
4. Test whether plausible changes in assumptions reverse the order; choose a portfolio the team can actually support.

## Product Bet Boundary

Compare mutually reinforcing product bets, including enabling work, against target customer and advantage. State resource displacement inside the product envelope. A request to take capital from another business activity is a Founder Advisor handoff, not an implicit product prioritization decision.

## Decision Rules

- If scores are too sensitive to speculative estimates, use a learning experiment or qualitative tiering.
- If a bet consumes the same scarce resource as a higher-value one, make displacement explicit.

## Output Contract

Ordered investment choices, rejected or delayed bets, rationale, sensitivity, and review triggers.

## Quality Gates

- Does the selected set fit real constraints and avoid double-counted benefits?
- Are urgent obligations separated from elective strategic bets?
- A product recommendation does not allocate company-wide resources without the company decision.

## Failure Modes

- Scoring ritual hides judgment: explain criteria and uncertainty.
- Everything marked critical: identify what will not receive resources.

## Handoffs

Product Manager and Project Manager assess execution consequences; Financial Analyst validates economics; Founder Advisor reviews any change to the company resource envelope.
