# Market Opportunity

Context: [Product Strategist](README.md).

## Purpose

Assess whether a specific customer problem offers an attractive product opportunity.

## Activate When

A new segment or unmet need is proposed for product investment.

## Do Not Use When

Market Researcher owns broad market sizing; Product Manager owns the detailed problem and scope decision.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Customer/problem hypothesis, alternatives, access route, and company/product constraints.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Market totals are optional for an initial rejection; demand and access evidence must precede scale investment.

## Workflow

1. Inspect observed frequency, severity, workarounds, and spending around the problem.
2. Segment users whose need and buying constraints differ; avoid averaging incompatible customers.
3. Compare current alternatives and barriers to switching, including doing nothing.
4. Estimate a reachable wedge and testable value proposition before extrapolating scale.

## Wedge Test

Separate user pain, buyer willingness, switching friction, and reachable distribution. Describe the first realistically reachable customer group and the reason it can adopt now. If willingness is only stated, design a behavioral test with a real trade-off rather than interpreting polite interest as demand.

## Decision Rules

- If users acknowledge a problem but will not change behavior, test friction or value before investing.
- If access costs dominate expected value, narrow the segment or reject the opportunity.

## Output Contract

Opportunity assessment with need intensity, underserved gap, reachable users, feasibility, uncertainties, and next test.

## Quality Gates

- Are customer need, reachability, and economic attractiveness separately evidenced?
- Does the proposed next test discriminate between pursue and stop?
- An opportunity can be attractive in theory yet rejected because the reachable wedge is uneconomic.

## Failure Modes

- Total market mistaken for addressable demand: define eligibility and access.
- Competitor absence assumed attractive: investigate why demand may not exist.

## Handoffs

Market Researcher validates external evidence; Financial Analyst checks plausible economics; Product Manager tests the problem.
