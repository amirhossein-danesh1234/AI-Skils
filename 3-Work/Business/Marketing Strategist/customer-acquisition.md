# Customer Acquisition

## Purpose

Choose an economical path from reachable audience to qualified customers.

## When to Use

The business needs new customers or acquisition efficiency is deteriorating.

## When Not to Use

Do not assume paid ads or content are the default, or optimize acquisition while ignoring retention.

## Required Inputs

### Required

Target segment, offer, funnel, channel evidence, contribution economics, budget, and capacity.

### Helpful

Audience evidence, offer, product positioning, channel history, budget, funnel, brand constraints, and claim substantiation.

### Optional

Previous decisions, comparable cases, or preferred output format. Their absence must not block a bounded first pass.

If a required fact is missing, identify which decision it affects. Continue with a labeled assumption only when the consequence is low risk and reversible; otherwise ask the smallest question needed. Do not invent project facts, approvals, measurements, or test results.

## Output

Acquisition plan with hypotheses, channels, funnel actions, cost limits, experiments, and stop rules.

## Operating Principles

Optimize qualified outcomes and learning, not impressions alone; distinguish attribution from incrementality and preserve brand credibility.

Separate verified facts, supplied information, external evidence, assumptions, estimates, inferences, opinions, and unknowns. Show the basis of consequential claims. Scale rigor to team capacity and risk; a framework is optional, and its limitations must be stated when used.

## Workflow

1. Inspect where the audience discovers and evaluates solutions.
2. Map the path from first contact to qualified value and identify the current constraint.
3. Compare channels on reachability, intent, cost, time to learn, and operational fit.
4. Run bounded tests and evaluate downstream customer quality, not only lead volume.

## Decision Rules

- If acquisition exceeds support or fulfillment capacity, constrain exposure before scaling.
- If payback depends on speculative retention, use conservative scenarios.

## Validation

- Are acquisition costs and qualified outcomes measured consistently?
- Does the plan fit cash and team capacity?

## Common Failure Modes

- Cheap leads mistaken for customers: track qualification.
- Attribution equals incrementality: state limits.

## Escalation and Collaboration

Sales validates lead quality; Product Analyst measures behavior; Financial Analyst validates payback.

Do not perform external writes, production changes, purchases, disclosures, or commitments merely because this protocol recommends them. Confirm the actual task authorizes the action and stop at the boundary of that authority.

## Completion Criteria

The defined output answers the activation question; its decision rules and validation checks have been applied. Explicitly separate a proposal from an approved decision and a planned test from an observed result. Record the recommendation or changed artifact, the validation actually performed, remaining uncertainty, and the next action with an owner or proposed owner. Include priority, dependency, and definition of done when work remains. A blocked execution can produce a useful assessment, but it is not a completed implementation.
