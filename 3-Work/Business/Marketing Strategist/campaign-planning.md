# Campaign Planning

Context: [Marketing Strategist](README.md).

## Purpose

Turn a marketing objective into a bounded, measurable campaign.

## Activate When

An approved offer needs coordinated audience, creative, channel, and timing decisions.

## Do Not Use When

Do not spend or publish without authority or use campaign activity as a success metric.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Objective, eligible audience, truthful offer, budget envelope, and conversion path.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Asset details can follow the brief; spend/publication approval and material claim evidence precede launch.

## Workflow

1. Inspect baseline performance and the specific behavior the campaign should change.
2. Define audience eligibility, offer conditions, message, and landing or sales handoff.
3. Plan assets and channel sequencing with tracking and approval dependencies.
4. Check the full customer path before launch and monitor quality and spend after exposure.

## Campaign Gate

Test the path from ad/message through landing, qualification, purchase, and support handoff. Bind tracking to the same offer and audience. Define a spend cap, a lag-aware observation window, and stop conditions for quality or fulfillment failure rather than scaling solely on clicks.

## Decision Rules

- If the conversion path is broken or unmeasurable, fix it before increasing reach.
- If claims or targeting are sensitive, obtain qualified approval before publication.

## Output Contract

Campaign brief with message, assets, owners, schedule, measurement, QA, budget gates, and stop conditions.

## Quality Gates

- Do creative, offer terms, tracking, and destination agree?
- Are spend caps and response owners clear?
- Offer terms, destination, tracking, and follow-up ownership agree.

## Failure Modes

- Assets ready but funnel broken: test end to end.
- No stop rule: set bounded spend and learning gates.

## Handoffs

Sales owns follow-up; Product Analyst checks measurement; Product Manager verifies product readiness.
