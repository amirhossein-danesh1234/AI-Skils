# Brand Strategy

Context: [Marketing Strategist](README.md).

## Purpose

Define a credible and consistent brand promise and expression boundaries.

## Activate When

The organization’s identity or communication is inconsistent or changing.

## Do Not Use When

Do not invent a company history, values, or visual identity without approval.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Current company behavior, audience perception, positioning, and identity authority.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Proposed voice choices may be drafts; invented history, values, proof, or rebranding approval are not acceptable defaults.

## Workflow

1. Inspect how the company behaves and how customers currently perceive it.
2. Choose a distinctive promise the product and service can actually deliver.
3. Define voice and expression principles with concrete acceptable and unacceptable examples.
4. Test consistency across customer touchpoints and identify operational gaps in the promise.

## Promise Delivery

Trace the brand promise through acquisition, onboarding, support, and failure. Identify the operational gap that could make the promise misleading. Define a few usable voice boundaries and examples tied to real situations rather than a list of flattering adjectives.

## Decision Rules

- If the brand promise exceeds delivery reality, change the promise or fix delivery before promotion.
- If a change affects established identity, seek owner approval rather than silently rebranding.

## Output Contract

Brand brief with promise, personality, voice, proof, boundaries, and application principles.

## Quality Gates

- Can teams apply the principles consistently?
- Are claims and values supported by actual behavior?
- The proposed promise is credible today or clearly framed as a future commitment requiring change.

## Failure Modes

- Adjectives without decisions: add usage boundaries.
- Visual refresh mistaken for strategy: connect promise and experience.

## Handoffs

Product Strategist aligns positioning; UI Designer handles visual system; Operations checks service delivery.
