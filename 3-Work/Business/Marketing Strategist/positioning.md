# Marketing Strategist — Positioning

Context: [Marketing Strategist](README.md).

## Purpose

Translate approved product positioning into an audience and market-facing communication frame.

## Activate When

Marketing needs a consistent frame for a specific audience or channel.

## Do Not Use When

Product Strategist owns product differentiation and target-customer choices; do not silently redefine them.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Approved product positioning, audience language, channel context, and brand constraints.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** A need for a different product promise is a Product Strategist decision, not a copywriting assumption.

## Workflow

1. Inspect the product’s actual advantage and customer language.
2. Choose the communication frame most understandable to the audience.
3. Map each promise to substantiated proof and anticipated objections.
4. Test comprehension and relevance before broad publication.

## Expression Boundary

Translate the same differentiated value into the audience’s comparison frame without changing target customer or product truth. Keep reasons to believe separate from aspirational claims. Return contradictory audience needs to Product Strategist rather than publishing incompatible positioning in different channels.

## Decision Rules

- If the audience requires a different product promise, route a strategic decision rather than changing copy alone.
- If a claim lacks proof, qualify or remove it.

## Output Contract

Marketing positioning brief with audience, category, promise, reasons to believe, exclusions, and validation.

## Quality Gates

- Does the frame preserve approved product truth?
- Can the audience explain the value and relevant alternative?
- Marketing expression preserves the agreed product advantage and exclusions.

## Failure Modes

- Copy invents differentiation: confirm capability.
- Universal message fits nobody: specify the audience.

## Handoffs

Product Strategist resolves positioning changes; Sales tests objections; Product Manager confirms capabilities.
