# Marketing Analysis

Context: [Marketing Strategist](README.md).

## Purpose

Evaluate marketing outcomes and identify the next allocation or learning decision.

## Activate When

Campaign or channel results need interpretation.

## Do Not Use When

Do not treat attributed conversions as proven incremental impact.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Spend, exposure, outcome definitions, attribution method, and comparable windows.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Missing incrementality evidence limits causal budget recommendations; descriptive attribution can still be reported.

## Workflow

1. Validate tracking, time windows, cost completeness, and conversion definitions.
2. Separate reach, response, qualification, conversion, retention, and contribution.
3. Compare segments and channels while accounting for mix, lag, and overlapping attribution.
4. Recommend scale, revise, stop, or experiment based on uncertainty and incremental economics.

## Attribution Reconciliation

Reconcile platform claims with deduplicated customer outcomes and include unattributed conversions. Account for time-to-convert, repeat customers, organic capture, and cross-channel overlap. Show cost per qualified outcome and contribution alongside ROAS; a revenue ratio can hide unprofitable acquisition.

## Decision Rules

- If tracking changed, repair comparability before interpreting trends.
- If a channel looks efficient only because it captures existing demand, test incrementality before reallocating heavily.

## Output Contract

Performance analysis with normalized metrics, attribution limits, economics, drivers, and action.

## Quality Gates

- Are cost and outcome periods aligned?
- Do recommendations consider downstream quality and cash?
- One conversion is not counted as independently incremental in several channels.

## Failure Modes

- ROAS hides margin and retention: examine contribution.
- Last-click attribution dictates strategy: expose limitations.

## Handoffs

Product Analyst validates behavior; Financial Analyst reconciles economics; Sales explains lead quality.
