# Trend Analysis

Context: [Market Researcher](README.md).

## Purpose

Assess whether an observed change is durable and decision-relevant.

## Activate When

A team is considering action based on a market or technology trend.

## Do Not Use When

Do not extrapolate a short spike or confuse publicity with adoption.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Trend claim, comparable observations over time, and affected decision.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** A short series permits a hypothesis, not a durable extrapolation; forecasts need a causal mechanism.

## Workflow

1. Inspect source continuity, definition changes, and measurement coverage.
2. Separate structural drivers from seasonality, promotions, policy shocks, or reporting changes.
3. Seek independent evidence of adoption, spending, or behavior.
4. Test scenarios for continuation, plateau, and reversal and identify leading signposts.

## Structural Change Test

Check definition breaks, channel expansion, seasonality, incentives, and reporting coverage before growth interpretation. Look for independent spending or behavior evidence. Identify saturation or reversal mechanisms and a signpost that would alter the recommendation before the organization commits irreversibly.

## Decision Rules

- If the underlying measurement changed, do not interpret the break as market growth.
- If adoption lacks an economic mechanism, treat durability as uncertain.

## Output Contract

Trend assessment with baseline, mechanism, competing explanations, persistence signals, and implications.

## Quality Gates

- Is the claimed trend visible across a meaningful period and relevant population?
- Are alternative explanations and lag effects considered?
- The trend survives relevant measurement and composition alternatives or is explicitly bounded.

## Failure Modes

- Media mentions become demand: seek behavior.
- Linear extrapolation ignores saturation: examine limits.

## Handoffs

Business Strategist evaluates strategic response; Product Strategist assesses relevance; Financial Analyst models sensitivity.
