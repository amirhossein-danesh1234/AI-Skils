# Competitor Research

Context: [Market Researcher](README.md).

## Purpose

Build a current, comparable evidence base about relevant customer alternatives.

## Activate When

A decision requires competitor capabilities, pricing, distribution, or positioning facts.

## Do Not Use When

[Competitive-analysis.md](../Business%20Strategist/competitive-analysis.md) interprets strategic implications; do not treat vendor claims as verified outcomes.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Customer alternative set, comparison question, geography, and evidence date.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** An absent website claim means unknown, not absent capability; direct tests require permitted access.

## Workflow

1. Include direct competitors, substitutes, manual options, and non-consumption.
2. Collect official product, pricing, terms, and release information; label claims and independent observations.
3. Normalize tiers, features, customer types, and periods before comparison.
4. Record uncertainty and identify gaps requiring direct testing or customer evidence.

## Claim Provenance

Separate advertised capability, documented restriction, observed behavior, and customer report. Normalize plan tier, contract length, usage limits, and target segment before comparison. Preserve source/version/date so downstream strategy can recheck a claim likely to change.

## Decision Rules

- If a feature is advertised but not verified, label it a company claim.
- If offerings target different segments, avoid a misleading rank.

## Output Contract

Competitor profiles with sources, comparable claims, unknowns, and meaningful differences.

## Quality Gates

- Are all comparisons on compatible scope and current terms?
- Are absent data and genuinely absent capability distinguished?
- A factual comparison does not silently mix vendor claims with independently verified behavior.

## Failure Modes

- No website mention means no feature: mark unknown.
- Single review generalized: retain source limitations.

## Handoffs

Business Strategist assesses response and advantage; Product Strategist evaluates customer relevance.
