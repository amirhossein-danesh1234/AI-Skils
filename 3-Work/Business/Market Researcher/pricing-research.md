# Pricing Research

Context: [Market Researcher](README.md).

## Purpose

Collect evidence about price levels, willingness to pay, and buying trade-offs.

## Activate When

A pricing decision lacks a defensible external evidence base.

## Do Not Use When

[Commercial-pricing.md](../Commercial-Trade%20Specialist/commercial-pricing.md) sets deal economics; research does not determine price alone.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Offer, buyer segment, pricing unit, comparables, and permitted method.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Stated willingness to pay remains hypothetical until behavior supports it; final price needs economics.

## Workflow

1. Inspect list prices, discounts, contract terms, bundles, and total customer cost.
2. Normalize pricing units and compare equivalent customer situations.
3. Use appropriate direct research or experiments while distinguishing stated willingness from actual purchase.
4. Identify reference prices, value drivers, and adoption barriers with uncertainty.

## Comparable Price

Normalize currency/date, tax treatment, tier, contract term, included usage, implementation, and service. Distinguish list from realized prices. Use ethical research or bounded offer tests that preserve truthful terms; do not interpret a leading survey question as a demand curve.

## Decision Rules

- If competitors bundle different scope, do not compare headline price directly.
- If evidence is hypothetical, validate with an ethical real decision before broad pricing changes.

## Output Contract

Pricing evidence with normalized comparisons, behavioral signals, study limits, and test recommendations.

## Quality Gates

- Are currency, tax, period, unit, and contract conditions aligned?
- Are sample and method limitations explicit?
- The resulting price evidence makes differences in scope and buying context visible.

## Failure Modes

- Survey answer treated as demand curve: validate behavior.
- List price assumed realized price: note discount uncertainty.

## Handoffs

Financial Analyst models margin; Marketing tests framing; Sales supplies objections and realized terms.
