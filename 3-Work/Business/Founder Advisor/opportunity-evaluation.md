# Opportunity Evaluation

Context: [Founder Advisor](README.md).

## Purpose

Decide whether a new opportunity deserves any company commitment or further investigation.

## Activate When

A new market, partnership, product idea, or exceptional customer request competes with existing focus.

## Do Not Use When

Market Researcher sizes and validates evidence; Business Strategist develops a chosen economic path; this is the entry gate, not a full strategy.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Opportunity claim, company priorities, reachable customer or partner, next commitment needed, and the resource it would displace.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Market totals or detailed forecasts are not needed to reject a clear mismatch. Missing demand evidence permits a capped learning proposal, not an assumed market.

## Workflow

1. State why the opportunity exists now and what changes for a buyer, partner, or the company. Identify whether the opportunity is inbound noise, a strategic option, or a real obligation.
2. Test fit with the company advantage, distribution, runway, and founder focus. Include do nothing, refer, delegate, partner, and delay.
3. Find the assumption most likely to invalidate pursuit: purchase, access, technical feasibility, regulatory permission, or economics.
4. Compare the cheapest evidence action with its opportunity cost and expiry window. Specify an answer that would cause rejection as well as one that supports continuation.
5. Recommend decline, defer, delegate, bounded discovery, or advancement to strategy with a time/cash/attention cap.

## Decision Rules

- If the only evidence is excitement or a large market, do not recommend a full build.
- If the window expires before meaningful evidence is obtainable, price the irreversible downside explicitly; urgency is not proof of value.

## Output Contract

Opportunity gate memo with fit, alternatives, displacement, weakest assumption, evidence action, cap, and next decision.

## Quality Gates

- The recommendation makes clear whether it funds learning or execution.
- The test can change the pursue decision; it is not a promotional demonstration.

## Failure Modes

- Attractive idea mistaken for a company priority: name displaced commitments.
- Sunk discovery effort creates obligation to build: evaluate the next commitment only.

## Handoffs

Market Researcher verifies critical claims; Business Strategist can supply bounded economic/feasibility input before the entry gate, then develop full strategy within an approved pursuit envelope; Financial Analyst evaluates material downside. Actual founder decides investigation versus investment separately.
