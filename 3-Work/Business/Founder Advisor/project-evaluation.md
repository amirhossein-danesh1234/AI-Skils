# Project Evaluation

Context: [Founder Advisor](README.md).

## Purpose

Evaluate whether a proposed or ongoing project merits the company’s next tranche of resources.

## Activate When

Leadership must approve a project, compare funding requests, or review a major project gate.

## Do Not Use When

Project Manager evaluates delivery feasibility; continue-pivot-stop handles a failed thesis; this skill compares project value against company opportunity cost.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Project outcome, next milestone, remaining cost and benefit, alternatives, dependencies, and company constraints.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Precise financial benefit may be a range for research projects; require a measurable learning outcome and spending cap instead of fictional ROI.

## Workflow

1. Separate original rationale, accepted deliverables, actual results, and the remaining decision. Past spending is context, not a benefit of continuation.
2. Compare the next tranche with cancel, narrow, delay, outsource, or reuse; include closure, customer obligations, and migration costs in the baseline.
3. Test benefit ownership, adoption, dependency availability, and the delivery forecast with the accountable specialists.
4. Evaluate marginal value, downside, cash timing, founder distraction, and opportunity displaced. Distinguish a deliverable milestone from a business-benefit milestone.
5. Recommend conditional approval, re-scope, pause, or reject with evidence gates and a named benefit owner.

## Decision Rules

- If project completion does not establish benefit, require an adoption or outcome gate rather than calling delivery success.
- If a project is mandatory, compare compliant ways to deliver it; do not reject the obligation because ROI is low.

## Output Contract

Project decision brief: next tranche, incremental benefit/cost, alternatives, benefit owner, delivery confidence, obligations, conditions, and review date.

## Quality Gates

- Remaining costs include operating and transition consequences, not just development.
- Funding gates have observable evidence and can genuinely halt the next tranche.

## Failure Modes

- Percentage complete drives funding: assess future value.
- Technical sponsor alone owns business benefits: name the outcome owner.

## Handoffs

Project Manager supplies forecast; Financial Analyst computes incremental economics; Product Manager supplies adoption evidence; executive approves resources.
