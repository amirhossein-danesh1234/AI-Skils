# Resource Allocation

Context: [Founder Advisor](README.md).

## Purpose

Recommend a feasible reallocation of company cash, people, and attention across competing uses.

## Activate When

A resource envelope changes or the present allocation no longer supports company priorities.

## Do Not Use When

Team Manager assigns people within authority; Project Manager schedules delivery; this skill recommends the company-level envelope.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Available and restricted cash, current commitments, resource-specific constraints, and the priority outcomes.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Precise utilization data can be sampled; financing not committed must remain a separate scenario, not available funding.

## Workflow

1. Build a resource ledger with horizon, unrestricted availability, committed obligations, reserves, and flexibility. Distinguish budget approval from cash availability.
2. Map proposed uses to incremental cash timing, skill capacity, founder attention, minimum viable commitment, and exit cost. Include maintenance and integration burdens.
3. Allocate the scarce resource to the highest-value feasible uses; account for complementary resources so a partly funded initiative is not falsely counted as viable.
4. Stress the allocation for delayed receipts, slower hiring, and correlated overruns. Identify the first shortfall and when an action must occur to avoid it.
5. Propose staged transfers with owners, release conditions, and commitments to reduce. Have the executive choose among explicit trade-offs.

## Decision Rules

- If cash is restricted, do not allocate it as operating liquidity.
- If an allocation requires unapproved headcount or assumed financing, mark it conditional and retain a feasible fallback.

## Output Contract

Allocation ledger before/after, constraints, reserve, incremental commitments, displaced work, shortfall dates, and approval conditions.

## Quality Gates

- No resource is double-booked and a proposed transfer has an identified source.
- Essential service and contractual obligations remain funded or are explicitly escalated.

## Failure Modes

- Annual budget hides an early cash gap: model timing.
- Headcount moved on paper: include skill fit, notice, onboarding, and switching cost.

## Handoffs

Financial Analyst validates cash; Team Manager validates people capacity; Founder Advisor strategic-prioritization sets the preference order. Executive owner authorizes transfers.
