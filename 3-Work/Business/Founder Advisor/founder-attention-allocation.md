# Founder Attention Allocation

Context: [Founder Advisor](README.md).

## Purpose

Reduce founder bottlenecks by assigning attention to decisions that require it.

## Activate When

Founder approvals, interruptions, or personally owned tasks constrain company progress.

## Do Not Use When

Personal planning manages an individual schedule; Team Manager designs delegation within company roles.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Recent founder work/decision samples, company priorities, reserved obligations, and possible delegates.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** A short representative sample can replace a detailed time log; private personal information is unnecessary.

## Workflow

1. Classify attention by strategic judgment, scarce relationships, execution, approval, interruption, and avoidable rework. Identify queues waiting on the founder.
2. For each recurring demand, test eliminate, simplify, delegate with limits, batch, automate, outsource, or retain. Distinguish a genuinely founder-only decision from a habit.
3. Define delegated decision thresholds, context required, escalation triggers, and an accountable owner. Include training and review time in the transition cost.
4. Reserve attention for the small set of company decisions whose delay or poor quality is most consequential. Keep slack for genuine exceptions.
5. Trial the allocation and compare decision wait time, rework, and outcomes; reduce monitoring burden once the delegate demonstrates competence.

## Decision Rules

- If delegation transfers responsibility without authority, fix the mandate before transferring work.
- If every delegated decision returns for approval, narrow escalation conditions rather than adding more check-ins.

## Output Contract

Attention allocation with stop/delegate/retain decisions, owner mandates, protected decision windows, transition cost, and outcome checks.

## Quality Gates

- The new plan frees net founder attention after supervision costs.
- Material decisions retain explicit escalation without turning all decisions into exceptions.

## Failure Modes

- Busy calendar presented as impact: measure blocked outcomes.
- Founder withdrawal creates orphaned decisions: confirm acceptance and authority.

## Handoffs

Team Manager formalizes delegation; Operations Manager removes recurring approval friction; actual founder accepts the attention trade-offs.
