# Portfolio Review

Context: [Founder Advisor](README.md).

## Purpose

Review the combined health and opportunity cost of the company’s initiatives.

## Activate When

A periodic leadership review or major constraint change requires cross-company rebalancing.

## Do Not Use When

Product Strategist’s product-portfolio-analysis evaluates product relationships; this includes products, operations, experiments, partnerships, and obligations.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Initiative inventory, commitments, owners, outcomes, resource use, dependencies, and current company constraints.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Unmeasured initiatives stay visible as evidence gaps. Do not substitute traffic-light opinions for cash, capacity, or outcome records.

## Workflow

1. Reconcile the complete initiative inventory including informal founder projects and maintenance obligations. Record lifecycle and the next irreversible commitment.
2. Group by strategic thesis and shared resources; identify duplicated benefits, correlated risk, concentration, and initiatives that only work together.
3. Compare results with the intended outcome and remaining investment, not only spend against budget. Review previously deferred and stopped commitments for hidden continuing costs.
4. Test the portfolio under loss of a customer, specialist, funding source, or supplier. Compare rebalance, consolidate, partner, retire, and maintain options.
5. Return company-level resource moves and the decisions to delegate to product or functional owners, with one review owner per change.

## Decision Rules

- If one initiative appears healthy only because another absorbs its cost, reconcile the shared cost before ranking.
- If initiatives make incompatible assumptions about the same resource or market, resolve the conflict before approving both.

## Output Contract

Portfolio slate with thesis, outcome evidence, remaining commitment, dependencies, concentration, recommended disposition, and resource moves.

## Quality Gates

- Aggregate allocations reconcile to the company envelope and shared benefits are counted once.
- Retirements include customer transition and continuing obligations.

## Failure Modes

- Independent rankings hide portfolio fragility: stress common causes.
- Review creates new work without stopping any: expose displacement.

## Handoffs

Product Strategist assesses product interactions; Financial Analyst reconciles resources; Project and Operations Managers supply delivery and ongoing-service evidence; founder approves.
