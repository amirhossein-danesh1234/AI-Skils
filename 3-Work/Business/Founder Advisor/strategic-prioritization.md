# Founder Advisor — Strategic Prioritization

Context: [Founder Advisor](README.md).

## Purpose

Choose which company commitments deserve scarce attention and resources now.

## Activate When

Competing company initiatives cannot all fit the available cash, founder bandwidth, or execution capacity.

## Do Not Use When

Product Strategist orders product bets within an agreed envelope; Business Strategist develops the economic strategy of a pursued problem.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Current commitments, company objective, cash/attention constraints, and candidates competing for the same resources.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Detailed delivery estimates may be ranges for triage. Obtain implementer estimates and a cash downside before recommending an irreversible commitment.

## Workflow

1. Identify the binding constraint: survival cash, founder decisions, a scarce specialist, distribution access, or learning time. Do not collapse different constraints into one score.
2. Separate contractual or safety obligations from discretionary bets. Include a no-new-commitment baseline and identify work already consuming capacity.
3. For each candidate, compare the next bounded commitment, upside mechanism, downside, time to evidence, reversibility, and what it displaces. Reject double-counted benefits across initiatives.
4. Select a feasible funded set, a deliberately unfunded set, and protected reserve. Stress the set with a common delay or customer loss, not only each initiative independently.
5. Return a recommended order and explicit stop/delay/delegate decisions for the actual founder to approve. Set review triggers tied to evidence or resource changes.

## Decision Rules

- If two attractive bets require the same bottleneck resource, sequence or remove one; do not allocate the same attention twice.
- If preference scores reverse under plausible assumptions, fund a small discriminating test rather than pretending the ranking is robust.
- If a mandatory obligation makes the portfolio infeasible, escalate the trade-off instead of silently starving the obligation.

## Output Contract

Decision slate: fund/maintain/test/delay/stop, next commitment cap, binding resource, displaced work, downside, owner, and revisit trigger.

## Quality Gates

- The funded set fits each separate resource constraint and preserves essential obligations.
- For each funded initiative, a specific unfunded alternative and the cost of waiting are visible.

## Failure Modes

- Everything labeled strategic: require an explicit not-now list.
- Founder enthusiasm dominates the slate: compare evidence against the same constraints.

## Handoffs

Financial Analyst supplies a reconciled cash envelope; Team Manager confirms usable capacity; Business/Product Strategists explain mechanisms. The actual founder approves priorities.
