# Build Buy Partner

Context: [Founder Advisor](README.md).

## Purpose

Choose an ownership model for a capability based on strategic importance and lifecycle burden.

## Activate When

The company could build, buy, outsource, partner, use a manual process, or do without a capability.

## Do Not Use When

Software Architect selects technical fit within the chosen route; procurement and legal own binding contract details.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Capability outcome, differentiation claim, urgency, constraints, internal ability, and viable external routes.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Vendor quotes can initially be ranges; data rights, integration feasibility, exit terms, and real support obligations must be checked before commitment.

## Workflow

1. Separate differentiating capability from commodity support. Identify which knowledge, data, or control must remain inside the company.
2. Compare no action, manual service, internal build, purchase, contracting, and partnership against the same outcome and horizon.
3. Model integration, migration, customization, recurring cost, founder supervision, security review, supplier failure, switching, and exit; include opportunity cost of internal capacity.
4. Test the most uncertain constraint with a bounded proof: real integration, portability export, service quality, or partner incentives. Check dependence on uncommitted roadmap promises.
5. Recommend a route, retained responsibilities, reversible trial, contract conditions, and a trigger to reconsider ownership.

## Decision Rules

- If vendor limits prevent required rights or control, do not compensate with a low price score.
- If a capability is differentiating but internal delivery cannot meet the needed window, compare a temporary external bridge with an explicit exit path.

## Output Contract

Option comparison with total lifecycle cost, strategic control, time to usable value, risks, retained owner, proof results, and commitment gates.

## Quality Gates

- The comparison uses equivalent scope, service level, and time horizon.
- Data export, termination, continuity, and internal ownership are covered for external routes.

## Failure Modes

- Buy assumed integration-free: test the real boundary.
- Build treated as free staff time: account for displaced work and maintenance.

## Handoffs

Software Architect validates fit; AI Engineer evaluates AI behavior when relevant; Commercial-Trade Specialist and legal review supplier terms; Financial Analyst models cost.
