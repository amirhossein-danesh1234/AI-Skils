# Strategic Risk Review

Context: [Founder Advisor](README.md).

## Purpose

Challenge threats that can invalidate company strategy or destroy important options.

## Activate When

A major commitment or change in exposure requires a founder-level risk decision.

## Do Not Use When

Business Strategist handles risks within a pursued business model; specialist risk reviews establish mechanisms and controls.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Company objectives, irreversible commitments, concentrated exposures, loss-bearing capacity, and decision owners.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Numerical probabilities are optional unless defensible. Scenario mechanisms and timing are required for consequential risks.

## Workflow

1. Identify the few paths to company-level failure: liquidity loss, customer or supplier concentration, critical-person dependency, permission to operate, loss of trust, or an irreversible strategic lock-in.
2. Trace each path from trigger through dependencies to impact and time available to respond. Combine correlated risks rather than simply adding independent scores.
3. Assess existing prevention, detection, contingency, and exit options with evidence that each can work before the loss becomes unaffordable.
4. Compare smaller commitments, reserve, diversification, staged learning, partner routes, and avoidance. Price lost upside and carrying cost of optionality.
5. Recommend accept, reduce, defer, or avoid, with trigger, response owner, decision deadline, and executive risk acceptance.

## Decision Rules

- If a credible downside exceeds survival capacity, do not hide it behind low unsubstantiated probability.
- If mitigation depends on future fundraising or unavailable authority, treat it as conditional rather than an active control.

## Output Contract

Strategic risk brief with causal scenarios, concentration, response time, controls, residual exposure, preserved/lost options, and decision request.

## Quality Gates

- The leading signpost arrives early enough for the proposed response.
- Residual exposure is within an explicit owner-approved tolerance, or remains an open gate.

## Failure Modes

- Long risk catalog obscures existential exposure: prioritize failure paths.
- Optionality treated as free: account for reserve and delay costs.

## Handoffs

Financial Analyst tests liquidity; Security, Commercial, Team, and Operations specialists validate their mechanisms. Only actual leadership accepts company-level risk.
