# Competitive Analysis

Context: [Business Strategist](README.md).

## Purpose

Explain competitive forces and alternatives that affect business viability.

## Activate When

A market-entry or strategic decision depends on rivalry and bargaining power.

## Do Not Use When

Market Researcher gathers competitor facts; this skill interprets strategic consequences.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Customer alternatives, market definition, competitor evidence, and value-chain context.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Missing competitor facts remain unknown; strategy interpretation cannot fill factual gaps.

## Workflow

1. Inspect direct rivals, substitutes, internal workarounds, and doing nothing.
2. Compare customer value, distribution, economics, switching, and access to scarce resources.
3. Assess likely competitor responses and buyer or supplier bargaining power.
4. Identify a defensible wedge and evidence that would invalidate it.

## Response Scenario

Model a credible rival response such as discounting, bundling, distribution exclusion, or copying the feature. Test whether the proposed advantage survives and where buyer/supplier power captures the margin. Include manual workarounds and doing nothing rather than comparing only named software rivals.

## Decision Rules

- If a framework such as Five Forces helps, use only relevant forces and state evidence limits.
- If advantage is easily copied, do not treat it as durable differentiation.

## Output Contract

Competitive assessment with alternatives, advantages, constraints, response scenarios, and implications.

## Quality Gates

- Are factual comparisons current and like-for-like?
- Does the conclusion account for customer alternatives outside the category?
- The conclusion identifies a mechanism of advantage or vulnerability, not just a feature rank.

## Failure Modes

- Feature matrix mistaken for strategy: analyze economic consequences.
- Competitors assumed static: model plausible response.

## Handoffs

Market Researcher verifies facts; Product Strategist evaluates value differentiation; Financial Analyst tests margin effects.
