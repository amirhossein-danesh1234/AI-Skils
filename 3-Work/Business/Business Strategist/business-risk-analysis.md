# Business Risk Analysis

Context: [Business Strategist](README.md).

## Purpose

Prioritize threats to business objectives and define proportionate responses.

## Activate When

A strategy or operating decision carries material uncertainty.

## Do Not Use When

Do not turn an exhaustive risk list into a substitute for choosing a strategy.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Pursued business objective, dependencies, exposures, and loss-bearing capacity.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Qualitative likelihood is acceptable with rationale; catastrophic correlated exposure goes to Founder Advisor.

## Workflow

1. Identify failure scenarios across demand, cash, execution, concentration, compliance, and reputation.
2. Distinguish controllable risks, external uncertainty, and assumptions requiring validation.
3. Assess downside timing and correlated exposures rather than scoring risks independently.
4. Choose avoid, reduce, transfer, monitor, or accept responses with decision authority.

## Business Mechanism

Express each risk as cause, event, and economic consequence, with the time available to react. Compare controls against that mechanism and include their operating cost. Separate a material company survival issue from a local commercial risk so acceptance goes to the right owner.

## Decision Rules

- If one scenario threatens survival, examine it even if probability is uncertain.
- If a mitigation costs more than the exposure it reduces, compare avoidance or monitoring.

## Output Contract

Risk register with scenarios, likelihood rationale, impact, triggers, controls, residual risk, and owners.

## Quality Gates

- Are triggers observable and responses feasible?
- Are residual risks accepted by authorized owners?
- Mitigation has a feasible owner, trigger, and evidence that it changes the exposure.

## Failure Modes

- Heatmap without action: specify trigger and owner.
- Risks treated independent: test common causes.

## Handoffs

Financial Analyst assesses cash exposure; Security and Commercial specialists assess domain risks; executive owner accepts residual risk.
