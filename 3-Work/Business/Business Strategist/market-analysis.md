# Market Analysis

Context: [Business Strategist](README.md).

## Purpose

Interpret market structure and dynamics for a business decision.

## Activate When

The business needs to understand where value, demand, and constraints sit.

## Do Not Use When

Market research acquires evidence; market sizing estimates scope; this skill explains strategic meaning.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Defined segment/geography, buying behavior, value chain, and economic evidence.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** A descriptive market overview can start with incomplete data but must not become an investment recommendation.

## Workflow

1. Inspect market definitions and separate customer demand from supplier activity.
2. Map the value chain, buyer decisions, distribution access, and profit concentration.
3. Analyze demand drivers, cyclicality, barriers, and changes in regulation or technology using current sources.
4. Translate findings into options and tests rather than a descriptive industry report.

## Profit-Pool Interpretation

Distinguish industry spending from revenue accessible to the proposed business. Trace bargaining power, distribution access, switching cost, regulation, and cyclicality to the margin mechanism. End with a strategic choice or evidence gap, not a long list of trends.

## Decision Rules

- If definitions conflict across sources, reconcile them before comparing figures.
- If a trend changes supply but not customer willingness to pay, avoid assuming opportunity.

## Output Contract

Market diagnosis with value pools, buying dynamics, constraints, uncertainty, and strategic options.

## Quality Gates

- Are strategic implications traceable to evidence?
- Are geography, period, and segment boundaries explicit?
- Each claimed opportunity connects demand, access, and economic capture.

## Failure Modes

- Large category implies attractive entry: assess access and economics.
- Trend extrapolation ignores constraints: test mechanisms.

## Handoffs

Market Researcher validates evidence; Commercial-Trade Specialist checks trade constraints; Product Strategist assesses customer value.
