# Moat Analysis

Context: [Business Strategist](README.md).

## Purpose

Assess whether an advantage can persist against imitation and substitution.

## Activate When

A company claims defensibility or must choose investments that strengthen it.

## Do Not Use When

Do not label every brand, dataset, or feature a moat.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Specific advantage claim, customer behavior, competing capability, and horizon.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Unverified mechanisms remain hypotheses; possessing data or a brand is not itself defensibility.

## Workflow

1. Identify the claimed mechanism: scale, switching, network effects, access, learning, or another specific cause.
2. Test whether the mechanism actually improves customer choice or economics as the business grows.
3. Estimate imitation, substitution, multi-homing, and regulatory erosion paths.
4. Compare reinforcement investment with simpler alternatives and opportunity cost.

## Erosion Test

Ask how a competent competitor could reproduce, bypass, or neutralize the advantage. For network or data effects, identify the actual feedback loop and its limits, including multi-homing and portability. Compare reinforcement cost with the economic value protected and potential customer harm.

## Decision Rules

- If a dataset is replicable or unusable, do not count it as defensibility.
- If switching friction harms customers without delivering value, assess churn and reputational risk.

## Output Contract

Defensibility assessment with mechanism, evidence, erosion risks, reinforcement options, and limits.

## Quality Gates

- Can the causal mechanism be explained and evidenced?
- Would the advantage survive a well-funded competitor or changed customer behavior?
- The mechanism explains persistence and has observable evidence beyond the moat label.

## Failure Modes

- Moat by label: demand a mechanism.
- Current lead assumed permanent: specify erosion conditions.

## Handoffs

Product Strategist examines customer advantage; Financial Analyst tests economics; legal specialists assess enforceability where material.
