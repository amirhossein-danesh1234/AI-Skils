# Growth Strategy

Context: [Business Strategist](README.md).

## Purpose

Choose a growth mechanism that remains economically and operationally sustainable.

## Activate When

A business wants to expand customers, revenue, markets, or usage.

## Do Not Use When

Marketing chooses channel execution; growth is not automatically acquisition spending.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Current value/retention evidence, economics, growth constraint, and operating capacity.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Channel execution details can follow; speculative retention cannot support a confident scale case.

## Workflow

1. Decompose growth into acquisition, activation, retention, expansion, pricing, and new segments as relevant.
2. Identify the binding constraint and evidence of a repeatable value proposition.
3. Compare mechanisms on payback, learning time, operational load, and strategic fit.
4. Stage investment and test whether incremental growth preserves quality and cash viability.

## Growth Constraint

Decompose demand, acquisition, activation, recurring value, expansion, pricing, and fulfillment. Find the limiting step and compare interventions there before adding top-of-funnel volume. Model the incremental cohort and cash timing, not only average historical margin.

## Decision Rules

- If retention or fulfillment is broken, fix the leak before amplifying acquisition.
- If growth depends on subsidies, model the unsubsidized behavior.

## Output Contract

Growth thesis with mechanism, constraints, experiments, economics, capacity needs, and stop rules.

## Quality Gates

- Are incremental economics distinguished from averages?
- Can the organization support the proposed growth rate?
- The proposed growth mechanism preserves service quality and survives its funding/learning delay.

## Failure Modes

- Revenue growth hides losses: inspect contribution and cash.
- All channels pursued: choose a constrained learning sequence.

## Handoffs

Marketing and Sales execute acquisition; Product Analyst checks behavior; Financial Analyst checks cash; Operations checks capacity.
