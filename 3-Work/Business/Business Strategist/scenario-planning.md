# Scenario Planning

Context: [Business Strategist](README.md).

## Purpose

Prepare strategic choices for distinct plausible external futures.

## Activate When

A long-horizon decision is sensitive to uncertain market or operating conditions.

## Do Not Use When

Financial [scenario-analysis.md](../Financial%20Analyst/scenario-analysis.md) quantifies driver combinations; scenarios are not forecasts with invented probabilities.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Strategic decision, horizon, high-impact uncertainties, and feasible options.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Scenario probabilities are optional and must not be invented; use conditional choices when evidence is weak.

## Workflow

1. Identify uncertainties that materially alter the decision rather than every possible change.
2. Construct internally coherent futures with different demand, competition, regulation, or resource conditions.
3. Test each strategic option across futures for viability and reversibility.
4. Choose robust actions and conditional commitments tied to observable signposts.

## Signpost Timing

Select a few distinct causal futures, not arbitrary optimistic/base/pessimistic percentages. For each option, test viability and the latest point to act. A signpost is useful only if it arrives early enough to execute the response; pre-position an option when lead time is longer.

## Decision Rules

- If all scenarios favor the same option, challenge whether the range is genuinely decision-relevant.
- If a contingent action has long lead time, prepare the option before the trigger arrives.

## Output Contract

Coherent scenarios, implications, robust actions, contingent moves, signposts, and review cadence.

## Quality Gates

- Are scenarios plausible and distinct without being arbitrary extremes?
- Do signposts connect to actual decisions?
- The chosen robust or contingent action changes meaningfully across plausible futures.

## Failure Modes

- Best/base/worst labels without mechanisms: describe causal conditions.
- Scenario treated as prediction: preserve uncertainty.

## Handoffs

Market Researcher validates drivers; Financial Analyst quantifies consequences; executive owner chooses commitments.
