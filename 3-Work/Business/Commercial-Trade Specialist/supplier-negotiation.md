# Supplier Negotiation

Context: [Commercial-Trade Specialist](README.md).

## Purpose

Prepare supplier terms that balance cost, quality, delivery, and cash risk.

## Activate When

A supplier quote or renewal needs negotiation.

## Do Not Use When

Do not negotiate only unit price or commit beyond the authorized mandate.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Required specification, alternatives, total cost, payment/risk limits, and mandate.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Forecast volumes must be labeled; do not trade a speculative volume promise for a binding concession.

## Workflow

1. Inspect cost drivers, supplier incentives, capacity constraints, and credible alternatives.
2. Prioritize quality, lead time, payment, inspection, remedies, and price by business impact.
3. Prepare conditional packages and identify terms requiring legal or financial approval.
4. Record proposed versus accepted terms and reconcile them into the final documents.

## Tradeable Terms

Build packages across price, lead time, payment, inspection, defect remedies, minimum order, and transport. Compare net economics and exposure rather than celebrating a unit-price reduction. Reconcile agreed terms into the actual documents and identify any acceptance still awaiting approval.

## Decision Rules

- If a lower price increases defect, freight, or financing cost, compare total economics.
- If a concession increases prepayment exposure, require a corresponding risk control.

## Output Contract

Negotiation plan with target terms, tradeable variables, limits, conditional concessions, and confirmation steps.

## Quality Gates

- Are agreed terms operationally measurable and within authority?
- Do quote, order, and contract reflect the same understanding?
- A concession does not transfer an unpriced quality, cash, or delivery risk.

## Failure Modes

- Price win hides risk transfer: map obligations.
- Informal promise not documented: confirm critical terms.

## Handoffs

Financial Analyst validates cash terms; Operations checks feasibility; legal counsel reviews enforceability.
