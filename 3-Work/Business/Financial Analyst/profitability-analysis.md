# Profitability Analysis

Context: [Financial Analyst](README.md).

## Purpose

Explain profit and contribution by product, customer, channel, or period.

## Activate When

A business needs to understand where economic value is created or lost.

## Do Not Use When

Do not confuse contribution, gross profit, operating profit, and cash.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Revenue/cost records, period, segment grain, and allocation/recognition basis.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Unclear accounting treatment needs qualified confirmation; present contribution separately from disputed allocations.

## Workflow

1. Reconcile revenue and costs to source records for the same period and scope.
2. Separate variable, fixed, direct, allocated, recurring, and one-off items.
3. Analyze price, volume, mix, cost, returns, and support burden.
4. Test whether conclusions depend on arbitrary allocation and recommend actions on controllable drivers.

## Profit Bridge

Decompose movement into price, volume, mix, returns, cost, and one-off items on a reconciled basis. Show segment contribution before and after shared-cost allocation when the ranking changes. Distinguish avoidable future cost from allocated overhead that remains if a segment closes.

## Decision Rules

- If allocated overhead changes rankings, show contribution before and after allocation.
- If revenue timing differs from cash, explain the distinction.

## Output Contract

Profit bridge with margin definitions, segment contribution, allocation limits, drivers, and decisions.

## Quality Gates

- Do totals reconcile and margin formulas use consistent denominators?
- Are segment comparisons on comparable scope?
- A recommendation does not assume allocated costs disappear merely because revenue is removed.

## Failure Modes

- High revenue implies profitability: inspect full relevant costs.
- Allocation presented as causal cost: disclose basis.

## Handoffs

Business Strategist evaluates choices; Operations validates cost drivers; accountant resolves recognition treatment.
