# Sales Negotiation

Context: [Sales Strategist](README.md).

## Purpose

Prepare and conduct an authorized commercial negotiation around mutual value and limits.

## Activate When

A qualified deal has unresolved price, scope, risk, or terms.

## Do Not Use When

Do not make binding concessions without a mandate or use deceptive pressure.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Buyer interests, alternatives, economic limits, and actual concession mandate.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Unknown approval limits block binding concessions, not preparation of alternatives.

## Workflow

1. Inspect both parties’ interests and alternatives to agreement.
2. Quantify the cost and value of price, scope, timing, payment, and support concessions.
3. Prepare conditional packages rather than one-sided discounts.
4. Record tentative agreements and obtain required approvals before commitment.

## Conditional Package

Evaluate price, volume, payment timing, support, implementation, and risk together. Attach concessions to meaningful reciprocal terms and assess total deal contribution/cash. Record proposed, tentative, approved, and accepted terms distinctly; winning outside a mandate is not a successful negotiation.

## Decision Rules

- If a concession reduces price, seek a justified reciprocal change such as scope, timing, or certainty.
- If terms breach margin, risk, or authority limits, pause or walk away.

## Output Contract

Negotiation plan with tradable variables, conditional concessions, limits, approvals, and agreement record.

## Quality Gates

- Are all concessions within mandate and economically understood?
- Does the final record distinguish agreed from proposed terms?
- The final package is deliverable, economically understood, and within approved authority.

## Failure Modes

- Winning overrides deal quality: test delivery and cash risk.
- Verbal ambiguity persists: confirm terms accurately.

## Handoffs

Financial Analyst checks economics; Commercial-Trade Specialist reviews delivery/payment terms; legal owner reviews obligations.
