# Sales Funnel

Context: [Sales Strategist](README.md).

## Purpose

Measure and improve progression through a defined sales pipeline.

## Activate When

Pipeline conversion, aging, or forecast quality is unclear.

## Do Not Use When

Do not confuse marketing traffic funnels with evidence-based sales opportunity stages.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Opportunity grain, historical transitions, stage meanings, and observation horizon.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** A current snapshot can describe pipeline but cannot establish historical conversion without transition data.

## Workflow

1. Validate stage entry/exit criteria and reconstruct historical progression.
2. Separate leads, qualified opportunities, proposals, and won revenue at consistent grain.
3. Analyze conversion and time in stage by cohort and relevant segment.
4. Investigate bottlenecks and distinguish qualification, offer, process, and data problems.

## Pipeline Cohorts

Track entry cohort, time in stage, reopen/loss treatment, and mature outcomes. Separate stage probability assumptions from observed conversion. Investigate stale deals and repeated proposals that never produce buyer action before interpreting pipeline value as a revenue forecast.

## Decision Rules

- If stage definitions changed, do not compare rates without reconciliation.
- If opportunities remain open indefinitely, define aging and closure rules.

## Output Contract

Funnel analysis with conversion, aging, leakage, segment differences, data issues, and interventions.

## Quality Gates

- Do counts reconcile and reopened or duplicate opportunities have explicit treatment?
- Are forecasts separated from realized outcomes?
- Counts and transitions reconcile without duplicate or indefinitely open opportunities inflating coverage.

## Failure Modes

- Snapshot pipeline implies conversion: use transition history.
- Stale deals inflate coverage: apply evidence-based hygiene.

## Handoffs

[CRM-process.md](crm-process.md) improves data; Marketing explains lead quality; Financial Analyst evaluates revenue timing.
