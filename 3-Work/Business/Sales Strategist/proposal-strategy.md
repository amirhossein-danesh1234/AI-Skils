# Proposal Strategy

Context: [Sales Strategist](README.md).

## Purpose

Prepare a proposal that links customer needs to deliverable value and clear terms.

## Activate When

A qualified opportunity is ready for a formal offer.

## Do Not Use When

Do not invent scope, guarantees, pricing approval, or contractual language authority.

## Required Context

**Needed:** Buyer evaluation criteria, approved capability/scope, commercial mandate, and delivery feasibility.

**Can be deferred or bounded:** Legal wording and unusual terms require qualified owners; a persuasive draft is not an approved contract.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the buyer’s priorities and how proposals will be evaluated.
2. Map each promised outcome to an actual deliverable and evidence.
3. Clarify assumptions, dependencies, acceptance, support, and exclusions.
4. Review economics and approval requirements before presenting the offer.

## Promise Trace

Map each promised outcome to deliverable, dependency, acceptance, price/term, and owner. Check support, onboarding, customer inputs, and exclusions. If a promise needs future product work, obtain an actual scope/delivery decision instead of burying it in confident proposal language.

## Decision Rules

- If delivery depends on customer action, state the dependency and timing explicitly.
- If a requested commitment exceeds authority or capability, escalate rather than obscure it.

## Output Contract

Proposal outline or draft with problem, value, scope, exclusions, proof, commercial terms, risks, and next decision.

## Quality Gates

- Can delivery teams fulfill the proposal as written?
- Are value claims, scope, price, and terms internally consistent?
- Delivery owners can fulfill the proposal as written within the stated conditions.

## Failure Modes

- Persuasive ambiguity becomes delivery dispute: specify boundaries.
- Discount hides weak value: clarify the outcome first.

## Handoffs

Product Manager and Project Manager validate scope and delivery; Commercial and Financial specialists review terms.
