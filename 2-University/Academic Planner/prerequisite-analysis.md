# prerequisite-analysis

[Academic Planner](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Planning Purpose

Trace prerequisite, co-requisite and eligibility chains from authoritative sources.

## Use This Skill When

Eligibility or sequencing depends on prerequisite, co-requisite, standing, grade or permission rules.

## Authority Boundary

Never infer a prerequisite from topic difficulty or historical custom.

## Required Planning Evidence

Exact course identifiers, governing catalog/term, official descriptions/policies, transcript status, transfer/substitution records and target enrollment date.

## Planning Protocol

1. Collect current authoritative wording for the target and each upstream requirement, preserving identifiers, minimum grades, concurrency and permission clauses.
2. Build a directed dependency chain and mark completed, in progress, unmet, conditional, ambiguous and advisor-only nodes.
3. Check timing against result publication, registration deadlines and course availability. Separate content preparation from formal eligibility.
4. Report the earliest verified path, alternative path and precise questions for the institution; never convert likely practice into a rule.

## Eligibility Trace

Each edge must quote or accurately paraphrase an identifiable institutional source with catalog/version and access date. Student preference and planning assumptions may affect the route but cannot create or waive an edge.

## Decision Rules

- Course numbering and topic dependence are not official prerequisites.
- A transfer course or instructor permission counts only after the institution recognizes it.

## Planning Deliverable

Prerequisite graph with source ledger, status per node, timing, ambiguity, feasible paths and escalation questions.

## Verification Gates

- Every formal edge is sourced and directionally correct.
- The path distinguishes readiness advice from enrollment eligibility.

## Planning Failure Modes

- **Inventing a missing edge to make sequence sensible:** label a recommendation, not rule.
- **Ignoring minimum-grade or co-requisite clauses:** parse the complete condition.

## Handoffs

Course Selection uses the verified graph; Graduation Planning finds critical paths; advisors resolve policy ambiguity.
