# semester-planning

[Academic Planner](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Planning Purpose

Build a feasible term around verified obligations, sequencing and student capacity.

## Use This Skill When

An upcoming term needs an academically valid and realistically executable course plan.

## Authority Boundary

Use for an accepted horizon; long-term degree clearance belongs to graduation planning.

## Required Planning Evidence

Verified requirements and offerings, prerequisite status, timetable, course workload ranges, assessment patterns, student goals/preferences, outside capacity and registration deadlines.

## Planning Protocol

1. Set the term objective and constraints, labeling institutional rules, verified curriculum requirements, preferences and planning assumptions.
2. Generate feasible course bundles after prerequisite and timetable screening. Map contact time, travel, labs, deadlines and weekly/peak workload.
3. Compare progression value, coherence, risk and capacity. Create a primary plan plus add/drop or unavailable-course fallback.
4. Define enrollment/advising actions, early warning indicators and a review at syllabus release or before the add/drop deadline.

## Term Feasibility Gate

A semester is valid only when eligibility, timetable, workload, progression and the student’s real-life capacity agree. A recommended schedule is conditional until institutional registration succeeds.

## Decision Rules

- Do not fill to the maximum credit limit merely because policy permits it.
- Preserve buffer when course effort, health, work or commute conditions are uncertain.

## Planning Deliverable

Semester plan with labeled constraints, selected/fallback bundles, weekly/peak load, risks, decision dates and verification tasks.

## Verification Gates

- No known prerequisite or timetable conflict remains hidden.
- The plan includes an executable response to an unavailable or unexpectedly heavy course.

## Planning Failure Modes

- **Planning from course titles only:** inspect official details and assessment structure.
- **Treating preferences as fixed constraints without checking trade-offs:** make ownership explicit.

## Handoffs

Course Selection compares bundles; Workload Analysis quantifies demand; Learning Coach plans execution; university systems confirm enrollment.
