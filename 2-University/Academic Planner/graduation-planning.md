# graduation-planning

[Academic Planner](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Planning Purpose

Map remaining verified requirements into resilient completion paths.

## Use This Skill When

The student needs a multi-term path to complete a program or evaluate delay risk.

## Authority Boundary

Produces a planning audit, not an official degree clearance or guarantee.

## Required Planning Evidence

Program/catalog version, verified degree audit, transcript, substitutions/waivers, requirement groups, prerequisite chains, offering cadence, credit/residency rules and student constraints.

## Planning Protocol

1. Reconcile completed, in-progress, planned and unresolved requirements against the authoritative curriculum version. Do not infer equivalence from titles.
2. Build a dependency graph and identify critical-path courses, seasonal offerings, credit ceilings/floors and administrative milestones.
3. Construct a primary and at least one disruption path for failed/unavailable courses, while testing workload and prerequisite feasibility term by term.
4. Produce a decision/audit list for formal advising and update triggers after grades, catalog changes, approvals or offering changes.

## Completion Audit

Classify each item as institutionally verified satisfied, verified remaining, pending result/approval, or planning assumption. A spreadsheet that sums credits without satisfying categories, residency and prerequisites is not a graduation plan.

## Decision Rules

- Use the student’s governing catalog unless the institution confirms another rule set.
- Never count a waiver, substitution or future offering before formal confirmation.

## Planning Deliverable

Graduation map with requirement ledger, dependency/critical path, term scenarios, risks, advisor questions and evidence dates.

## Verification Gates

- Credit totals reconcile with category-level requirements and no course is counted twice unless rules allow it.
- Each planned term respects known prerequisites and offering constraints.

## Planning Failure Modes

- **Guaranteeing a completion date from tentative offerings:** present conditional paths.
- **Missing administrative requirements:** include applications, thesis, residency or clearance where verified.

## Handoffs

Prerequisite and Semester Planning provide detailed paths; university degree audit/advisor supplies official clearance.
