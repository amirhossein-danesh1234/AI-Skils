# course-selection

[Academic Planner](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Planning Purpose

Choose a coherent course set under verified program rules and student preferences.

## Use This Skill When

The student must choose among offered courses for a specific term or objective.

## Authority Boundary

Use only with a stated catalog/term; preferences and assumptions cannot be presented as eligibility rules.

## Required Planning Evidence

Catalog/version, official requirements, verified offerings/times, prerequisite status, student goals/preferences, workload evidence, alternatives and advising deadlines.

## Planning Protocol

1. Define the selection objective and separate mandatory requirements from preferences such as topic, instructor, schedule or exploration.
2. Verify each candidate’s offering, eligibility, credit role and timetable using current authoritative institutional sources; record unresolved questions.
3. Compare feasible bundles on progression, prerequisite unlocks, workload peaks, learning coherence and student preferences. Include a lighter or alternative path.
4. Recommend a bundle conditionally, naming assumptions, displaced options, registration actions and questions requiring institutional confirmation.

## Rule-to-Preference Matrix

For every inclusion or exclusion label the basis: institutional rule, verified curriculum requirement, student preference or planning assumption. A prerequisite cannot be inferred from course numbering, topic sequence or past offerings.

## Decision Rules

- Hard verified constraints screen options before preference scoring.
- A popular or interesting course does not become required, and a requirement does not become preferred.

## Planning Deliverable

Course-selection memo with candidate bundles, rule evidence, preference trade-offs, workload implications, unresolved items and registration decision points.

## Verification Gates

- Every eligibility and requirement claim has a catalog/version or is explicitly unresolved.
- The selected bundle has no known time collision and passes a workload check.

## Planning Failure Modes

- **Optimizing a single attractive course while breaking progression:** compare bundles.
- **Treating a tentative offering as guaranteed:** preserve a fallback.

## Handoffs

Prerequisite Analysis verifies chains; Workload Analysis tests demand; university advising confirms exceptions and eligibility.
