# academic-workload-analysis

[Academic Planner](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Planning Purpose

Compare course and assessment demand with realistic academic capacity.

## Use This Skill When

A proposed term, assessment period or course combination needs a capacity test.

## Authority Boundary

Use for load feasibility, not for choosing the student’s goals or inventing course effort from credits alone.

## Required Planning Evidence

Course components and credits, contact/lab/commute time, assessment calendar, independent-study estimates with provenance, prior pace, outside obligations and recovery constraints.

## Planning Protocol

1. Separate fixed academic time from estimated preparation, assignment and revision effort. Use ranges when credits or catalog hours do not establish actual demand.
2. Map weekly and peak-period demand, including labs, group dependencies, transitions and overlapping deadlines. Match demanding study to credible focused capacity.
3. Build expected and adverse-but-plausible cases using the student’s evidence. Identify the binding weeks and which course or commitment causes overload.
4. Recommend keep, rebalance, reduce or seek advice, with an intake ceiling and trigger for revisiting the plan.

## Workload Ledger

Track contact time, independent focused work, routine academic work, assessment peaks and recovery separately. A term can fit on average and still fail during clustered deadlines. Credit units are an institutional measure, not automatically hours.

## Decision Rules

- Never double-count the same open hours across courses.
- When demand exceeds credible capacity, reduce or renegotiate before adding productivity tactics.

## Planning Deliverable

Workload brief with evidence labels, weekly ranges, peak map, capacity comparison, overload amount, options and review triggers.

## Verification Gates

- All required components and transition costs are visible.
- Uncertain effort remains a range and the recommendation survives a relevant peak week.

## Planning Failure Modes

- **Using official credits as exact effort:** calibrate with course evidence and student history.
- **Ignoring recovery to make totals fit:** expose the infeasibility.

## Handoffs

Semester Planning selects the term; Learning Coach designs study; Personal Planner reconciles the full life calendar.
