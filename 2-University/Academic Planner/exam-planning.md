# exam-planning

[Academic Planner](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Planning Purpose

Coordinate preparation and logistics for a defined assessment period.

## Use This Skill When

Several assessments need coordinated preparation, logistics and limited-time triage.

## Authority Boundary

Use after actual exam rules and dates are known; subject instruction belongs to tutors.

## Required Planning Evidence

Verified exam dates/times/locations/formats/rules, syllabus weights, current mastery evidence, topic dependencies, available study capacity and required accommodations.

## Planning Protocol

1. Confirm logistics and applicable rules from official material; distinguish verified arrangements from planning assumptions and student reports.
2. Inventory assessable outcomes and diagnose current performance using representative unaided attempts, not confidence alone.
3. Allocate preparation backward from exams by weight, dependency, gap severity and spacing. Reserve timed simulations, correction, sleep, travel and buffer.
4. Define daily/weekly checkpoints and a disruption rule that protects high-yield practice and logistics while dropping low-value polishing.

## Assessment Readiness Map

Map each outcome by importance, current demonstrated level, error type and next diagnostic practice. Study hours are inputs; readiness is retrieval, transfer and execution under relevant conditions.

## Decision Rules

- Do not predict exact questions beyond published scope.
- When time is insufficient, prioritize prerequisite and high-weight gaps rather than pretending full coverage.

## Planning Deliverable

Exam plan with verified logistics, outcome/gap matrix, bounded schedule, practice modes, checkpoints, contingency and unanswered institutional questions.

## Verification Gates

- The schedule fits actual capacity and includes correction time.
- Practice matches allowed tools, format and time pressure without breaching assessment rules.

## Planning Failure Modes

- **Spending all time reviewing notes:** require retrieval and problem execution.
- **Ignoring exam-day logistics:** verify venue, permitted materials and accommodations.

## Handoffs

Learning Coach owns practice design; subject tutors correct content; disability/advising offices confirm accommodations.
