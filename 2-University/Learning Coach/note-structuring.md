# note-structuring

[Learning Coach](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Learning Aim

Create notes that support understanding, retrieval and problem solving.

## Activate When

Existing or new notes must support comprehension, retrieval and later problem solving.

## Coaching Boundary

Do not optimize appearance, transcription volume or exhaustive capture.

## Learner Evidence

Purpose/use cases, source/provenance, topic structure, learner’s own explanations, common confusions, examples and expected retrieval tasks.

## Coaching Loop

1. Decide what the notes must enable: explain, compare, derive, solve, locate evidence or prepare recall.
2. Organize around questions, definitions, relationships, assumptions and worked reasoning rather than source order alone.
3. Separate source claims from learner paraphrase; attach provenance and mark unresolved confusion. Compress examples to decision-relevant steps and checks.
4. Add retrieval prompts, links to prerequisites and a small index. Test whether the learner can reconstruct reasoning without rereading everything.

## Generative Notes

Use concise self-explanation, contrast tables, concept maps or worked-example annotations only when each reveals structure. A concept map must label relationships; decorative arrows are not understanding.

## Durable Learning Rules

- Do not transcribe material already easy to retrieve.
- Keep exact quotes and external claims linked to their source.

## Learning Plan Output

Note set or structure with guiding questions, concepts/relations, worked reasoning, misconceptions, provenance and recall prompts.

## Mastery Checks

- A future learner can tell fact, inference, example and uncertainty apart.
- The structure supports at least one realistic retrieval or problem-solving task.

## Coaching Failure Modes

- **Formatting consumes the session:** use a minimal structure.
- **Summaries omit assumptions and conditions:** preserve what changes validity.

## Handoffs

Subject tutors correct the content; Citation Management handles formal references; PKM belongs to Personal Systems.
