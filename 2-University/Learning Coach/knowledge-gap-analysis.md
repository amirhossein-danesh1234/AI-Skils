# knowledge-gap-analysis

[Learning Coach](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Learning Aim

Diagnose missing knowledge or procedure from observed attempts and error patterns.

## Activate When

The learner is stuck, repeatedly wrong or unsure what prerequisite is missing.

## Coaching Boundary

Do not infer ability, motivation or diagnosis from one mistake.

## Learner Evidence

Objective, attempted work with reasoning, answer/feedback, prerequisite map, error history and conditions of the attempt.

## Coaching Loop

1. Ask the learner to reproduce the point of failure and preserve their intermediate reasoning rather than viewing only the final answer.
2. Classify the first causal gap: missing fact/definition, concept relation, representation, procedure, strategy selection, execution or monitoring.
3. Test the hypothesis with a discriminating prompt or near/far example; do not infer a broad deficit from a single failure.
4. Specify the smallest remediation and a transfer check. Reassess after practice and update the dependency map.

## First Broken Link

Trace backward only until the earliest missing prerequisite that explains the observed failure. Later mistakes may be consequences. Confidence, speed and correctness are separate signals.

## Durable Learning Rules

- Use multiple observations before calling a pattern stable.
- If the learner succeeds with prompts but not independently, cue dependence remains a gap.

## Learning Plan Output

Gap diagnosis with evidence, confidence, prerequisite link, remediation, diagnostic exercise and mastery/transfer criterion.

## Mastery Checks

- The diagnosis explains the observed error and predicts a testable follow-up.
- It avoids labels about intelligence, motivation or health.

## Coaching Failure Modes

- **Correcting the final step only:** find the first causal gap.
- **Teaching an entire chapter for one missing relation:** narrow the intervention.

## Handoffs

Subject tutors provide remediation; Practice Design supplies discriminating tasks; appropriate support handles persistent wellbeing concerns.
