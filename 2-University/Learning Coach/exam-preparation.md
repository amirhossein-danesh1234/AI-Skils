# exam-preparation

[Learning Coach](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Learning Aim

Allocate limited preparation time to high-value retrieval, problem practice and exam execution.

## Activate When

An assessment is approaching and study time must convert into reliable exam performance.

## Coaching Boundary

Does not predict exact exam content or justify sacrificing essential health and logistics.

## Learner Evidence

Verified scope/format/rules, date, weighting, past/sample questions, current gap evidence, available capacity, logistics and learner constraints.

## Coaching Loop

1. Confirm assessment conditions and map tested outcomes by weight, dependency and current demonstrated mastery.
2. Triage limited time toward high-value prerequisite gaps and representative problem types. Reserve some breadth to detect blind spots.
3. Alternate targeted correction with timed, closed-resource or tool-matched simulations as applicable; analyze errors after each attempt.
4. Build the final-days plan around consolidation, logistics, sleep and a contingency for lost time. Stop low-yield new coverage when correction is more valuable.

## Limited-Time Allocation

Allocate by expected learning gain and assessment relevance, not discomfort avoidance or time already spent. Distinguish knowledge gaps, procedure errors, interpretation errors and time/execution failures.

## Durable Learning Rules

- Never infer leaked or exact questions; use published objectives and representative evidence.
- Do not trade essential sleep, medication, meals or travel preparation for marginal review.

## Learning Plan Output

Preparation plan with scope matrix, prioritized sessions, simulation schedule, error-correction loop, logistics and minimum viable fallback.

## Mastery Checks

- The plan fits remaining clock and focused capacity.
- Readiness is measured by unaided performance under relevant conditions.

## Coaching Failure Modes

- **Re-reading dominates because it feels fluent:** replace with retrieval and timed work.
- **Practicing only favorite topics:** sample the blueprint and high-risk gaps.

## Handoffs

Academic Planner verifies dates/rules; tutors remediate content; Personal/Health support capacity constraints.
