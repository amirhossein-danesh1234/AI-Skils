# revision-planning

[Learning Coach](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Learning Aim

Schedule revisits based on forgetting, assessment timing and demonstrated mastery.

## Activate When

Previously learned material needs timed revisits before an assessment or long-term retention goal.

## Coaching Boundary

A fixed spacing calendar must yield to errors, dependencies and limited capacity.

## Learner Evidence

Objectives, exam/use dates, prior retrieval scores, error types, prerequisite dependencies, available capacity and existing review queue.

## Coaching Loop

1. Audit each objective with a brief unaided retrieval or representative problem; do not schedule solely from last-read date.
2. Prioritize weak, high-value and prerequisite items while preserving occasional strong-item checks. Assign review mode to the error type.
3. Space successful retrieval farther apart and shorten intervals after failure, with interleaving once methods can be discriminated.
4. Fit reviews to capacity, cap backlog growth and define what is dropped, merged or escalated when the queue becomes infeasible.

## Adaptive Review Queue

The next review date follows evidence and stakes, not a universal spacing formula. Retrieval success with high cueing does not justify a long interval; delayed transfer is stronger evidence.

## Durable Learning Rules

- Correct errors before spacing them.
- Do not let low-value easy items crowd out weak prerequisites or problem practice.

## Learning Plan Output

Revision schedule with objective status, next review/mode, capacity cap, error remediation, interleaving and readiness checkpoints.

## Mastery Checks

- Review intervals trace to observed performance and assessment timing.
- The queue remains feasible after a missed session through an explicit triage rule.

## Coaching Failure Modes

- **Rigid calendar survives contrary evidence:** adapt intervals.
- **Backlog guilt causes mass review:** triage by value and prerequisite.

## Handoffs

Active Recall supplies retrieval tasks; Exam Preparation sets assessment priorities; Learning Plan changes the broader sequence.
