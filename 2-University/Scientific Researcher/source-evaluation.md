# source-evaluation

[Scientific Researcher](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Research Aim

Assess a source’s provenance, methods, relevance, freshness and claim support.

## Use This Protocol When

A source may support an academic claim and its credibility, currency and relevance must be assessed.

## Evidence Boundary

Authority and citation count are signals, not substitutes for reading the evidence.

## Required Research Record

Full source/record, target claim and context, publication/version/date, provenance, methods/data access, citations/retractions/corrections and comparison sources.

## Research Protocol

1. Verify identity and provenance through authoritative bibliographic or publisher records; check version, date, authorship, corrections/retractions and source type.
2. Read the source at the evidence location, identifying its question, design, data, analysis and exact claim rather than relying on snippets or citations.
3. Assess relevance/directness, methodological quality, bias, uncertainty, freshness and independence. Compare with primary or authoritative sources.
4. Assign a bounded use: central evidence, contextual background, method source, lead only or exclude; record citation and limitations.

## Provenance Ladder

Prefer primary evidence for empirical claims and current authoritative sources for standards, definitions or changing facts. Secondary sources can synthesize but add source distance. Preprints may be valuable and must remain labeled unreviewed.

## Evidence Rules

- A reputable venue does not make every claim correct.
- Do not cite a source for a claim it mentions but does not actually support.

## Research Output

Source appraisal with verified identity, source type/version, claim-evidence fit, method/quality, currency, conflicts/corrections, use decision and citation data.

## Provenance and Quality Gates

- Bibliographic details are verified rather than guessed.
- The final claim strength does not exceed the source’s design and directness.

## Research Failure Modes

- **Citation count/authority becomes quality score:** inspect methods.
- **Review cited instead of original study when central details matter:** trace primary source.

## Handoffs

Literature Review manages search; Paper Analysis performs deep reading; Citation Management stores verified metadata.
