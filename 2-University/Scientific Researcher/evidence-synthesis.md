# evidence-synthesis

[Scientific Researcher](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Research Aim

Combine research evidence while preserving heterogeneity, bias and uncertainty.

## Use This Protocol When

Multiple studies or sources must be combined into a claim with calibrated confidence.

## Evidence Boundary

Do not pool incompatible outcomes or turn a majority of papers into certainty.

## Required Research Record

Focused question, included source records/full texts, extracted populations/exposures/interventions/outcomes, methods, effect estimates/uncertainty, quality judgments and scope.

## Research Protocol

1. Group evidence by genuinely comparable question, design, population, exposure/intervention, comparator, outcome and time horizon.
2. Build an extraction table and assess directness, bias, precision, consistency and independence; detect shared datasets or duplicated cohorts.
3. Synthesize quantitatively only when measures and assumptions permit; otherwise use structured direction/magnitude and mechanism-aware comparison.
4. Explain heterogeneity, publication/reporting limits and residual uncertainty; state which findings are robust, conditional, conflicting or absent.

## Claim–Evidence Matrix

For each conclusion link supporting and contradicting sources, design strengths, limitations and applicability. Count independence and quality, not papers. A pooled estimate inherits study and synthesis assumptions.

## Evidence Rules

- Do not convert “no significant effect” into evidence of no effect without precision/power context.
- Keep causal, predictive, mechanistic and descriptive claims aligned with study designs.

## Research Output

Synthesis with question/scope, extraction matrix, quality assessment, convergence/heterogeneity, calibrated conclusions, limitations and research implications.

## Provenance and Quality Gates

- Every substantive claim traces to real included evidence.
- Disagreement is explained or preserved rather than averaged away rhetorically.

## Research Failure Modes

- **Vote counting by positive/negative papers:** examine effects and uncertainty.
- **Dominant large study hides bias or population mismatch:** assess directness and design.

## Handoffs

Source Evaluation appraises individual records; Literature Review documents search; Academic Writer communicates conclusions.
