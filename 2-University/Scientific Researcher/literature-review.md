# literature-review

[Scientific Researcher](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Research Aim

Conduct a reproducible scoped search and synthesize the relevant literature.

## Use This Protocol When

A question needs a transparent search, selection and synthesis of current relevant research.

## Evidence Boundary

A narrative from remembered or convenient sources is not a literature review.

## Required Research Record

Question/scope, discipline, databases/search access, date/language limits, inclusion/exclusion criteria, prior key works and intended review type.

## Research Protocol

1. Operationalize the question into concepts, synonyms and controlled terms; prerecord scope, databases, dates and eligibility criteria proportionate to the review.
2. Search authoritative indexes and primary literature, record exact queries/dates, deduplicate and extend through backward/forward citation tracing.
3. Screen titles/abstracts then full texts against criteria, logging exclusion reasons and unavailable evidence. Never invent metadata or citations.
4. Extract comparable evidence, evaluate quality and synthesize themes/findings, disagreement, gaps and search limitations; update time-sensitive claims near delivery.

## Search Provenance

A reproducible search record contains database/platform, exact query, filters, search date, result count and screening disposition. Prefer primary studies and current authoritative literature; use reviews to orient and citation-trace, not to conceal source distance.

## Evidence Rules

- Claim comprehensiveness only when search breadth and screening support it.
- A missing full text remains unavailable, not safely summarized from title/abstract.

## Research Output

Review package with protocol/scope, search log, flow/counts, included-study table, quality appraisal, synthesis, limitations and verified bibliography.

## Provenance and Quality Gates

- All citations resolve to real records and cited claims were checked in the source.
- Search limits, date coverage and exclusion decisions are explicit.

## Research Failure Modes

- **Convenience sources define the field:** broaden authoritative databases and citations.
- **Search query revised repeatedly without record:** preserve versions and reasons.

## Handoffs

Research Question defines scope; Evidence Synthesis integrates findings; Citation Management maintains references.
