# research-gap-analysis

[Scientific Researcher](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Research Aim

Identify a defensible unresolved question from mapped evidence and feasibility.

## Use This Protocol When

A mapped literature needs a defensible next question rather than a generic “more research” statement.

## Evidence Boundary

Novelty cannot be claimed from a limited search or merely because studies disagree.

## Required Research Record

Review scope/search limits, evidence map, disagreements, populations/settings/measures, methods, uncertainty, feasibility and intended contribution.

## Research Protocol

1. Inventory what is well supported, contested, untested or weakly measured within the actual searched scope.
2. Classify candidate gaps: evidence, population/context, mechanism, measurement, method, theory integration, replication or implementation.
3. Test each candidate against citation tracing and recent primary/current authoritative literature; distinguish true absence from search or access limitation.
4. Rank by importance, answerability, novelty confidence, ethics/resources and how an answer would change theory or practice; formulate a next question.

## Gap Claim Standard

A defensible gap states searched scope/date, what evidence exists, precisely what remains unresolved, why resolution matters and what study could address it. Contradictory results may indicate heterogeneity rather than an empty gap.

## Evidence Rules

- Do not claim novelty from a single database or memory.
- A feasible project is not automatically an important research gap.

## Research Output

Gap analysis with evidence map, candidate gaps, search/novelty confidence, prioritization, proposed question/method direction and limitations.

## Provenance and Quality Gates

- Each gap claim is supported by verified literature and bounded to search coverage.
- The selected gap is ethically and methodologically answerable or states required development.

## Research Failure Modes

- **“Few studies” used without count/scope:** document evidence.
- **Minor variable substitution presented as contribution:** articulate decision/theory value.

## Handoffs

Literature Review improves coverage; Research Question scopes the candidate; Methodology Design tests answerability.
