# paper-analysis

[Scientific Researcher](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Research Aim

Read a paper from claim through methods, results and applicability.

## Use This Protocol When

A difficult paper must be understood and critically appraised rather than merely summarized.

## Evidence Boundary

A paper’s abstract, prestige or statistical significance is not enough to establish validity.

## Required Research Record

Full paper and supplements where available, citation details, reader’s question/prerequisites, disciplinary context and relevant comparison literature.

## Research Protocol

1. Orient from title/abstract/figures to the research question and claimed contribution, then define unfamiliar terms without assuming claims are true.
2. Reconstruct design, sample/data, variables, method and analysis. For equations/algorithms, map symbols and work through a minimal example or derivation.
3. Trace each central claim to specific results/figures/tables and distinguish author interpretation from direct evidence.
4. Assess bias, uncertainty, robustness, replicability and applicability; follow critical methods/data/predecessor citations when the claim depends on them.

## Claim Audit

For each major claim record evidence location, design support, effect/uncertainty, assumptions, alternative explanations and scope. Read figures with axes, denominators and uncertainty; do not let the abstract override the body.

## Evidence Rules

- Statistical significance alone does not establish importance, causality or replication.
- If a method cannot be understood from available material, state the limitation rather than filling gaps.

## Research Output

Paper brief with question, contribution, method map, claim-evidence table, key figures/results, assumptions, strengths, limitations, applicability and follow-up sources.

## Provenance and Quality Gates

- Quoted/paraphrased claims match the paper and location.
- The review separates comprehension, validity and relevance judgments.

## Research Failure Modes

- **Abstract-only appraisal:** inspect methods/results/supplement.
- **Prestige or citations substitute for evidence:** evaluate the actual study.

## Handoffs

Source Evaluation appraises provenance; tutors unpack technical content; Evidence Synthesis places the paper in the field.
