# methodology-design

[Scientific Researcher](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Research Aim

Design a study whose data and analysis can answer the research question.

## Use This Protocol When

A research question needs a study design capable of producing interpretable evidence.

## Evidence Boundary

Does not replace ethics, safety, preregistration or specialist statistical review when required.

## Required Research Record

Research question/hypothesis, target population/system, constructs/outcomes, causal or descriptive aim, prior evidence, resources, feasibility, ethics/safety and analysis needs.

## Research Protocol

1. Translate the question into estimand or target inference, units of analysis, variables and temporal ordering; identify plausible alternative explanations.
2. Compare designs and sampling strategies for identification, bias, feasibility and generalizability. Define controls/comparators, randomization/blinding where relevant.
3. Operationalize measures, data collection, quality controls, missing-data handling and a priori analysis with effect sizes and uncertainty—not significance alone.
4. Plan pilot, power/precision rationale, reproducibility artifacts, deviations and validation; route required ethics, safety and data approvals before execution.

## Inference Chain

Trace question → design → measurement → data → analysis → claim. A strong statistical procedure cannot recover an unmeasured construct or unidentified causal effect. Mark exploratory versus confirmatory components.

## Evidence Rules

- Do not choose sample size from convention alone; tie it to precision/power and feasibility.
- Predefine primary outcomes and exclusion rules when selective flexibility would bias inference.

## Research Output

Method plan with estimand, design rationale, sampling, variables/measures, procedures, bias controls, analysis, uncertainty, ethics, reproducibility and limitations.

## Provenance and Quality Gates

- The planned data can discriminate the target claim from key alternatives.
- Every major source of bias has prevention, measurement, sensitivity analysis or an explicit limitation.

## Research Failure Modes

- **Method chosen because familiar:** compare fit to inference.
- **Pilot data silently used as confirmatory:** label and separate.

## Handoffs

Research Question sets target; Source Evaluation supports instruments; institutional ethics/statistical experts approve high-consequence designs.
