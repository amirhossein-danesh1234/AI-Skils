# concept-explanation

[Mathematics Tutor](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Mathematical Goal

Explain a mathematical concept through definitions, representations and examples.

## Use This For

A learner needs the meaning, structure and use of a mathematical definition or theory.

## Validity Boundary

Intuition and examples illuminate a definition but do not establish a universal theorem.

## Definitions and Preconditions

Target concept, learner prerequisites, formal definitions/conventions, context or application, known confusion and desired rigor.

## Reasoning Protocol

1. State the formal definition with domain and quantifiers, then unpack each clause in plain language.
2. Give canonical and boundary examples plus a near-miss/non-example that isolates why each condition matters.
3. Connect algebraic, geometric, graphical, probabilistic or computational representations where valid, without conflating them.
4. Ask the learner to classify or construct a new case and explain the definition; then connect to theorems whose hypotheses use it.

## Four Evidence Modes

Label statements as intuition, formal definition/theorem, heuristic or numerical illustration. Intuition motivates; examples refute universal claims but do not prove them; numerical plots reveal patterns but may hide exceptional cases.

## Validity Rules

- Introduce only prerequisite notation needed for the concept.
- When conventions differ, state the convention and consequences explicitly.

## Mathematical Output

Concept lesson with definition, clause analysis, representations, examples/non-examples, common confusion, theorem links and transfer question.

## Independent Checks

- No example violates the stated domain or convention.
- The learner can use the definition on an unfamiliar case without relying on visual resemblance.

## Logical Failure Modes

- **Metaphor replaces definition:** return to quantifiers and conditions.
- **Only friendly examples shown:** include a boundary or counterexample.

## Handoffs

Proof Analysis establishes claims; Problem Solving applies them; Physics Tutor supplies physical interpretation when relevant.
