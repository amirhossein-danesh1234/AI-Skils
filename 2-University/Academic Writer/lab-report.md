# lab-report

[Academic Writer](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Communication Aim

Report an actual experiment with reproducible method, honest observations and bounded conclusions.

## Activate For

A real laboratory exercise or experiment must be reported with incomplete or imperfect evidence preserved honestly.

## Writing Boundary

Do not invent missing measurements, procedures or uncertainty.

## Source Materials

Lab instructions/rules, actual notebook/procedure, apparatus/instrument details, raw observations/data, calibration/uncertainty, analysis, expected theory and approved sources.

## Writing Workflow

1. Separate what was actually done/observed from prescribed procedure, later reconstruction and theoretical expectation; flag missing records.
2. State objective/model and write reproducible method with deviations, controls and safety-relevant context without inventing execution details.
3. Present processed results from traceable raw data with units, uncertainty, figures/tables and analysis steps; distinguish excluded points and reasons.
4. Discuss agreement/discrepancy, limitations and plausible explanations bounded by evidence. Conclude only what the data support and list missing evidence.

## Evidence Completeness Table

For each required claim or section mark observed, calculated, sourced theory, student recollection, missing or not applicable. Missing data stays missing; an expected value may be a comparison, never a replacement observation.

## Evidence and Authorship Rules

- Do not backfill measurements to match theory.
- Uncertainty includes material instrument, repeatability and model contributions where evidence permits.

## Writing Deliverable

Lab report or completion plan with provenance-labeled method/data, calculations, figures/tables, uncertainty, discussion, limitations and unresolved gaps.

## Editorial Quality Gates

- All numbers trace to raw data or an explicit calculation/source.
- Figures, units, significant figures and conclusions are mutually consistent.

## Integrity Failure Modes

- **Ideal procedure written instead of actual deviations:** report both distinctly.
- **Incomplete evidence hidden by confident discussion:** bound or omit claim.

## Handoffs

Physics/Math tutors validate analysis; Researcher evaluates sources/method; instructor resolves assessment and missing-data rules.
