# paper-structure

[Academic Writer](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Communication Aim

Design a paper architecture that connects question, methods, evidence and conclusions.

## Activate For

A paper’s argument and evidence need a coherent section architecture before or during drafting.

## Writing Boundary

Use after contribution and evidence are known; section conventions do not supply an argument.

## Source Materials

Research question/contribution, actual methods/data/results, audience/venue conventions, evidence/claim inventory, limitations and length constraints.

## Writing Workflow

1. Write the paper’s central question, contribution and bounded conclusion; map each claim to evidence and each method to the result it enables.
2. Choose a discipline/venue-appropriate macrostructure and define the job and transition of each section before drafting prose.
3. Order results by argument or research question, not chronology of work; place methods/detail where reproducibility and reader flow require.
4. Audit forward/backward coherence among title, abstract, introduction, methods, results, discussion and limitations; cut sections without a distinct job.

## Argument Spine

The introduction establishes question and gap; methods establish how evidence was produced; results report evidence; discussion interprets within limitations. Adapt conventions, but do not collapse evidence and interpretation invisibly.

## Evidence and Authorship Rules

- No section may promise an analysis or contribution absent later.
- Figures/tables belong where they answer a defined question, with prose explaining the inference.

## Writing Deliverable

Paper outline with central claim, section purposes, claim-evidence map, figure/table plan, transitions, word budget and missing-evidence flags.

## Editorial Quality Gates

- Every major claim has an evidentiary destination and limitations have a home.
- Abstract and conclusion can be reconciled with the planned body without new claims.

## Integrity Failure Modes

- **Generic IMRaD headings used without argument:** assign section jobs.
- **Literature review becomes disconnected catalog:** organize around the question.

## Handoffs

Scientific Writing drafts claims; Researcher supplies evidence; Abstract Writing compresses the completed work.
