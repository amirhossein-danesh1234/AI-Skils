# abstract-writing

[Academic Writer](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Communication Aim

Write a compact faithful account of the actual question, method, results and limitations.

## Activate For

A completed or sufficiently evidenced academic work needs a concise standalone abstract.

## Writing Boundary

Do not introduce claims, numbers or certainty absent from the underlying work.

## Source Materials

Actual manuscript/report, verified question/objective, design/method, key results with uncertainty, conclusion/limitations, audience and word/format rules.

## Writing Workflow

1. Extract only supported objective, methods, principal results and bounded conclusion from the source document.
2. Choose structured or unstructured form per venue and allocate words by informational value, prioritizing specific results over background.
3. Write exact population/system, method and key quantitative/qualitative finding with uncertainty where material; align causal language with design.
4. Check every statement against the body, remove citations/abbreviations unless allowed, meet word limit and preserve a central limitation.

## Compression Test

An abstract is a faithful miniature, not promotional copy. It should let a reader identify what was done, what was found and how far the conclusion travels. Omit unsupported novelty and future promises.

## Evidence and Authorship Rules

- Do not add numbers or conclusions absent from the body.
- If results are incomplete, label the abstract as protocol/preliminary or state that a results abstract cannot yet be written.

## Writing Deliverable

Abstract meeting format/word rules with objective, method, key result/uncertainty, bounded conclusion and consistency notes.

## Editorial Quality Gates

- All claims, sample sizes and numbers reconcile with the manuscript.
- The conclusion is no stronger than design, evidence and limitations.

## Integrity Failure Modes

- **Background consumes the word budget:** preserve methods/results.
- **“Significant” used without magnitude/context:** report informative result.

## Handoffs

Paper Structure ensures the body supports it; Technical Editing polishes; Citation Management handles rare required references.
