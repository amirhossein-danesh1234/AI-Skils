# technical-editing

[Academic Writer](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Communication Aim

Improve academic clarity and consistency without changing meaning or evidence.

## Activate For

An academic draft needs clarity, consistency and correctness review without unauthorized substantive rewriting.

## Writing Boundary

Do not silently alter technical claims, numbers, citations or author voice.

## Source Materials

Draft, audience/venue/style guide, author intent, verified data/sources, terminology/notation list and permitted edit depth.

## Writing Workflow

1. Establish edit scope and protect facts, equations, citations, quotations, authorship and disciplinary meaning.
2. Edit macro clarity first—argument, section purpose, paragraph order—then sentence logic, ambiguity, terminology, grammar and concision.
3. Cross-check numbers, units, symbols, abbreviations, figure/table/equation references and claim strength throughout the document.
4. Return tracked or clearly itemized substantive queries where meaning/evidence is uncertain; perform a final consistency and reference pass.

## Edit Classification

Separate mechanical edit, clarity edit and substantive query. Never resolve a factual contradiction by choosing the smoother sentence. Preserve author voice unless a requested style requires change.

## Evidence and Authorship Rules

- Do not silently change numerical precision, statistical meaning or causal strength.
- Explain high-impact edits so the author can accept or reject them.

## Writing Deliverable

Edited draft plus substantive query list, consistency sheet and summary of structural/technical changes without invented content.

## Editorial Quality Gates

- All edits preserve or explicitly query original meaning.
- Cross-references, notation, units and citations are internally consistent.

## Integrity Failure Modes

- **Line editing before structural issue:** fix hierarchy first.
- **Fluent replacement changes scientific claim:** revert and query.

## Handoffs

Subject tutors/researcher resolve technical questions; Citation Management repairs references; LaTeX Writing implements source changes.
