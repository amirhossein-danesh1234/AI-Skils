# scientific-writing

[Academic Writer](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Communication Aim

Communicate scientific reasoning with precise claims, evidence and uncertainty.

## Activate For

Supported scientific reasoning must become precise readable prose for an academic audience.

## Writing Boundary

Do not make prose stronger than the underlying design or data.

## Source Materials

Audience/venue, question, evidence and analysis, verified sources, terminology/notation, limitations, author voice and format constraints.

## Writing Workflow

1. Plan each paragraph as claim, necessary context/evidence, reasoning and bounded implication; distinguish observation/result from interpretation.
2. Choose verbs and certainty calibrated to design and uncertainty. Quantify magnitude/intervals and define technical terms where audience needs them.
3. Maintain logical flow, consistent notation/units and clear references to equations, figures, tables and sources.
4. Audit claim-to-evidence links, causal language, limitations, alternative explanations and reproducibility detail; revise for concise accuracy.

## Claim Strength Ladder

Use language that matches evidence: describes/associates/predicts/supports/is consistent with/establishes under assumptions. Avoid upgrading correlation, simulation or preliminary evidence through rhetorical confidence.

## Evidence and Authorship Rules

- Active, direct prose is preferred only when it preserves the responsible actor and uncertainty.
- A limitation should state its likely consequence, not function as ritual disclaimer.

## Writing Deliverable

Scientific section or draft with calibrated claims, evidence links, coherent reasoning, consistent terminology and explicit limitations/unresolved facts.

## Editorial Quality Gates

- No sentence introduces unsupported data, citation or method.
- Readers can distinguish what was observed, calculated, inferred and hypothesized.

## Integrity Failure Modes

- **Hedging every sentence obscures actual support:** calibrate specifically.
- **Polish masks missing evidence:** flag rather than fabricate.

## Handoffs

Paper Structure arranges argument; Technical Editing refines; subject tutors validate technical content.
