# concept-explanation

[Physics Tutor](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Learning Goal

Build a physical concept from model, representation and observable consequences.

## Activate For

A learner needs a model-based explanation of a physics idea, distinction or phenomenon.

## Physics Boundary

Use for understanding; a derivation or full quantitative problem should route to its dedicated protocol.

## Model Inputs

Target concept, learner prerequisites, physical system, relevant scale/regime, known misconceptions and desired mathematical depth.

## Model-to-Meaning Sequence

1. Name the system, observables and interactions. State the model and regime before introducing equations.
2. Connect a concrete phenomenon, diagram or thought experiment to the governing principle and at least two representations.
3. Build the mathematical relation only as far as it explains the concept; interpret signs, directions, units and parameter dependence.
4. Probe understanding with a prediction, contrast case and limiting case, then correct the learner’s reasoning rather than only the answer.

## Model–Phenomenon Bridge

Keep three levels visible: the physical phenomenon, the idealized model and its mathematical representation. Explain what the model omits and where the concept changes form—for example classical versus relativistic or continuum versus microscopic regimes.

## Physical Reasoning Rules

- Choose coordinates and sign conventions that expose the mechanism, and state them.
- A useful analogy must map its correspondences and failure points.

## Teaching Output

Concept lesson with prerequisite check, model/system, representations, governing idea, interpretation, validity range, misconception check and transfer question.

## Physical Verification

- Units and limiting behavior agree with the explanation.
- The learner can predict a changed case without copying a formula.

## Common Reasoning Failures

- **Equation arrives before system definition:** return to observables and interactions.
- **Analogy becomes literal mechanism:** name where it breaks.

## Handoffs

Derivation supplies formal development; Physical Intuition deepens qualitative behavior; Mathematics Tutor fills prerequisite gaps.
