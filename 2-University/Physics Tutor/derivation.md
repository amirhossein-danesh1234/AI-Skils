# derivation

[Physics Tutor](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Learning Goal

Derive a physical relation from explicit principles, assumptions and conventions.

## Activate For

A physical relation must be developed from stated laws rather than recalled.

## Physics Boundary

Do not present a memorized formula or algebraic rearrangement as a physical derivation.

## Model Inputs

Target relation, system/diagram, governing principle, assumptions, coordinate/sign convention, initial/boundary conditions and learner’s mathematical prerequisites.

## Model-to-Meaning Sequence

1. Define the system, quantities, coordinates and positive directions; check whether the target is scalar, vector, field or statistical relation.
2. State governing law and constitutive/model assumptions. Derive stepwise, explaining the physical meaning of each transformation and any integration constant.
3. Apply initial/boundary conditions and simplify only within declared regimes. Track dimensions and vector directions throughout.
4. Verify with units, limiting cases, symmetry/conservation and—when possible—an alternative short derivation or substitution.

## Assumption Ledger

Attach each major term or simplification to a physical assumption such as rigidity, small angle, quasistatic behavior, negligible drag or equilibrium. If an assumption is removed, identify the first equation that changes.

## Physical Reasoning Rules

- Do not use the target equation as an unstated premise.
- Distinguish exact relation within a model from later approximation and empirical fit.

## Teaching Output

Derivation with diagram/conventions, premises, numbered steps, conditions, units, limiting cases, physical interpretation and verification.

## Physical Verification

- No algebraic step violates the domain or loses a vector/sign condition.
- The final relation has the expected dimensions and behavior in at least two informative limits.

## Common Reasoning Failures

- **Formula substitution masquerades as derivation:** expose the governing principle.
- **Boundary condition applied silently:** show how it fixes constants.

## Handoffs

Mathematics Tutor checks formal transformations; Problem Solving applies the result; Scientific Researcher verifies empirical laws.
