# dimensional-analysis

[Physics Tutor](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Learning Goal

Use dimensions and units to constrain, check or scale a physical relationship.

## Activate For

A relation needs a units audit, scale form or Buckingham-Pi analysis.

## Physics Boundary

Cannot determine dimensionless constants, signs or full functional form without additional physics.

## Model Inputs

Quantities with dimensions and unit system, proposed relation or phenomenon, independent variables, suspected governing factors and regime assumptions.

## Model-to-Meaning Sequence

1. Assign base-dimension vectors consistently and convert units before numerical comparison.
2. For checking, test every additive term and both sides; isolate the exact mismatch and likely missing power or quantity.
3. For inference, form independent dimensionless groups using a rank-aware basis and express the most general dimensionally compatible relation.
4. Use physical assumptions or data to choose functional form/constants, then test limiting behavior and scale consequences.

## Dimension Matrix

Dimensions answer which combinations are possible, not which mechanism is true. State the selected repeating variables, matrix rank and number of independent groups when using Buckingham Pi; different valid bases encode equivalent information.

## Physical Reasoning Rules

- Angles and counts may be dimensionless yet physically meaningful.
- Unit consistency is necessary but never sufficient for correctness.

## Teaching Output

Dimension audit or scaling result with quantity table, conversions, group construction, constrained relation, unresolved constants/functions and checks.

## Physical Verification

- All additive terms share dimensions and conversions retain significant scale.
- The output states what dimensional analysis cannot determine.

## Common Reasoning Failures

- **Treating radians or dimensionless ratios as irrelevant:** preserve meaning.
- **Choosing a dependent set of repeating variables:** check rank.

## Handoffs

Physical Intuition selects regimes; Scientific Problem Solver handles cross-domain groups; Verification performs broader checks.
