# physical-intuition

[Physics Tutor](README.md) / [University domain](../../README.md)

## Learning Goal

Develop qualitative predictions through mechanisms, scales and limiting cases.

## Activate For

A learner can manipulate formulas but cannot predict direction, scale, mechanism or limiting behavior.

## Physics Boundary

Intuition guides and checks reasoning but does not replace calculation or evidence when precision matters.

## Model Inputs

Physical system, model assumptions, quantities/parameters, known baseline case, relevant regimes and the learner’s current prediction.

## Model-to-Meaning Sequence

1. Ask for a prediction before calculation and identify the causal mechanism or conservation transfer that should control the response.
2. Vary one parameter at a time, using diagrams, symmetry, energy/force/flux stories and scale comparisons appropriate to the topic.
3. Examine zero, very large/small, symmetric and threshold cases; note when the model or dominant effect changes.
4. Compare the prediction with the governing relation or simulation/observation, then refine the mental model and pose a transfer case.

## Competing Effects

When intuition conflicts, list effects and their scaling rather than choosing a slogan. Identify the dimensionless ratio or crossover at which dominance changes. Keep direction/sign separate from magnitude.

## Physical Reasoning Rules

- Qualitative reasoning must name its model and validity regime.
- A surprising result is an invitation to check assumptions, not proof the mathematics is wrong.

## Teaching Output

Intuition map with mechanism, signed prediction, parameter trends, limits, crossover/validity and a learner prediction check.

## Physical Verification

- The explanation agrees with conservation, symmetry and dimensions.
- It predicts at least one novel case and states when the prediction may fail.

## Common Reasoning Failures

- **Everyday intuition imported outside its regime:** identify scale/model change.
- **Single-cause story ignores competition:** compare scaling.

## Handoffs

Concept Explanation supplies foundations; Derivation formalizes; Solution Verification resolves conflicts with calculation.
