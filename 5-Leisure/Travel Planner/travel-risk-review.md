# travel-risk-review

[Travel Planner](README.md) / [Leisure domain](../../README.md)

## Travel Goal

Review current entry, safety, health, weather and disruption uncertainties before commitments.

## Use This For

A candidate or planned trip needs a current pre-commitment or pre-departure risk audit.

## Travel Boundary

Does not guarantee safety, give legal advice or replace official alerts/insurance terms.

## Trip Context

Destination/route/dates, traveler nationality/documents only when needed, itinerary/bookings, health/access needs, activities, insurance, local contacts, weather and risk tolerance.

## Travel Workflow

1. Identify decision-relevant categories: entry/transit, official advisories, health, weather/environment, transport disruption, local law/custom, activity safety, documents/money and communication.
2. Verify current official sources for requirements/advisories and current operator/local conditions; distinguish official rule, reported condition, estimate and unknown.
3. Rate consequence, likelihood evidence, controllability and lead time; attach prevention, monitoring, contingency and escalation without catastrophizing.
4. Issue go/modify/wait/avoid-pending-information conditionally, with deadlines to recheck before booking and departure.

## Risk Register with Triggers

A useful risk has cause/event/consequence, evidence date, control, owner and trigger. Entry permission and safety can never be guaranteed; low-risk ordinary travel should not become a crisis checklist.

## Travel Decision Rules

- Official requirements and local emergency/contact details must match jurisdiction and date.
- Do not collect sensitive document copies unless explicitly necessary and authorized.

## Travel Output

Travel risk brief with current source ledger, prioritized register, controls/contingencies, insurance/cancellation questions, recheck schedule and conditional verdict.

## Feasibility and Currency Gates

- High-consequence uncertainties are resolved before irreversible booking where feasible.
- Controls are specific and proportionate to the trip.

## Travel Failure Modes

- **Generic danger list obscures key risks:** prioritize.
- **Old advisory used as current:** verify version/date.

## Handoffs

Destination Research supplies context; Itinerary/Transport implement controls; Health Research supports medical evidence; official authorities decide entry.
