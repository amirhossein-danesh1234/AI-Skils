# destination-research

[Travel Planner](README.md) / [Leisure domain](../../README.md)

## Travel Goal

Assess destinations against dates, interests, entry/logistics, budget and local conditions.

## Use This For

One or more destinations need comparison for a particular trip window.

## Travel Boundary

A destination summary is not a safety guarantee or permission to enter.

## Trip Context

Departure location, dates/flexibility, trip purpose/interests, budget, duration, companions, passports/nationalities only if voluntarily needed, accessibility, climate tolerance and risk preferences.

## Travel Workflow

1. Define hard constraints and desired trip experience, then create a comparison basis that includes door-to-door reachability and season.
2. Research current entry/transit requirements from official authorities and current transport, weather/climate, closures/events, local costs and material conditions from authoritative sources.
3. Compare a few destinations on experience fit, total time/cost range, entry friction, seasonality, accessibility and disruption risk.
4. Recommend a primary and distinct fallback, listing dated facts to reverify before booking/departure and unresolved traveler-specific questions.

## Destination Evidence Layers

Separate stable character/history, seasonal pattern, current operating condition and traveler-specific legal/health requirement. Passport, nationality, route and date can change entry conclusions; never generalize from another traveler.

## Travel Decision Rules

- Official entry and advisory sources outrank travel blogs for requirements.
- A low risk summary is not a guarantee of safety.

## Travel Output

Destination memo with criteria, current source/date ledger, entry/transit flags, time/cost range, season/local conditions, comparison, recommendation and fallback.

## Feasibility and Currency Gates

- The recommended destination is reachable within duration and budget assumptions.
- Every volatile requirement is verified or explicitly pending.

## Travel Failure Modes

- **Pretty shortlist ignores journey/entry:** screen first.
- **Climate average treated as forecast:** distinguish and recheck weather.

## Handoffs

Risk Review deepens uncertainty; Transportation/Accommodation test logistics; Personal Finance checks affordability.
