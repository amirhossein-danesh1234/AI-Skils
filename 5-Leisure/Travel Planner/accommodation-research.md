# accommodation-research

[Travel Planner](README.md) / [Leisure domain](../../README.md)

## Travel Goal

Compare lodging on verified total cost, location, policies and trip suitability.

## Use This For

The trip needs lodging compared on location, full terms and traveler fit.

## Travel Boundary

Research does not create a reservation or guarantee listing accuracy.

## Trip Context

Destination/areas, dates and party/room needs, budget/currency, purpose/itinerary, accessibility, amenities, check-in/out, transport, safety preferences and cancellation flexibility.

## Travel Workflow

1. Map the useful location relative to actual trip anchors and arrival/departure transport, not just the destination center.
2. Search current primary property/booking sources and verify exact dates, room/occupancy, total price, taxes/fees, cancellation, check-in/out and material amenities.
3. Compare a few properties on total cost, door-to-door time, review patterns with recency caveats, accessibility, policy and disruption exposure.
4. Recommend conditionally with a backup and a pre-book verification checklist; never imply a listing has been reserved.

## Lodging Fit Matrix

Separate verified listing facts, recent guest reports and inference. Location value is itinerary-dependent; a cheaper distant stay can cost more in transit, time and fatigue.

## Travel Decision Rules

- Prices, inventory, policies and neighborhood conditions require execution-time verification.
- Do not request or expose passport/payment details for research.

## Travel Output

Lodging comparison with dates/party, total costs, location/transit, policies, suitability, evidence timestamp, primary choice, backup and booking checklist.

## Feasibility and Currency Gates

- All options are compared for the same occupancy, dates and fee basis.
- Check-in/out and arrival/departure logistics are feasible.

## Travel Failure Modes

- **Nightly headline excludes fees:** compare totals.
- **Map proximity measured to wrong anchor:** use itinerary.

## Handoffs

Itinerary integrates stay; Travel Budgeting totals; Risk Review checks current conditions; user authorizes booking.
