# travel-budgeting

[Travel Planner](README.md) / [Leisure domain](../../README.md)

## Travel Goal

Estimate the full trip cost with dates, currencies, uncertainty and contingency.

## Use This For

A trip needs a realistic cost envelope and payment timeline.

## Travel Boundary

A budget is not affordability approval or booking authority.

## Trip Context

Dates/party, currency/home currency, transport/lodging/activity candidates, meals/local transit, entry/insurance/connectivity, exchange/payment fees, cancellation terms and contingency preference.

## Travel Workflow

1. Define included/excluded costs and per-person versus group responsibility, then timestamp base currency and exchange assumptions.
2. Estimate category ranges from current comparable prices, preserving known bookings versus quotes versus assumptions and optional spend.
3. Add timing of deposits/final payments, cancellation exposure, exchange/fee sensitivity and a contingency sized to uncertainty rather than a universal percentage.
4. Compare base, lean and disruption cases and identify approval/affordability questions before any booking.

## Trip Cost Ledger

Track known booked amount, refundable exposure, current quote, estimate, assumption and contingency separately. Total planned cost and cash due by date are different views.

## Travel Decision Rules

- Current prices, exchange rates, taxes/fees and terms require verification when material.
- Do not treat unused budget as a spending target.

## Travel Output

Budget with category ranges/status, currency/rate date, payment timeline, cancellation exposure, scenarios, contingency and unresolved affordability/approval.

## Feasibility and Currency Gates

- All major door-to-door and stay costs are included once.
- Upper credible cost and payment dates are visible.

## Travel Failure Modes

- **Attraction/food/local transit omitted:** add realistic categories.
- **Current quote presented as locked price:** label.

## Handoffs

Personal Finance assesses affordability/liquidity; Transport/Accommodation supply quotes; user authorizes purchase.
