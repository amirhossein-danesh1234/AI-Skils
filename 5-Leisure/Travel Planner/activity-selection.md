# activity-selection

[Travel Planner](README.md) / [Leisure domain](../../README.md)

## Travel Goal

Choose trip activities that fit purpose, location, energy, timing and companions.

## Use This For

A trip needs experiences prioritized without sacrificing logistics or rest.

## Travel Boundary

Does not pack the itinerary or assume current opening/booking conditions.

## Trip Context

Trip purpose, destination/areas, dates, companions, interests/no’s, energy, accessibility, budget, booking tolerance, weather and itinerary anchors.

## Travel Workflow

1. Define the trip’s desired experiences and select a small set of anchor, optional and spontaneous activities.
2. Research current opening days/times, duration, location, booking, access, cost and seasonal/weather conditions from primary sources.
3. Compare fit and clustering, allowing meals, queues, transit, rest and companions’ different needs; avoid redundant attractions.
4. Recommend a balanced set with reservation deadlines, drop order and indoor/low-energy alternatives, without booking.

## Anchor–Optional Portfolio

Use a few high-value anchors, flexible nearby options and deliberately open time. An activity earns an anchor slot when missing it would materially diminish the trip and advance booking is justified.

## Travel Decision Rules

- Do not place two fixed activities without a credible transfer/buffer.
- Current opening and reservation facts must be rechecked near execution.

## Travel Output

Activity portfolio with fit, verified timing/cost/access, geographic cluster, booking need, energy load, alternatives and itinerary handoff.

## Feasibility and Currency Gates

- Selected activities fit trip purpose and party constraints.
- The portfolio remains enjoyable if an optional item is dropped.

## Travel Failure Modes

- **Checklist tourism ignores purpose:** prioritize experience.
- **Every attraction marked must-do:** preserve trade-offs.

## Handoffs

Itinerary sequences; Budget totals; Risk reviews weather/local conditions; Activity Planner handles a local outing.
