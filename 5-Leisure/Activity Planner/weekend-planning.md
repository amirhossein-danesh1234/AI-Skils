# weekend-planning

[Activity Planner](README.md) / [Leisure domain](../../README.md)

## Activity Goal

Build a feasible weekend mix of recreation, obligations, rest and spontaneity.

## Activate When

The user wants a realistic leisure-oriented weekend that coexists with obligations and rest.

## Coordination Boundary

Leisure planning does not take ownership of the user’s entire priority system.

## Activity Context

Weekend time window, fixed obligations/commute, desired experience, energy/recovery, people, location, budget, weather, preparation and preferred spontaneity.

## Planning Workflow

1. Anchor fixed obligations, sleep/meals, transit and recovery, then ask what would make the weekend feel worthwhile.
2. Choose one primary leisure outcome and at most a small supporting set, matching activity demand to energy and full door-to-door time.
3. Add buffer, unscheduled space and an indoor/low-energy alternative; verify current venue/weather/transport facts near execution.
4. Publish a base and disrupted version with a clear drop order rather than compressing everything when plans change.

## Enjoyment Capacity

Free clock time is not automatically activity capacity. Social energy, travel, preparation and the desire for unstructured time are real constraints. A restful weekend can be a complete plan.

## Activity Decision Rules

- Do not optimize every block or treat rest as a failure to plan.
- Travel Planner owns overnight/trip complexity; Personal Planner owns broader competing priorities.

## Activity Output

Weekend outline with anchors, primary experience, logistics, meals/rest, buffer, spontaneous space, current checks and weather/disruption fallback.

## Feasibility Gates

- The plan fits door-to-door and includes recovery.
- Optional items are visibly droppable without cascading failure.

## Activity Failure Modes

- **Three major outings fit only on paper:** count friction.
- **Bad weather removes entire plan:** add real alternative.

## Handoffs

Activity Discovery supplies options; Group Activity coordinates people; Travel Planner handles trips; Personal Planner resolves value conflicts.
