# activity-discovery

[Activity Planner](README.md) / [Leisure domain](../../README.md)

## Activity Goal

Find local or remote activities suited to interests, time, place and accessibility.

## Activate When

The user needs a small set of things to do in a place, time or remote context.

## Coordination Boundary

Discovery produces a shortlist, not current availability or a booking.

## Activity Context

Location/radius, date/time window, interests and disliked activities, solo/group, energy, budget, accessibility, transport, weather tolerance and desired novelty.

## Planning Workflow

1. Define the desired experience and hard constraints before searching; distinguish indoor, outdoor and remote possibilities.
2. Research current candidates from primary venue/organizer and reliable local sources, checking location, timing, access and total time/cost when action is near.
3. Score for fit, friction, novelty and resilience; include an option that needs little planning and a weather/cancellation alternative.
4. Return a concise shortlist with next verification/booking step but do not contact, reserve or spend without authority.

## Friction Budget

Include travel, parking/transit, preparation, queues, booking, accessibility and coordination. A modest activity with low friction may produce a better leisure experience than a headline option.

## Activity Decision Rules

- Opening times, prices, availability and local conditions are volatile facts.
- Do not make all free time productive or novel.

## Activity Output

Shortlist with experience fit, total time, estimated/verified cost, logistics/access, current-fact timestamp, fallback and authorization boundary.

## Feasibility Gates

- Each option is feasible in the full door-to-door window.
- Recommendations differ meaningfully and do not exceed the requested list size.

## Activity Failure Modes

- **Discovery becomes catalog dump:** curate.
- **Venue hours assumed from old listings:** verify primary source.

## Handoffs

Weekend Planning integrates obligations/rest; Group Activity coordinates people; Travel Planner handles trip-scale logistics.
