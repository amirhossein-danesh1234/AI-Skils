# group-activity-planning

[Activity Planner](README.md) / [Leisure domain](../../README.md)

## Activity Goal

Coordinate a shared activity across preferences, availability, access and logistics.

## Activate When

Several people need a fair and feasible shared leisure plan.

## Coordination Boundary

Does not assume consensus, share personal information or contact participants without authority.

## Activity Context

Participants, organizer/decision method, availability, location/transport, preferences and hard no’s, accessibility, budget/payment, coordination channel, occasion and privacy constraints.

## Planning Workflow

1. Collect the minimum needed constraints and distinguish hard access/time limits from preferences; avoid sharing private details beyond the group need.
2. Generate a few options that satisfy all hard constraints and distribute preference wins fairly, including a low-coordination fallback.
3. Compare total logistics, arrival/departure, payment, reservation/cancellation and weather alternatives using current facts where material.
4. Propose a decision rule and concise message draft; obtain authorization before contacting anyone, booking or collecting money.

## Fairness and Consent

Fair does not require everyone’s first choice each time. It requires transparent constraints, voluntary participation, accessible exit and no hidden cost or pressure. Track rotation only if the group wants it.

## Activity Decision Rules

- Silence is not consent to cost, travel or participation.
- Do not expose one participant’s sensitive reason for an accommodation.

## Activity Output

Group plan with constraints, shortlist/trade-offs, decision method, logistics/payment, fallback, message draft and explicit action approvals.

## Feasibility Gates

- Every participant can feasibly attend or decline.
- The selected option has a clear cancellation/weather path.

## Activity Failure Modes

- **Endless consensus polling:** use agreed decision rule.
- **Organizer’s preference silently dominates:** expose trade-offs.

## Handoffs

Budget Activity handles costs; Weekend Plan fits time; Communication Coach supports sensitive group messages.
