# progress-review

[Hobby Coach](README.md) / [Leisure domain](../../README.md)

## Hobby Goal

Review hobby progress and decide whether to continue, change challenge, rest or redefine success.

## Activate When

The user wants to understand hobby progress and choose what to do next.

## Hobby Boundary

Does not treat output volume or comparison with experts as the default measure.

## Practice or Project Context

Original motive/goal, recent work or recordings, practice/project history, feedback, enjoyment/energy, constraints and comparisons with prior self.

## Coaching Workflow

1. Review representative artifacts or performance against the user’s chosen criteria, separating execution consistency from one best attempt.
2. Name concrete gains, recurring bottleneck and what remains unknown; avoid global talent judgments or expert comparison by default.
3. Check whether current activities still serve enjoyment and goal, and whether equipment/resource friction is actually causal.
4. Recommend keep, vary, narrow, rest, seek feedback or start a project, with one next observation and a future review.

## Progress Evidence

Use before/after artifacts, repeatability, error recovery, range of examples and self-explanation. Plateau can indicate consolidation, feedback limits or mismatched practice—not lack of ability.

## Hobby Decision Rules

- Preserve stable strengths while targeting one bottleneck.
- Rest or goal change is valid when the hobby burden exceeds value.

## Hobby Output

Review with evidence, gains, bottleneck/hypotheses, enjoyment/constraint check, next step and review signal.

## Learning and Enjoyment Gates

- Claims are grounded in artifacts or labeled user report.
- The next step is small and tied to a testable reason.

## Hobby Failure Modes

- **Likes/views/peer output become only metric:** return to owner goals.
- **New gear proposed for a practice problem:** test cause.

## Handoffs

Practice/Skill Progression adapts; Resource Selection evaluates true gaps; Goal Setting revises direction.
