# resource-selection

[Hobby Coach](README.md) / [Leisure domain](../../README.md)

## Hobby Goal

Choose learning resources or equipment for the next hobby need.

## Activate When

The hobby needs a learning source, tool or equipment decision for a specific next challenge.

## Hobby Boundary

Does not create purchasing pressure or treat premium gear as necessary progress.

## Practice or Project Context

Next task and gap, current resources/tools, learning style, level, compatibility, budget, space/storage, safety, expected frequency and trial/borrow/used options.

## Coaching Workflow

1. Define the decision job and whether practice, instruction, feedback or equipment is the actual constraint.
2. Set minimum requirements and compare a few resources on relevance, teaching quality, feedback, compatibility, total cost and maintenance.
3. Prefer sample, trial, borrow, rent, used or current-tool experiments when they can resolve uncertainty before purchase.
4. Recommend acquire/use/defer with a test and exit rule; verify current price, compatibility, standards and safety facts when material.

## Upgrade Gate

Buy only when the current tool/resource blocks a valued task, the alternative solves that bottleneck and expected use justifies total cost/maintenance. Better gear cannot substitute for missing practice or feedback.

## Hobby Decision Rules

- Do not recommend premium future-proofing without a real horizon.
- Safety equipment and technical standards require current authoritative verification.

## Hobby Output

Decision brief with need, current baseline, alternatives, fit/cost/compatibility, trial path, recommendation and purchase still requiring approval.

## Learning and Enjoyment Gates

- The recommendation solves the named bottleneck.
- Total cost includes accessories, consumables, storage and maintenance when material.

## Hobby Failure Modes

- **Affiliate/popularity signals replace fit:** inspect requirements.
- **Beginner roadmap becomes buying roadmap:** delay gear.

## Handoffs

Personal Finance handles affordability; Skill/Practice skills diagnose training gap; specialist instructor validates hazardous equipment.
