# practice-planning

[Hobby Coach](README.md) / [Leisure domain](../../README.md)

## Hobby Goal

Plan manageable hobby practice around time, tools, feedback and enjoyment.

## Activate When

A hobby skill needs repeatable practice under available time and tools.

## Hobby Boundary

Does not prescribe hazardous technique beyond competence.

## Practice or Project Context

Specific ability/project, current level and attempt, time/energy, tools/space, feedback, enjoyment preferences, safety and upcoming opportunity.

## Coaching Workflow

1. Define the practice target and success evidence, then identify the smallest component that limits the next meaningful performance.
2. Design a session with warm-up/setup if needed, focused attempt, feedback, varied/whole practice and a satisfying finish.
3. Fit frequency and duration to realistic free time, using minimum and playful alternatives for low-energy weeks.
4. Track only useful evidence, review after several attempts and adjust difficulty or method rather than adding hours automatically.

## Practice–Play Balance

Deliberate practice targets errors; free play preserves experimentation and enjoyment. The proportion should match the user’s motive. A hobby can prioritize play without apologizing for slower progression.

## Hobby Decision Rules

- Safety-critical tools or physical technique require qualified instruction and current guidance.
- Do not compensate missed practice with punishing volume.

## Hobby Output

Practice plan with target, session loop, frequency/minimum version, feedback, play component, safety/setup and review criteria.

## Learning and Enjoyment Gates

- The session can be executed with current tools and time.
- Practice produces evidence without excessive tracking burden.

## Hobby Failure Modes

- **Repeating entire activity hides bottleneck:** isolate briefly.
- **Drills remove enjoyment entirely:** retain meaningful play.

## Handoffs

Skill Progression sequences difficulty; Resource Selection fills a gap; specialist instructor handles hazardous technique.
