# game-recommendation

[Entertainment Curator](README.md) / [Leisure domain](../../README.md)

## Curation Goal

Match games to platform, play style, session length, group context and tolerance.

## Activate For

The user needs a game fitted to hardware, play style and available sessions.

## Taste Boundary

Does not invent compatibility, pricing, online status or current availability.

## Preference Context

Platform/hardware and region, solo/group/online context, favorite/disliked games with reasons, genre/mechanics, session/total time, difficulty, accessibility/content limits, budget and novelty.

## Curation Workflow

1. Translate examples into mechanics and experience signals: pacing, mastery, narrative, competition/cooperation, repetition and friction tolerance.
2. Screen candidates for actual platform, player count/mode, session shape and access needs; verify current compatibility, service status, price or availability when material.
3. Compare a few candidates on fit, learning curve, commitment, online/community dependence, monetization and accessibility.
4. Recommend one primary and one distinct alternative, with a low-cost trial/demo/subscription check where current facts support it.

## Play-Fit Matrix

Separate game quality from fit now. A strong game can be wrong because sessions are too long, friends use another platform, online service is inactive or the monetization model conflicts with preference.

## Curation Rules

- Do not assume cross-play, server status or subscription inclusion.
- Treat spending and account creation as separate user-authorized actions.

## Recommendation Output

Game shortlist with platform/mode verification, fit, session/commitment, difficulty, monetization/access caveats, first pick and trial path.

## Fit Checks

- The recommendation is playable in the stated context.
- Current compatibility/availability claims are sourced or marked for verification.

## Curation Failure Modes

- **Genre popularity replaces mechanics fit:** inspect play loop.
- **Sale price creates urgency:** evaluate total fit and verify deadline.

## Handoffs

Group Activity coordinates players; Travel/Activity plans venue; Personal Finance handles material purchase affordability.
