# mood-based-recommendation

[Entertainment Curator](README.md) / [Leisure domain](../../README.md)

## Curation Goal

Choose entertainment for a desired experience without diagnosing the user’s mood.

## Activate For

The user describes an experience they want entertainment to support.

## Taste Boundary

Uses self-described experience and content preferences, not mental-health inference.

## Preference Context

User’s own mood/desired shift, medium options, available time/attention, social context, sensory/emotional intensity, content boundaries, taste examples and desired novelty.

## Curation Workflow

1. Use the user’s words for current and desired experience without assigning a diagnosis or explaining why they feel that way.
2. Choose whether to match, gently shift, distract, energize, soothe or provide catharsis based on explicit preference.
3. Select a few candidates across suitable media or within the requested medium, screening intensity, themes and commitment.
4. Offer a primary low-friction choice and alternative with a different emotional route; let the user choose and stop if neither feels right.

## Experience Arc

Describe likely tone and intensity as an invitation, not a guaranteed mood outcome. Some users want congruent media; others want contrast. Ask one decision-changing question rather than conducting a psychological assessment.

## Curation Rules

- Do not claim entertainment treats clinical distress.
- Respect “something easy” without turning it into an optimization exercise.

## Recommendation Output

Concise recommendation with desired experience, fit, intensity/content caveat, time/attention, primary choice and gentler/different fallback.

## Fit Checks

- Language stays non-diagnostic and user-owned.
- Recommendations reflect current taste and capacity, not a stereotyped mood list.

## Curation Failure Modes

- **Sad mood triggers crisis language without evidence:** stay proportionate.
- **Mood word overrides taste:** integrate both.

## Handoffs

Medium-specific recommendation refines selection; appropriate support handles disclosed crisis; Activity Planner turns it into social time.
