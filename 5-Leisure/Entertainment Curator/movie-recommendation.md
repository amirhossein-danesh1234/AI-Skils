# movie-recommendation

[Entertainment Curator](README.md) / [Leisure domain](../../README.md)

## Curation Goal

Recommend films with spoiler-aware reasons tied to the user’s taste and viewing context.

## Activate For

A viewing session needs a small spoiler-free film shortlist.

## Taste Boundary

Does not overwhelm with a generic ranked catalog or invent streaming availability.

## Preference Context

Liked/disliked films and reasons, genres/eras/languages, runtime/window, audience, attention, tone, content boundaries, novelty and platforms/region if availability matters.

## Curation Workflow

1. Extract signals for pacing, tone, storytelling, visual style, humor, intensity and ending preference without revealing plot.
2. Screen candidates against runtime, audience and content boundaries; build a shortlist with distinct experience profiles.
3. Verify current regional availability/rental terms only when the user will act on them, using current platform sources.
4. Rank a few choices with spoiler-free fit and caveats, then give one confident pick and a fallback for a different energy level.

## Spoiler-Safe Fit

Explain using premise-level information, style, pace, intensity and comparison titles. Avoid twists, late-character information and promotional claims that reveal structure.

## Curation Rules

- Do not equate ratings or awards with fit.
- A platform catalog claim needs current region/date verification.

## Recommendation Output

Film shortlist with runtime, fit reasons, tone/intensity, spoiler-safe caveat, verified access if needed, primary and fallback.

## Fit Checks

- Every option fits the viewing window and audience.
- No spoiler or stale availability is presented as fact.

## Curation Failure Modes

- **Generic “top movies” list:** anchor to taste.
- **All picks share the same trade-off:** diversify meaningfully.

## Handoffs

Mood-Based adjusts experience; Group Activity handles shared viewing; Series Recommendation handles longer commitment.
